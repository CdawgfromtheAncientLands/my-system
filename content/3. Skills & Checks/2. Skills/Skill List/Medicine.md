Medicine is healing knowledge - diagnosis, treatment, intervention, and long-term care. It blends practical skill with anatomical understanding and the expertise to stabilize or save a life under pressure.
### **First Aid** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
Rapid stabilizing care under pressure.
- Bandaging, splinting
- Blood loss control
### **Surgery & Treatment** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
In-depth medical practices requiring tools and skill.
- Removing foreign objects
- Treating disease or trauma
- Long-term recovery plans
