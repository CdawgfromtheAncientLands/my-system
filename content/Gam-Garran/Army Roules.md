The basic idea of the Army fight is the UNIT. Units are creatures tucked into a cohesive group.

Units have several characteristics:
SIZE: An expression of occupied space, and loose analogue for no. of medium-sized bodies.
DISCIPLINE: An expression of willingness to obey orders, stay in formation, 
MIGHT: The unit's physical strength and capability to resist intrusion
ALACRITY: The unit's organized reaction time and maneuver speed.





 Single units can move through anything and don't really play by the rules at all.

Squadron (~20 bodies) can hold NO lines (unless otherwise said). 1 platoon = 2 squadrons.

Platoon (~60 bodies) can hold ONE line. 1 hex = 2 platoons.

Company (~150) can hold TWO lines. 1 company = 2 platoons = 1 hex.



Actions: March, March (Double Time), Attack, Mount/Dismount, Form Up, Charge

Attack: 



Formations: Loose, tight

Tight = nothing
Loose = Extra defense against ranged attacks, but extra damage from melee attacks

After a charge or double-time march, roll Discipline or else automatically enter a loose formation




There are two types of melee engagements: CLOSED-RANK and OPEN-RANK.

CLOSED-RANK engagements occur when a unit initiates a melee attack on a unit that it shares a border with.

OPEN-RANK engagements occur as soon as:
1. Either unit in a CLOSED-RANK engagement BREAKS THE LINE
2. Two opposing units occupy the same space, with at least one being PLATOON sized.
