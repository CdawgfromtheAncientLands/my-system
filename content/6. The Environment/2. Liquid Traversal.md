Moving through liquid—whether water, sludge, mud, or viscous ooze—affects mobility depending on depth and consistency.

|**Type**|**Submersion**|**Effects**|
|---|---|---|
|**Watery Liquids**|_Partially (up to knees)_|Creatures without a **Swim Speed** move as if in **Difficult Terrain**.|
||_Mostly (up to waist)_|Creatures without a **Swim Speed** move as if in **Difficult Terrain** and become **Clumsy 1**.|
||_Fully (Glub Glub)_|Creatures must have a **Swim Speed** or succeed at **Swim** checks to move more than 5 ft at once. Those without a **Swim Speed** are **Clumsy 2**.|
|**Viscous Liquids**|_Partially (up to knees)_|Creature movement counts as **Difficult Terrain**, and they are **Clumsy 1**.|
||_Mostly (up to waist)_|Creatures move as if in **Difficult Terrain** and are **Clumsy 2**.|
||_Fully (Glub Glub)_|Everything submerged is **Strained 2**. Creatures must have a **Swim Speed** or succeed at **Swim** checks to move more than 5 ft at once.|

**Strained 2** applies to all submerged creatures and items in fully viscous liquid.

---

## Additional Notes

### **Projectiles Through Liquid**

Use the rules for **Storm Winds** as a baseline. Adjust DCs based on viscosity, turbulence, and the physical properties of the projectile. Thrown or fired attacks passing into or through liquid typically gain increasing penalties the deeper the attack travels.

### **Auditory Effects**

- Normal liquid can be treated as **Noisy**.
    
- **Viscous** liquids may count as **Loud** or even **Deafening**, depending on density and pressure.
    

### **Visual Effects**

For seeing or tracking through liquid, apply the same visibility rules used for:

- rain
    
- snowfall
    
- fog
    
- obscuring mist effects
    

Liquids distort visibility, impose blur, and reduce the clarity of silhouettes depending on depth and agitation.
