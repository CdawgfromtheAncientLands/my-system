# <font color="#d83931">Suffocation</font>

When a creature has no air, it begins to lose breath. Running out of breath triggers suffocation, which rapidly leads to Dying.

## Holding Your Breath

All creatures have a breath capacity.  
For an untrained humanoid, use:

> **Breath Rounds = Physique ÷ 2**

Some creatures have more (amphibious, trained swimmers), some less.

When a creature has no breath remaining, it must either:

- make a **beginning-of-turn Fortitude save**, or
    
- begin **Suffocating** immediately.
    

After a creature succeeds on a number of Fortitude saves equal to **half** its maximum breath rounds, it automatically fails the next one.

## Suffocation

A suffocating creature immediately begins **Dying**.  
Real drowning takes minutes, not seconds, but in combat this is the relevant state.

A suffocating creature **cannot** be assisted with Death Saves until it is no longer suffocating.

## Willingly Exhaling

Speaking, yelling, releasing a breath weapon, or forcefully exhaling causes the creature to **lose 3 rounds of breath** instantly.

## Breath Loss From Damage

If a creature would take damage **severe enough to force failed Death Saves** (usually 1–3 failures), instead of failing, it loses that many **breath rounds** automatically.

Example:  
Joe has Physique 6 → **3 breath rounds**.  
He takes 20 damage but survives normally.  
He still loses **3 breath rounds** due to the force of the blow.

### Injury Interaction

Suffering an **Injury** from damage causes the creature to lose **double** the normal breath amount.
