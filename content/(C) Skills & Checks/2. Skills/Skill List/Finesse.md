**Finesse** is precision, subtlety, and the art of remaining graceful and unnoticed. It governs how you move when every sound matters, how you manipulate tools under pressure, and how you bypass defenses meant to stop intruders.
### **Sneak** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|INS]]
Remaining unseen and unheard while moving or hiding.
- Sneaking past guards
- Hiding behind cover
- Evading detection in darkness or crowds
### **Quick Fingers** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
Speed and precision with hands under pressure.
- Pickpocketing coins, tokens, or devices
- Palming objects or concealing small items
### **Trickwork** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Manipulating locks, traps, mechanisms, and small devices.
- Opening safes, strongboxes, or chests
- Understanding tumblers, catches, and latches
