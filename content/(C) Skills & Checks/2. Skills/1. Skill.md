*Skills are the muscle memory, knowledge, instincts, and techniques utilized in performing certain courses of action or pertaining to distinct subjects. They are the meat and cheese of this system.*

Skills come in two parts - the Primary Skill (or, alternatively, just "Skill") and the Subskill. Every [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] in [[The Daring]] is made with a [[1. Skill|Subskill]] in mind (ex. "Make a [[Melee|Blade]] check", "Make a [[Survival|Navigate]] check".) 

The full formula for a skill check's usual bonus = [[1.3 Primary Attribute|Attribute Mod]] + [[1. Skill|Primary Skill Score]] + [[1. Skill|Subskill Score]].
## Primary Skills
Primary Skills can range from +0 to +4. A PC must invest in several Primary skills before they can unlock higher skill levels. Every time you raise a Primary Skill, you may increase one of its [[1. Skill|Subskill]]s to *Adept* for free (if it isn't already).

| Skill Score | EXP cost from previous | Subskill Max | Prerequisite                 |
| ----------- | ---------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------- |
| 0           | 0                      | Adept (+1)   | N/A                          |
| +1          | 4                      | Adept (+1)   | None                         |
| +2          | 7                      | Expert (+2)  | +1 in 3 other Primary Skills |
| +3          | 12                     | Master (+3)  | +2 in 2 other Primary Skills |
| +4          | 18                     | Master (+3)  | +3 in 1 other Primary Skill  |
## Subskills
Subskills range from Adept (+1) to Master (+3). Every time you raise a [[1. Skill|Primary Skill]], you may increase one of [[1. Skill|Subskill]]s to *Adept* for free if you haven't already.

| **Level** | **Bonus** | EXP cost from previous |
| --------- | --------- | ---------------------- |
| *Adept*   | +1        | 3                      |
| *Expert*  | +2        | 5                      |
| *Master*  | +3        | 9                      |
#### <font color="#ff0000"><u>Linked Attribute</u></font>
Every subskill has one or more Linked Attributes that contribute their [[1.3 Primary Attribute|Attribute Modifier]] to the [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]. In the case of several Linked Attributes, a player may choose whichever they prefer, or the GM may say whichever one is relevant to the situation – but a check will NOT benefit from 2 Linked Attributes at once.
# Thingus
It may be painfully obvious, on occasion, that some skills are more generally-applicable than others. If this is the case, it is the adventure’s fault – but this may not even be a bad thing! Be sure to understand what skills are most useful where, and which ones should be the focus of a campaign.
