A **Difficulty Class** (or **DC**) is a number that the result of a **Skill Check** must **Meet or Beat** to succeed.

So, what is difficulty? In my terms, it’s pretty much “the likelihood that an average person could try and succeed at a task”. That’s about it. When [[1.2 Difficulty Class|Difficulty Class]] is being assigned to a task in-game, a player should **ALWAYS** know what it is after they confirm they'd like to try. The process should go like this:

1. Player says, “I want to do something.”
2. GM and player determine what skill the player will use for the check.
3. GM determines the DC and shares it with the player.
4. Player makes the check.

A player should always know the DC of the check they’re making **before** they make the roll – but **after** they decide they’re going to do the check. Whether or not a player can effectively discern the difficulty of a course of action before they undertake it is entirely GM fiat – some situations may be very obvious, while others may be quite opaque.
