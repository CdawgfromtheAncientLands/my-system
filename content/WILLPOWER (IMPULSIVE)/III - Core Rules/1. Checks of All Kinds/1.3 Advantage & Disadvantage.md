# Advantage
Certain spotlight moments and drastically good circumstances can be represented through [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]]. 

> For each instance of [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] you have on a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]], you may reroll 1 of the 2d12s and keep the better result. Reroll the same dice no more than once.
# Disadvantage
This is the exact same as [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]], but just has you take the worse result instead. Having [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] and [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] at the same time cancel each other out.

# Effects on Skill DCs
Each instance of [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] or [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] counts as +1/-1 on [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]]s.
