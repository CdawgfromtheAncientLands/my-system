#### <u>Clumsy X</u>
*You suffer from slow reflexes and poor balance.*
- You suffer **–X** to **[[1.1 Attributes|Agility]]**-based [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s
- You suffer **–X** to **[[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]]** checks
#### <u>Feeble X</u>
*Strength leaks from you.*
- You suffer a -X penalty to **[[1.1 Attributes|Physique]]** based [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s
- You suffer **–X** to damage rolls that would add your [[Athletics|Muscle]] bonus. 
#### <u>Strained X</u>
*Intense load has your body aching with every motion.*
- Your **[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]]s** are reduced by **5ft** per rank of this condition
- You are **[[2.2 Bodily Conditions|Feeble]] X** and **[[2.2 Bodily Conditions|Clumsy]] X**
#### <u>Sick X</u>
*You shiver, heave, and sweat as your senses go haywire.*
- You cannot willingly [[Ingest]] anything.
- You suffer a -X penalty to all [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s 
---
#### <u>Unconscious</u>
*You’re not awake.*
- You cannot [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Sense]] or do anything.
- You are **[[2.8 External Conditions|Vulnerable]] 2**.
---
#### <u>Bleeding X</u>
*Your vital fluids leak and spurt out as you move.*
- When you take a [[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] or [[Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]] action, lose X [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]]. 
- If you have no [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]] left, instead suffer [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +1.
---
#### <u>Slow X</u>
*Delayed senses and gummed-up muscles drag you down.*
- Whenever you would gain [[1.3 Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]]s, reduce the number gained by X.
- You can't move more than your base [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]] in the span of 1 [[1.3 Actions|Action]]. 
#### <u>Stunned X</u>
*Precious moments slip away from you and your thoughts white-out.*
- You **cannot use [[1.3 Actions|Reaction ↻]]s**.
- At the start of your turn, you lose **X total actions**, in this order:
	1. Minor ⋄
	2. Major ⟐
	3. Minor ⋄
- After losing these actions, **reduce [[2.2 Bodily Conditions|Stunned]] by the number of actions lost**.
