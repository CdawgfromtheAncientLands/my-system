#### <u>Blind</u>
*Light finds no purchase in your eyes.*
- Your movement speeds are **halved**.
- You are immune to **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Visual}]]** effects
- You suffer **–4** to **[[Observation|Perception]]** [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s
- You decrease your **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Detection Rating]]** of all creatures by **2 ranks**.
#### <u>Dazzled</u>
*Your vision is blitzed and disrupted.*
- Whenever you would be affected by a [[Action & Effect Traits|{Visual}]] effect, you are immune to it on a DC 4 [[1.4 Flat Check|Flat Check]].
- Decrease your **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Detection Rating]]** of all creatures by **1 rank**.
- You suffer **–2** to **[[Observation|Perception]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s**
#### <u>Deaf</u>
*Your hearing is reduced to the point of desperate tremor-feeling.*
- You are immune to **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Auditory}]]** effects
- You suffer **–1** to **[[Observation|Perception]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s**, and -4 to those utilizing your hearing only.
