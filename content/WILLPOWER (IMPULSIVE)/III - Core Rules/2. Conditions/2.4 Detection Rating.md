> How detectable a creature is to a given observer at this moment. Detection Rating is a ladder of discrete states; abilities and circumstances move you up or down the ladder. It governs whether you can be targeted, how precision attacks apply, and what actions you need to locate or reveal a foe.
## Using Detection Rating

- **Pick one state per observer.** Each creature has exactly one Detection Rating relative to each observer.
- **Move by effects.** Stealth, concealment, cover, light, distraction, and special actions/conditions can shift your Rating.
- **Targeting & Reactions.** Each state specifies what attacks or abilities can target you and what information the observer has.
## The Ladder
Detection Rating proceeds from least detectable to most:
**(1) Unnoticed → (2) Undetected → (3) Hidden → (4) Concealed → (5) Observed**
#### <u>(1) Unnoticed</u>
*An observer is unaware of your presence.*
- Observers you are Unnoticed by are **[[2.1 Reading a Condition|Vulnerable]] 2** to you.
- They cannot target you with **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]** [[1.3 Actions|Action]]s.
#### <u>(2) Undetected</u>
*An observer knows you are nearby, but can’t determine where.*
- Observers you are **(2) Undetected** by are **[[2.1 Reading a Condition|Vulnerable]] 1** to you.
 - They cannot target you with **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]** [[1.3 Actions|Action]]s.
#### <u>(3) Hidden</u>
*An observer knows the tactical area you occupy, but can't fully discern you.*
- Observers you are **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Hidden]]** to are **[[2.8 External Conditions|Vulnerable]]** to you.
- Their [[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]] [[1.3 Actions|Action]]s targeting you must make a **DC 7 [[1.4 Flat Check]]** or fail.
#### <u>(4) Concealed</u>
*You are somewhat obscured to an observer.*
- The **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]** [[1.3 Actions|Action]]s of observers targeting you must make a **DC 4 [[1.4 Flat Check]]** or automatically fail.
#### <u>(5) Observed</u>
*You are entirely visible to an observer through a Precise Sense.*
- No special effects - this is the default state.
