#### <u>Worn X</u>
*Your equipment's sheen dulls*
- If affected equipment is [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]], reduce its [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]]s by 1.
- If affected equipment is a [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon]], reduce its [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]] by 1.
- If affected equipment's Worn condition exceeds its [[Wear Threshold]], it is destroyed.
