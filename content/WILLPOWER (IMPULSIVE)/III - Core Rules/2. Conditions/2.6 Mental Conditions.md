#### <u>Enthralled</u>
*You’re compelled into a certain course of action.*
- This condition’s **specific effect text** directs your behavior; follow those instructions for how you act or may be directed.
#### <u>Panicked X</u>
*Fear and stress overwhelm you.*
- Suffer **–X** to **all** [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s
- At the end of your [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] or every ~10 seconds, suffer X [[3.4 Testing Composure|Stress]].
#### <u>Stupefied X</u>
*Your mind is fogged and your train-of-thought is jarred.*
- Suffer **–1** to [[1.1 Attributes|Logic]], [[1.1 Attributes|Charisma]], and [[1.1 Attributes|Awareness]]-based [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s.
- Whenever you take a **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]** [[1.3 Actions|Action]] or suffer a **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]]** effect, make a **DC (3X) [[1.4 Flat Check|Flat Check]]; 
	- On a failure, either your Concentrate action automatically fails or you are **immune to that [[Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]] effect**.
