# Action Surge (◌)
Cost: **2 [[1.7 Impulse|Impulse Points]]**  
Trigger: You are about to take a [[1.3 Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]] that has a [[1.3 Actions|Major ⟐ Action]] version.
Effect: The action is immediately treated as its Major version, gaining all ⟐ benefits and effects.
# Instant Step (◌)
Cost: **1 [[1.7 Impulse|Impulse Points]]**  
Frequency: **Once per turn**
Effect: You [[Movement Actions|Step]] up to 5ft. as a [[1.3 Actions|Free ✧ Action]].
# Resolute Reaction (↻)
Cost: **5 [[1.7 Impulse|Impulse Points]]**  
Trigger: A creature declares, begins, or ends an [[1.3 Actions|Action]], or a creature's [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] ends or begins.
Effect: You may take a non-[[Strikes|Strike]] [[1.3 Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]] as if it were a [[1.3 Actions|Reaction ↻]]. You must still meet all prerequisites for that action, such as having your [[1.3 Actions|Reaction ↻]] unspent and available.

# Second Wind (↻)
Cost: **2 [[1.7 Impulse|Impulse Points]]**  
Trigger: You just succeeded on a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]
Effect: Reduce [[3.4 Testing Composure|Stress]] by 3. If the check was a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Success]], reduce by 1d4+2 instead.
## Freedom of Action (◌)
Cost: **2 [[1.7 Impulse|Impulse Points]]**  
Trigger: You would be interrupted, restricted, or prevented from acting.
Effect: You ignore that restriction for this action. After the action resolves, you cannot take [[1.3 Actions|Reaction ↻]]s until your next turn.
