A little more boring than [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s and [[1.1 Attributes|Attribute]]s, but still necessary for play, are the [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Characteristic]]s. They are [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Sense]], [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]], and [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Size]]. 

The characteristics themselves are simple and well-integrated, enough so that they should be naturally understood and utilized without complex rules. They are Sense, Size/Weight, and Speed.
# Senses
## <u>Precise Senses</u>
*Precise* senses can be used to perceive the world in nuanced detail. [[Creature]]s can usually be detected automatically with a precise sense unless they are hiding or otherwise obscured. 

When a creature has **Precise Hearing** or **Precise Scent**, it is *usually only effective for a certain radius*. Creatures with poor vision may also have their **Precise Vision** limited to a certain distance.

**Examples of precise senses include:**
- Human vision
- Bat hearing
- Bloodhound scent
## <u>Imprecise Senses</u>
*Imprecise* senses can still distinguish things, but not in the same range of detail that a precise sense can. An imprecise sense can still be used to distinguish a creature’s location, but they will still remain **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Hidden]]** to you without a precise sense.

**Examples of imprecise senses include:**
- Human hearing
- Untrained dog’s smell
- Elephant sight
## <u>Vague Senses</u>
*Vague* senses can alert you to something’s **presence**, but cannot reliably detect anything but the most notable of changes in the environment. A vague sense can, at best, make a creature **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Undetected]]** instead of **[[2.4 Detection Rating|Unnoticed]]**.

**Examples of vague senses include:**
- Human smell
- Bat sight
- Elephant sight
## Special Senses & Sights
Different senses can exist—like **[[Darkvision]]**, **[[Tremorsense]]**, and other such things. These specialty senses, if ever listed, will describe if they are **Precise**, **Imprecise**, or **Vague** and specify the sense’s range.

# Speed
A creature’s **Speed** is how fast it can move in ~2 seconds at a brisk pace—otherwise known as a **[[Move]]** [[action]] (explained later). For every **+2 in [[2. Attributes & C|Agility]]** past **10**, a creature gets an additional **+5 ft** land speed.
## Land Speed
**Land speed** measures distance traveled on solid, relatively-flat ground—pretty much just walking or any of its analogs.
## Swim Speed
**Swim Speed** measures distance travelled through liquids during near-total submersion. Creatures without a swim speed can still **swim**, but must make **[[Swim]]** skill checks to do so.
## Burrow Speed
**Burrow speed** measures distance traveled through loose earth or similar substances—which does **NOT** leave a tunnel of any kind unless the GM says so. Creatures with a **Hard Burrow** speed can burrow through solid stone and similar materials. Creatures without a burrow speed can’t burrow.
## Climb Speed
**Climb speed** measures distance traveled up slopes too steep to walk—be that a steep hill, sheer cliff, or blank wall. Creatures without a climb speed can still **climb**, but must make **Labor** checks to do so (and have at least 1 hand free). Climbing creatures are **NOT** able to walk upside-down or hang onto ceilings unless it’s specified that they have a **Sticky Climb Speed** or a reasonable method of doing so.
## Fly Speed
**Fly speed** measures distance traveled through the air via some sort of special locomotion. At the end of a creature’s turn, if a creature is midair and doesn’t have **Hover Flying** or hasn’t used the **Fly** action this round, it falls. Creatures can’t fly without a fly speed.
