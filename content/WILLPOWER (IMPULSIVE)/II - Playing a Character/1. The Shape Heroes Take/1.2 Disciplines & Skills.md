# What's a skill?
*Skills are the muscle memory, knowledge, instincts, and techniques utilized in performing certain courses of action or pertaining to distinct subjects. They are the meat and cheese of this system.*

When the time comes to actually take action, you're going to roll a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] (2d12+bonus).

---
# Core Skills
Before we get into that, though... there's a set of THREE very special skills that define a PC's defenses - the Core Skills. As you might've seen, they're used in calculating [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]] and [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] - and they don't belong to a Discipline or anything. The Core Skills are:
### <u>Toughness</u>
*The ability to endure physical suffering.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Physique]]
### <u>Reflex</u>
*The ability to react, with speed, to stimulus.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Agility]]
### <u>Nerve</u>
*The ability to assert your will and resist mental strain.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Awareness]]

## Discipline
A Discipline is a wide family of general techniques and knowledge that contribute to one's excellence in several different (but similar) individual skills. A Discipline's rank bonus is added to every check made for Skills within that Discipline.

Disciplines can range from +0 (Untrained) to +4 (Paragon). See [[1.8 Levels & Progression]].

## Skills
Skills are where expression matters. They range from Layman (+0) to Legend (+4). Improving your [[1.8 Levels & Progression|Skill Rank]]s leads to unlocking new and powerful [[1.5 Character Talents|Talent]]s to prove your mettle with.

# Skill DC
When a skill of yours is contested (example - your [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]] by a blade-bearing assailant), the challenger's skill check must succeed against a Skill DC. This is equal to 13 + the bonus you'd add to a roll with said contested skill: attribute, discipline, skill, and any current modifiers you may have. 

Any modifier or effect that affects your skill checks with a skill affects Skill DC in the same way. If an effect would modify a skill check, it also modifies the skill's Skill DC unless it explicitly states otherwise.
