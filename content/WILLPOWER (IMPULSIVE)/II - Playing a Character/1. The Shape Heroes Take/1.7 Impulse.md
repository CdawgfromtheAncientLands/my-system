As you build [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Triumph]], they stews and flicker within your heart - a burning mixture of dopamine and adrenaline that lets you feel unstoppable! 
# **Impulse**
This confidence manifests in [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Impulse Points]]: a pool of points that you can spend,
- In lieu of [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] (1 point = 1 [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]])
- To perform [[Impulses]] during roleplay or encounters.
Whenever your turn begins in an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]], you gain [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Impulse Points]] equal to your current [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Triumph]]. [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Impulse Points]] goes away when an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]] ends.
### Lingering Impulse
**Lingering Impulse** is like normal Impulse Points... except it sticks around between encounters - track it differently from the normal stuff.
- Whenever you gain [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Triumph]], the GM may decide to also award 1 [[1.7 Impulse|Lingering Impulse]].
- A GM may also award [[1.7 Impulse|Lingering Impulse]] for good behavior, excellent roleplay, or particularly outstanding accomplishments that don't necessarily merit gaining [[1.6 Triumph & Tension|Triumph]].
