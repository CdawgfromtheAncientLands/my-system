Every player character starts with IDENTITY. It's WHO YOU ARE! A character's identity is expressed through tags, chosen from the following pairs. Although presented in opposition, a character may choose to create a character that utilizes opposing tags. In fact, many compelling characters do; Stories in _WILLPOWER_ are not just "days in the life" - they are disruptions of the status quo.
#### **Bold or Tortured**
> **What are danger and pain, to me?**  
#### **Dutiful or Free**
> **What do I owe, and who do I owe it to?** 
#### **Detached or Passionate**
> **How much do I lean into my emotions and impulses?**  
#### **Principled or Pragmatic**
> **What matters more: what I achieve, or how I act?**  
#### **Spiritual or Skeptical**
> **Where does meaning come from - what I feel, or what I see?**  
# Identity Traits
For each tag, you (and your GM) will benefit from you writing EXACTLY TWO short sentences that describe your feelings and behavior - specifically, stated truths of how you **feel** or **view the world**.

See examples in [[2.1 Identity Tags]].
