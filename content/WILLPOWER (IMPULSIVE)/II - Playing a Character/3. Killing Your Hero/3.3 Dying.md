# Injury
When you would suffer damage that you can't mitigate with [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]], this is called [[3.3 Dying|Injury]]. When you are injured
- Tick +1 [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Physical Peril]]. If it was a Critical Hit or the damage taken is greater than your [[1.1 Attributes|PHY]] score, tick another.
- Roll a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Toughness]] save. The [[1.2 Difficulty Class|Difficulty Class]] = (10 + the suffered damage). On a failure, you drop [[2.2 Bodily Conditions|Unconscious]] and begin [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Dying]]. 
#### Condition: <u>Dying</u>
*Your life is in peril as you lie crumbling.*
- At the beginning of your [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] (or every 10 seconds), roll 1d4 ([[1.4 Flat Check|Death Save]]). 
- If the result is less than your current total [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]], tick +1 [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Physical Peril]]. 
- If you would stop [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Dying]], you regain consciousness naturally in 1d4 hours. When you awaken, suffer a [[3.4 Testing Composure|Severe (2×)]] [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]].
