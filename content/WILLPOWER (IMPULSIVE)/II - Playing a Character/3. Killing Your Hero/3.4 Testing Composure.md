**Composure** represents your ability to hold yourself together under pressure: fear, doubt, shock, and stress. When you face danger, meaningful failure, or emotionally charged situations, the GM may impose a **[[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]]** (or simply "Roll Composure") to test your resolve.
# Making a Composure Check
> As rule of thumb: **If the moment would make a reasonable person hesitate, panic, or lose focus… call for a Composure Check.**

All [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]]s start with 1d10 (the Composure Die). When making a [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]], roll the [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Die]]. On a 1...
- Degrade the dice size by 1 (1d10->1d8->1d6->1d4)
- Suffer [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +1.
- If you are below half [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]], make a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]] save (DC = )

# Testing Your Composure
Not all pressure is equal. Certain circumstances may be more dire or upsetting than others.

|Challenge|When to Use It|Checks|
|---|---|---|
|Harsh|Uncomfortable - more than just annoying. You hesitate to advance.|1 check|
|Severe|Immediate danger or meaningful failure. Something has gone quite wrong.|2 checks|
|Traumatic|Major turning points, trauma, or overwhelming fear. Doom is on the horizon.|3 checks|

Regardless of intensity, a single event should NEVER trigger more than one [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]].

---
## Example Triggers
The following game events should always be accompanied by a [[3.4 Testing Composure|Composure Check]] of 
#### Harsh
- Beginning a meaningful combat [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]] or being ambushed
- [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]]ing a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]
- Suffering a [[Critical Hit]]
- Sudden and shocking brutality
- An ally suffers a [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]
- You or an ally suffers [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Injury]]
- Acting against your [[1. Character Identity 1|Identity]] or having it challenged.
### Severe
- An ally begins [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Dying]] or dies.
- Horrific or gut-wrenching brutality
### Traumatic
- A treasured ally dies

- Entering meaningful danger
- Escalation of stakes
- Consequences of failure
- Emotional turning points

# Player Approach

You can:
- **Avoid pressure** through preparation and caution
- **Push through it** when the situation demands
- **Mitigate it** with positioning, abilities, or resources
- **Take risks** when the reward is worth it
