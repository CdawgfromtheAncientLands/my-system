# What is Peril?
**Peril** is a tracker of how *doomed* you are.

It measures how close you are to irreparably breaking - physically, mentally, or both. It represents mounting injuries, faltering resolve, and the world closing in around you. As Peril rises, everything becomes harder, riskier, and more desperate.

As you tick [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]], it will have two tracks: physical and mental.
### Effects
You suffer a penalty equal to your total [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] to all [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s you make.
- If your total [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] is 3 or more, treat all of your [[1.1 Skill Checks|Failure]]s as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]]s.
- If your total [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] reaches 5, check what you have more of:
#### Traumatic Break
If you have more *mental peril*, then you are forever scarred.
#### Death or Severe Disability
If you have more *physical peril*, you DIE... or you be so disfigured/disabled you may wish you were dead.
