Not all harm is the same. 

A blade slices, a hammer crushes, fire consumes, and darker forces erode the mind or soul itself. In **WILLPOWER**, damage is categorized by how it affects you - what kind of force you’re enduring, and what defenses can stand against it. Understanding these damage types lets you anticipate threats, exploit weaknesses, and choose the right protection when it matters most.

Mechanically, this is ULTRA simple.
- Something deals [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]].
- The victim's [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] or [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Resistance]] reduces that damage by an amount.
- The victim mitigates the remaining damage with [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]] or is injured.

There are three overall categories of damage:
- [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Material Damage]], tangible harm that can be blocked or reduced by physical barriers.
- [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Immaterial Damage]] that bypasses conventional defenses and targets the body, mind, or soul more directly.
- [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Precision Damage]] - an extra special modifier representing devastating hits to vital points.

# Material Damages

## **Kinetic Damage**
Kinetic Damage results from direct physical impact: force applied through mass and motion. It includes:
- **🗡 Blade**: cutting and slicing strikes that exploit openings and carve through exposed material.
- **🔨 Blunt**: heavy impacts that crush, stagger, or transfer force through armor.
- **🔫 Ballistic**: ultra-fast piercing projectiles with a unique profile of force.
- **💥 Blast**: concussive shockwaves that  with sudden, overwhelming pressure.

## **Elemental Damage**
Elemental Damage is caused by extreme or unstable energy acting on a creature or object. Unlike Kinetic Damage, Elemental Damage carries **secondary effects** that alter the battlefield, disrupt targets, or create ongoing pressure.

- **🔥Fire**: Intense heat that burns, spreads, and worsens if ignored.
	*Elemental Effect:* The victim suffers *[[2.8 External Conditions|Burning]] (Fire) +1d4*. If they already are *Burning (Fire)*, they instead suffer *Burning (Fire) +1*. Damage from the condition cannot trigger the effect.

- **❄Cold**: Extreme cold that stiffens the body and locks down movement.
	*Elemental Effect:* The victim suffers *[[2.8 External Conditions|Chilled]] +1* until the end of their next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]].

- **⚡Electricity**: Violent electrical currents that interfere with control and coordination.
	*Elemental Effect:* The victim treats a random item they're holding as if though they were [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]]fully [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]]ed. If the affected item was already affected by [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]], they drop the item.

- **🧪Acid**: Corrosive reactions that break down material and defenses.
	*Elemental Effect:* The victim's Wear [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] suffers [[2.5 Item Conditions|Worn]] +1.
#### Elemental Effect Rules
Elemental effects trigger when:
- A creature suffers *3+ of an Elemental damage type* **after [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]]/[[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Resistance]]**, or
- The damage comes from a **Critical Hit**, **or**
- From a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]] against a damaging effect.
When a creature suffers an elemental effect, the threshold for triggering increases by **1** damage, resetting at the end of the affected creature's next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]].

# Immaterial Damages
Immaterial Damage bypasses conventional physical defenses, targeting deeper aspects of a creature. These effects are often harder to resist and may behave differently from Material Damage.

- **☠ Toxic**: poisonous infiltration that degrades the body’s internal systems, often lingering or worsening over time.
- **🧠 Mental**: direct disruption of thought, perception, and cognition, overwhelming the mind itself.  reduces one's reserves of [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] instead of [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]].
- **💀 Entropy**: the breakdown of a creature’s physical structure, accelerating decay and collapse at a fundamental level. After suffering Entropy Damage, a creature cannot regain [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]] until the end of its next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]].

---
### **Precision Damage**
**🎯 Precision**: a special damage type representing strikes against vital areas. Precision Damage is typically applied alongside another damage type, increasing its lethality when an opening is exploited - such as a [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Deadly}]] weapon on a critical hit.

---
# Armor & Resistance
**Armor & Resistance** protect you from harm by reducing the damage you take. **Armor** comes from Wear gear (plate, leather, shields) and can be damaged or removed. **Resistance** is innate; granted by your body, training, or supernatural traits, and cannot be taken away so easily. 

When you suffer damage, subtract both your Armor and your relevant Resistance from it - the rest is what gets through.
