**Artifice** is creation, engineering, and the manipulation of tools, structures, and devices. It governs your understanding of how things are built, how they break, and how they can be repaired or repurposed.
### **Mechanical** [[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]
Gears, levers, moving parts.
- Clockworks
- Engines and simple machines
### **Electrical** [[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]
Power systems, circuits, energy flow.
- Wiring, conductivity
- Basic power troubleshooting
### **Structural / Civil** [[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]
Architecture, load-bearing, and building design.
- Strongholds, bridges, foundations
- Hazard assessment
### **Fabrication** [[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]
Crafting, shaping, and assembling components.
- Metalwork, carpentry, 3D shaping
- Creating tools or prototypes
