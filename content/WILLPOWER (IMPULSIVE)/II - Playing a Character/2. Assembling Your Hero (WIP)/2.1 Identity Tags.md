# Bold or Tortured
*What are danger and pain, to me?*
#### BOLD
A *Bold* *character* overcomes through action. When a problem presents itself, it is to be handled - swiftly and decisively, with varying degrees of safety or elegance. To the *Bold*, danger and pain are signals that tell them "Push forward"!

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Escalate recklessly and force confrontation.

A bold [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels that they must always act as soon as they can - or else things will get worse.
Some examples of [[2.1 Identity Tags|Bold]] [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I will not be stopped until I've seen something through."
- "If I hesitate, I lose control of the situation."
- "If I'm afraid, that just means I need to push harder."

A [[2.1 Identity Tags|Bold]] [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] idealizes decisiveness and speed-of-action - after all, thinking never solved a problem on its own.
Some examples of [[2.1 Identity Tags|Bold]] [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "Problems should be met head-on."
- "If no one acts, nothing changes - so I must."
- "Waiting only lets an issue fester."
#### TORTURED
A _Tortured_ character is shaped by pain. When suffering appears, it is not cleanly avoided or overcome - it lingers, complicates, and demands to be reckoned with - sometimes to great catharsis. To the _Tortured_, danger and pain are not just obstacles... they are burdens that must be carried, understood, or resolved.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Fixate and act self-destructively.

A _Tortured_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to engage with suffering - lingering on it, absorbing it, or struggling to move past it even when they should.  
Some examples of _Tortured_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I can't move on until I understand why it stings."
- "Unless I suffered for it, I haven't earned it."
- "Backing down would make everything I’ve suffered pointless."

A _Tortured_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that pain has weight - that it changes people, settles debts, or cannot simply be ignored.  
Some examples of _Tortured_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "Anyone who isn't afraid of anything is lying to themselves."
- "Everyone needs to face the music someday."
- "You can tell who someone is by what they can’t let go of."

# Dutiful or Free
*What do I owe, and who do I owe it to?*
#### DUTIFUL
A _Dutiful_ character is defined by responsibility. When something becomes their obligation, it must be carried through - regardless of cost, comfort, or personal desire. To the _Dutiful_, life is a series of weights to bear; bearing them is proof of a life worthwhile.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Fulfill your most immediate obligations and promises over all else.

A _Dutiful_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to uphold responsibility - stepping in, holding the line, or carrying burdens others cannot.  
Some examples of _Dutiful_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "If I’m responsible for it, I stay until it’s done."
- "If no one else will do it, then I have to."
- "I cannot abandon something that depends on me."

A _Dutiful_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that responsibility defines a person - that what you owe matters more than what you want.  
Some examples of _Dutiful_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "Strength exists to be used for others."
- "A promise should be kept, no matter the cost."
- "Structure should be maintained to keep things from falling apart."
#### FREE
A _Free_ character rejects control. When pressure, authority, or expectation closes in, it must be broken, slipped, or torn away. To the _Free_, obligation is a cage... the moment it tightens, something inside them pushes back.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Reject obligations, break away, assert independence.

A _Free_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to resist being controlled - pulling away from pressure, direction, or expectation the moment it becomes binding.  
Some examples of _Free_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I push back the moment I feel cornered."
- "I can’t stand being told what to do."
- "I lose interest the moment I’m forced into something."

A _Free_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that autonomy is essential - that choice, independence, and self-direction matter more than safety or structure.  
Some examples of _Free_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "No one should be controlled against their will."
- "My freedom matters more than my safety."
- "A life under another's yoke isn't worth living."

# Detached or Passionate
*How much do I lean into my emotions and impulses?*
#### DETACHED
A _Detached_ character maintains control through distance. Emotion is a dangerous influence to be managed (or even ignored) so it does not interfere with judgement or action. To the _Detached_, losing control is more dangerous than any external threat.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Suppress any empathy for yourself or others.

A _Detached_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to maintain composure - pulling back from emotional influence to stay functional and in control.  
Some examples of _Detached_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I don’t move until I’m in control of myself."
- "If I get attached, I make worse decisions."
- "I keep distance from the things I care about."

A _Detached_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that control and clarity come from distance - that emotion must be managed to avoid mistakes.  
Some examples of _Detached_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "I have to put myself first."
- "Distance makes better decisions."
- "Feelings just make a clean situation messy."
#### PASSIONATE
A _Passionate_ character embraces emotion as a source of meaning and action. Deep feeling isn't some corrupting animal influence, but a signal of what matters. To the _Passionate_, suppressing emotion like driving through life with your eyes closed.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Act impulsively on emotion - protect, lash out, or commit fully without restraint.

A _Passionate_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to act on emotion - leaning into instinct, connection, and intensity in the moment.  
Some examples of _Passionate_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I can't act without feeling something first."
- "I refuse to hold things in."
- "If I care about something, I'll commit to it."

A _Passionate_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that emotion reveals truth - that what you feel is what gives meaning to your choices.  
Some examples of _Passionate_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "Action without emotion is just cruelty."
- "Strong feelings should not be questioned."
- "Strong feelings demand action."

# Pragmatic or Principled
*What matters more - what I achieve, or how I act?*
#### PRINCIPLED
A _Principled_ character is anchored by rules, values, or codes. When faced with uncertainty, they fall back on what they believe is _right_, regardless of outcome. To the _Principled_, abandoning your standards is worse than any other kind of failure.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Refuse compromise and adhere strictly to your beliefs.

A _Principled_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to act in accordance with their ideals - even when it’s inconvenient, dangerous, or costly.  
Some examples of _Principled_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I won't do something I know is wrong, even if it would help."
- "If I break my word, I’m nothing."
- "I would rather fail a mission than fail my morals."

A _Principled_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that integrity defines worth - that the way you act matters more than what you gain.  
Some examples of _Principled_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "How I act matters more than what I get out of it."
- "The wrong way stays wrong, even if it works."
- "Compromise wears away at a person's soul."
#### PRAGMATIC
A _Pragmatic_ character is driven by results. When faced with uncertainty, they choose whatever works - adapting, bending rules, or abandoning ideals if necessary. To the _Pragmatic_, outcomes define success... everything else is noise.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Pursue the most effective path.

A _Pragmatic_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to solve problems by any means necessary - adjusting tactics or morals to fit the situation.  
Some examples of _Pragmatic_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I won’t waste time on frivolous things."
- "I don’t let appearances get in the way of results."
- "I hate second-guessing something that already works."

A _Pragmatic_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that results justify action - that success, survival, or completion are what truly matter.  
Some examples of _Pragmatic_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "People need results more than good intentions."
- "Rules should be ignored the moment they get in the way."
- "Succeeding matters more than 'doing it the right way'."

# Spiritual or Skeptical
*Where does meaning come from - what I feel, or what I see?*
#### Spiritual
A _Spiritual_ character trusts in meaning that cannot always be proven. When faced with uncertainty, they rely on belief, instinct, or conviction to guide them forward. To the _Spiritual_, doubt is an illusion and trust is what gives direction.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Commit fully to your conviction, trust it with your life, ignore contradictions.

A _Spiritual_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to trust the intangible - leaning into instinct, conviction, or belief even without proof.  
Some examples of _Spiritual_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I don't need to explain something I know is right."
- "If I second-guess it, it probably wasn't meant to be."
- "Doubt only slows me down."

A _Spiritual_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that meaning must be trusted - that not everything important can be proven or explained.  
Some examples of _Spiritual_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "Not everything real can be proven."
- "Some things don't need your trust to work."
- "Meaning comes from what you believe, not what you can prove."
## SKEPTICAL

A _Skeptical_ character questions meaning that cannot be verified. When faced with uncertainty, they seek evidence, consistency, or proof before committing. To the _Skeptical_, belief without grounding is a risk... and risk demands scrutiny.

**[[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]:** Refuse to act: stall, question, and second-guess.

A _Skeptical_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] feels compelled to question and verify - resisting commitment until something makes sense or holds up to scrutiny.  
Some examples of _Skeptical_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Heart]] traits:
- "I don’t move until I know what's going on."
- "If it can't be proven, I refuse to trust it."
- "I will second-guess almost everything."

A _Skeptical_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] believes that truth must be tested - that meaning comes from what can be observed, proven, or repeated.  
Some examples of _Skeptical_ [[1.0 Character Identity|Soul]] traits:
- "If it can't be tested, it can't be trusted."
- "Verifiable patterns mean more than hearsay."
- "The road to Hell is paved with assumptions."
