Some environments feel particularly soothing or oppressive. 

| **Mood**       | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Soothing**   | _A gentle, restorative influence._  <br>• +2 to the results of [[1.4 Secondary Attribute\|Endurance]] dice rolled during rest.<br>• Restore 1 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute\|Focus]] every 10 minutes. |
| **Nice**       | _Pleasant, comfortable, unthreatening._  <br>• +1 to the results of [[1.4 Secondary Attribute\|Endurance]] dice rolled during rest.                                                                |
| **Normal**     | _No special effect._ Most places will be like this.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Oppressive** | _Heaviness, stagnation, pressure._  <br>• Every 30 minutes, suffer 1d3 [[3.4 Testing Composure\|Stress]] (same number applies to each PC).                                                                    |
| **Dreadful**   | _A place that doesn’t want you here._  <br>• Every **10 minutes**, suffer **1d2 Stress**.  <br>• -1 to the results of [[1.4 Secondary Attribute\|Endurance]] dice rolled during rest.              |
