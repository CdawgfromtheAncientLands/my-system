# **Pain is Mine to Bear**

You treat suffering as something you owe - not to anyone else, but to the world itself. Pain feels like equilibrium, like a debt being paid down stroke by stroke. Comfort confuses you. Protection embarrasses you. You take blows no one asked you to take, carry loads no one meant for you, because some part of you believes you’re supposed to. When others insist you don’t deserve the hurt, you give them that soft, pitying look… because they don’t understand.

_People may call you selfless, broken, stubborn, or unbearably brave._

• _“It’s fine. I can take it.”_  
• _“Your kindness is a waste, I don't need it.”_  
• _“No, I'm fine. Thanks.”_

# **Some Things Can’t Be Forgiven**

You believe certain wounds don’t close - shouldn’t close. Forgiveness is a luxury the world doesn’t always earn. When harm goes deep enough, the bond is gone, the trust is gone, the door stays shut. You stay vigilant because you remember too well what happened last time. Even innocent echoes of the past put your guard up. You’re not out for vengeance, but you can't pretend every wrong truly has its right.

_People may call you principled, harsh, traumatized, or unyielding._

• _“That line won’t be crossed twice.”_  
• _“I’m not forgetting what they did.”_  
• _“This... is unforgivable.”_

# **Someone Has to Pay**

You can’t swallow injustice. Every time evil goes without consequence, something wrenches inside of you. Wrongdoing demands balance. A debt hangs in the air until someone answers for it. When justice can’t reach the guilty, you look for the angle, the workaround, the pressure point that restores equilibrium. Mercy isn’t off the table, but only after a proper price has been paid.

_People may call you righteous, relentless, unsettling, or uncompromising._

• _“Debts don’t vanish. They’re paid.”_  
• _“If justice won’t come, I will.”_  
• _“Balance matters more than comfort.”_
