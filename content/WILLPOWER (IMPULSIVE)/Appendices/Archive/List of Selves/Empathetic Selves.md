# **Hope is My Blade**

Hope isn't only comfort - it’s forward momentum that every feeling being needs to survive. You lie if you must, perform if you must, push if you must, because despair is the true enemy and fear is the mind killer. When people start to fold, you seize their attention and drag them upward, even if you break under the weight.

_People may call you uplifting, unrealistic, dramatic, or coercive._

• _“Look at me. We're not done.”_  
• _“Onward! There is no way but onward.”_  
• _“No, no... it's fine, see? We'll get over this easily.”_

# **Generous Redeemer**

You treat restraint, forgiveness, and second chances as tools that reshape outcomes. Mercy is not softness - it’s intervention. Even when it hurts you, even when it risks the plan, you’d rather redirect someone’s life than end it. You hold the line on compassion so hard it becomes its own kind of pressure. When mercy fails, you don’t fold - you push again, because you can't afford to give up on the world.

_People may call you compassionate, naive, stubborn, or wiser than you look._

• _“Put the weapon down... can we end this another way?”_  
• _“How can we see people grow if we never give them a chance?”_  
• _“It's fine, really. Take it, please. You deserve it.”_

# **No Man Left Behind**

You can’t stomach the thought of someone falling through the cracks. Not on your watch, not ever. Leaving anyone behind feels like a personal failure, a guilt that claws too deep to ignore. So you turn back. You rush in. You drag people out even when the cost is brutal. Abandonment is worse than death - for you and for others.

_People may call you loyal, reckless, self-sacrificing, or painfully human._

• _“I'm going back for him. You don't have to follow me.”_  
• *“We LET THEM make that mistake... we've got to get them out of it.”*  
• _“I'm not moving until you do... get up, damn you!!.”_
