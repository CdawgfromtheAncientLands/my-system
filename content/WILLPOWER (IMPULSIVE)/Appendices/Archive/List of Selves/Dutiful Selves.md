# **Those Who Can, Must**

You believe power exists to be used responsibly. If you can lift a weight - physical, social, or otherwise - you *should*. Strength creates obligation. When someone is outmatched, you must step in - regardless of permission, standing, or feelings. A world worth living in is one where those who can bear the blow step forward first.

_People may call you noble, rigid, patronizing, or steadfast._

• _“Get behind me. This part’s mine.”_  
• _“I can't abide this. I need to do something.”_  
• _“You don’t have to like it... you just have to stay safe!”_

# **Words Are Sacred**

Your word is a contract - once spoken, it becomes a fixed point you refuse to betray. A promise is how trust survives stress, and breaking one rots everything around it. You stick to commitments past the point of comfort, sense, or self-preservation, because the alternative feels like tearing your own foundation out from under you. 

_People may call you reliable, inflexible, old-fashioned, or intense._

• _“I said so. That’s enough.”_  
• _“...Do you promise?”_  
• _“I can't. Please don't push it.”_

# **Anchored in Order**

You trust structure. Rules, routines, and plans aren’t constraints - they’re how people stay safe and how problems stay small. Chaos is what happens when everyone decides they’re the exception - and when that happens, sticking to the plan is how you stay anchored. 

_People may call you organized, tedious, or dependable._

• _“Follow the steps. They’re there for a reason.”_  
• _“You should have told me that BEFORE we actually got here.”_  
• _“Quit improvising and hold the damn line!”_

# **Ride or Die**

Your duty is to your people - the ones you’ve chosen or bled beside. Strategies shift, rules bend, promises break, but loyalty is the line you never cross. When danger rises, you side with your circle first and sort the fallout later. You fight harder for them, take risks you’d never take for strangers, and refuse to abandon anyone who carries your trust. If they betray you...

_People may call you devoted, shortsighted, dependable, or compromised._

• _“They’re mine. I stand with mine.”_  
• _“I care about them, not the odds!”_  
• _“I know you. I'll do it.”_
