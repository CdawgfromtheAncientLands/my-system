# Light Carry Kit
_A stripped-down system of reinforced belts, cross-straps, and concealed pockets, designed to keep the body unencumbered and reactive._

This kit favors items worn _on_ the body rather than carried _on_ the back: belt sheaths, wrist loops, inner linings, and close-fitting harnesses. Everything is placed for immediate reach and minimal shift during movement. There is little room for bulk - but nothing rattles, drags, or needs to be dug out.

**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]]:** 4 kg  
**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Weight Negation]]:** 2 kg  
**Reflex Penalty:** —

# Battle Carry Kit

_A dense, front-loaded harness of bandoliers, chest rigs, weapon slings, and rigid mounts built for immediate violence._

This kit assumes combat is imminent. Weapons, shields, ammunition, and combat tools are mounted forward and high on the body, favoring draw speed and redundancy over balance or comfort. Weight is concentrated on the torso, limiting twist, footwork, and recovery - but ensuring that nothing needed in the opening moments of a fight is ever out of reach. 

**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]]:** 6 kg  
**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Weight Negation]]:** 5 kg  
**Reflex Penalty:** –1

# Balanced Carry Kit
_A practical arrangement of belt pouches, modular straps, and a compact pack meant to support a full adventuring day._

This kit distributes weight across the hips and shoulders while keeping commonly used items close at hand. Weapons, tools, and consumables remain accessible without sacrificing room for rations, spare gear, or loot. It’s designed to stay out of the way in motion while still carrying enough to be self-sufficient. 

**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]]:** 3 kg  
**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Weight Negation]]:** 7 kg  
**Reflex Penalty:** –1

# Exploration Carry Kit
_A framed pack with external lash points, layered compartments, and weight-forward storage built for distance and endurance._

This kit prioritizes carrying _everything you might need_ over being ready at a moment’s notice. Gear is packed securely and efficiently, often requiring time to access. Bedrolls, tools, spare supplies, and environmental equipment dominate the load. 

**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]]:** 1 kg  
**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Weight Negation]]:** 9 kg  
**Reflex Penalty:** –1

# Mule Carry Kit

_A reinforced load frame with heavy harnessing, layered packs, and rigid supports meant to move serious weight._

This kit turns the wearer into a mobile supply depot. Most of the load is secured to a rigid frame and balanced for long hauls rather than agility. Drawing items takes effort, and sudden movement is punished by inertia, but the sheer volume of gear carried can sustain a party or haul valuable salvage. Chosen by porters, logistics-minded adventurers, or anyone willing to be slow so others don’t have to be unprepared.

**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]]:** 1 kg  
**[[3.1 Carry Kits|Weight Negation]]:** 18 kg  
**Reflex Penalty:** –2
