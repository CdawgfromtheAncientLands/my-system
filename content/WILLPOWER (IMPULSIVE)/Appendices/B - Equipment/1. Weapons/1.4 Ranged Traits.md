### **{Manual}**
This weapon requires a deliberate mechanical action (bolt cycling, hammer-cocking, cranking, etc.).  Whenever you perform a [[Strikes|Strike]] action with this weapon, you must perform an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action as part of the [[Strikes|Strike]]'s cost.

---
###  **{Misfire} X**
This weapon is prone to malfunction. Whenever you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon, treat both dice as maxing a DC X [[1.4 Flat Check]]. If both flat checks fail,  the weapon loses 1 durability and is rendered non-functional until cleared. 

To clear it, spend an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action and make a [[Survival|Fieldcraft]], or [[Artifice|Mechanical]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] (Simple = DC 15, Martial = DC 17, Advanced = 19). 
- [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]]: Weapon is functional again.
- [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]]: Weapon suffers -1 durability.

For each point of difference between a weapon’s **maximum Durability** and its **current Durability**, increase its Misfire rating by 1 (Misfire 1+ → 2+ → 3+, etc.) until repaired.

---
### **{Volley} X**
This weapon struggles at short range. When you [[Strikes|Strike]] a target within **X feet**, suffer -3 to the [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]].

---
### **{Versatile} (ranged)**
This weapon may be wielded in 1 or 2 hands.
- When wielding a 1-handed weapon in 2 hands, treat the weapon's weight class as 1 lighter.
- When wielding a 2-handed weapon in 1 hand, suffer -2 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s with the weapon.
