For travel that is measured in hours, not days or weeks, you may want to consult this page.

In general, when travelling, a creature's MPH (for an intentional marching-like pace) is equal to a quarter of their [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]] - so a creature with 20ft land speed is moving at 5mph.

This activity is considered Marching.

---
# March
**Effect:** You move at a brisk walking pace equal to a quarter of your [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]] in mph, rounded down to the nearest mile (up or down). Time spent Marching accounts for occasional rests and handwaves short mealtimes (unless time is particularly tight).

Every 6h marched in a day increases your [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Fatigued]] by +1.

# Hustling
Sometimes, it may be beneficial, wise, or necessary to *run*. For extended periods of time.

The GM may adjudicate exactly how this plays out. This will involve skill checks, possible [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] or [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] costs, and some other stuff.
