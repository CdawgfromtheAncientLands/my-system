# Short Rest - 10 Minutes
**Requirements:** A safe place to rest. 
**Effect:** Every 10 minutes spent resting, you may choose one:
- Spend 1 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Endurance]] dice and restore that much [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] or [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
- Spend 2 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Endurance]] to reduce your [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] from [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]s by 1.

# Long Rest - 10 Hours
**Requirements:** A comfortable place to rest, using 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep and 2 hours of light activity. An uncomfortable but still serviceable resting place (uncovered sleeping bag in the grass) can't grant more than the half-level benefits. [[8. Environmental Mood|Oppressive]] or [[8. Environmental Mood|Dreadful]] environments cannot grant more than the lowest-level benefits.

**Effect:** Restore your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] and [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to full,  progress your [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Injury]] [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Heal Period]]s by 1 day, and regain half your maximum [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Endurance]] dice. Clear all [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]] from [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]]s. Reduce your [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Fatigued]] condition by 1.
- If you slept at least half the required time (but not the full time), then you may only restore your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] OR your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] to full (the other is unchanged). Your [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Fatigued]] condition is unaffected.
- If you slept less than half the required time, restore half of either your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] or [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]], restore no more than 3 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Endurance]], and suffer [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Fatigued]] +1.
- If you do not take the 2 hours of activity before or after the rest, you are [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Fatigued]] +1 for an hours after waking up.

# Going Without Rest
You will suffer +1 mental [[3.2 Mortal Peril|Peril]].
