# Look Out
**Effect:** You prepare for an incoming confrontation. Gain +2 to the first [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]] roll you make while on the [[1.4 Other Activities|Look Out]] - and when you roll that [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]], you may [[Defensive Actions|Defend]] any number of times (once per relevant item you're wielding).

You cannot be on the [[1.4 Other Activities|Look Out]] and perform any other kind of activity besides travelling at half-speed.

# Track/Search
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Effect:** You track a creature or scan your environment as you travel at half-speed, rolling relevant [[Observation|Perception]] or [[Survival|Track]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s as the GM requires. You may move at full speed, but suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] twice over on the skill checks if you do.

---
# Avoid Notice
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Effect:** As [[1.4 Other Activities|Track/Search]], but you move stealthily to avoid detection instead. 

---
# Investigate
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Effect:** Roll [[Observation|Investigation]] with the goal of discovering something hidden. The time it takes to find something depends on what there is to find and how complex the environment you’re searching is – a lived-in apartment might take 1 to 2 hours to search through, a cabinet could take a few minutes, and so on. A GM may rule that particularly high degrees of success make investigation take shorter than it should.
