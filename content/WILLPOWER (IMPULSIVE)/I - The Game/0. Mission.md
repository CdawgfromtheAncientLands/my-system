This section might be a bit boring for those uninterested in the deeper details... but here's a little information on how I came to build **WILLPOWER:** a tactical RPG where character psychology meaningfully drives gameplay.

---

So I like TTRPGs. I like playing them a lot. I've played D&D 5e and Pathfinder 2e the most. I like the fights. I like roleplaying. I like planning, buying things, exploring, and experiencing a whole different world... and being important in it! I like the character options, too. I HATE.... that they're *generally inconsiderate toward any sort of social or mental meaning*. 

I'd love to be playing D&D and have my class linked to my personality, have hooks and powers that incentivize me to act a certain way SOCIALLY, not just mechanically. In D&D, the abilities influence how the players use them - but there's no more feedback there. Abilities and talents don't say anything about the character's feelings or mentality.

Then, I look at something like Masks... I don't like how loose it is. Then, Burning Wheel... the combat makes me upset, too complex. Cypher? Cyberpunk!? Idunno... I really like the action system of PF2e/Lancer

So... BOOM! **WILLPOWER**. Narratively-enhanced Tactical RPG. My hope is that this game is used to fuel and enhance long-term stories (9+ Sessions) with rich character moments, risky fun, and memorable PCs... while tuning my ideal version of tactical combat along the way.

Unfortunately for me, that means that the game has a little more overhead.. playing a 3rd-level AoW character is like a 7th-level 5e one. Oh well, that's the price you pay..! We'll see if it's worth it.
