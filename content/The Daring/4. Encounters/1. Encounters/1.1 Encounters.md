Whenever it's deemed necessary to divide things into a turn-based play mode, an **Encounter** is called by the GM. An encounter is defined by Rounds, Turns, Initiative, and the Actions performed in the encounter.
# Rounds & Turns
A **Turn** is a participant's allotted time in the spotlight during an encounter. 
A **Round** is the period of time it takes for one cycle of **Turns** to pass.
