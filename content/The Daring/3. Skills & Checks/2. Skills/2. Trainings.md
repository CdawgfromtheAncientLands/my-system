Certain specialties are not explicitly mentioned in the skill list, being too specific to make a distinct skill out of while possibly also being included in the use of a preexisting skill. These are [[2. Trainings]]s, and they add a bonus to skill checks.

A Lore should NEVER be considered an outright advantage in every combat (unless a specific feat would make it so) and should NEVER be as or more generally applicable than any skill listed in this volume.

| Lore Rank | Dice Bonus | Exp cost to reach from previous rank |
| --------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Adept     | +1d4       | 7                                    |
| Expert    | +1d6       | 11                                   |
| Master    | +1d8       | 15                                   |

### **Language Lore** 
*Language Lore* doesn’t necessarily add to skill checks but determines proficiency with a language. 
- Adepts have an awkward conversational grasp, 
- Experts can converse without much issue 
- Masters have high-level understanding of the language. 
- Legends have an incredible sense of some shit
> EX: English, Spanish, Venusian, etc.
### **Martial Arts Lore**
**Martial Arts Lore** acts as prerequisite for unlocking special talents and abilities, being otherwise useless.
> EX: Kung fu, Sambo, karate, taekwondo, so on.
### **Specialty Lore**
**Specialty Lore** adds its bonus to recalling knowledge about or performing activities related to a certain subject.
> EX: Smithing, accounting, appraisal, bicycling, lip-reading, boating, interpretive dance, gardening, knot-tying, farming, embalming, sculpting, gambling, geography, mapping, Egyptology, Greece, fiction, superheroes, flowers.
### **Instrument Lore**
*Instrument Lore* is necessary to play an instrument. The bonus is added to checks made regarding its respective instrument.
- Adept is the minimum  investment necessary to proficiently play an instrument. 
> EX: Oboe, double bass, guitar, waterphone, sousaphone, xylophone, French horn.
