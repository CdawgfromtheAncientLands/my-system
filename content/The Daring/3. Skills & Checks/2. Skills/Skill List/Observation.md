Observation is the union of sensory acuity, analytical strength, and emotional insight. It is both what you notice at a glance and what you uncover with careful attention, whether studying a crime scene or reading someone’s expression.
### **Perception** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|INS]]
Noticing details, threats, and changes in the immediate environment.
- Spotting ambushes
- Hearing distant movement
- Detecting hidden objects through sight, sound, or smell alone
### **Investigation** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|INS]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Studying patterns, evidence, and anomalies within the environment to gather/infer details.
- Intentionally checking for traps or secrets
- Reconstructing events
- Logical deduction
### **Empathy** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|INS]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|CHA]]
Reading emotions, motives, or subtle cues.
- Sensing lies or tension
- Understanding intentions
- Gauging trustworthiness
