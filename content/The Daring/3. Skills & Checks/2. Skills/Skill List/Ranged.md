Ranged measures your ability to attack from afar - whether with bows, firearms, or hurled weapons. It blends precision, timing, and an understanding of trajectories, recoil, and how to strike targets that are moving, obscured, or distant.
### **Archery** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
Drawn-and-loosed projectile weapons.
### **Pistols** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
One-handed firearms.
### **Longarms** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
Two-handed firearms or ranged platforms.
### **Throwing** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|AGI]]
Thrown weapons.
