# Help (⋄)
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must be able to meaningfully assist the other creature’s attempt.
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> Describe how you assist another creature ("I encourage them" isn't enough). Propose a Skill relevant to the assistance. The GM approves the Skill and sets the Action’s traits and cost (usually a Minor action).
Roll the chosen **Skill** versus an appropriate **DC** determined by the GM.
- **Crit Success**. As Success, but +2 instead.
- **Success.** You meaningfully assist them: they gain +1 to their next [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] that contributes toward whatever you helped them with.
- **Crit Fail.** As success but you hinder the ally; they suffer **-1** instead.

**Limits & Notes:** Help cannot be used when the fiction makes assistance impossible (too far away, restrained, unaware, or unable to meaningfully contribute). Different types of Help may have additional traits (Manipulate, Mental, Auditory, etc.).
# Change Grip (⋄)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must be holding something.
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> You shift your grip on an item you’re holding - changing hands, adjusting leverage, or reducing the number of hands committed to it. If you use this to release an item, it is a [[1. Types of Actions|Free ✧ Action]] and the item will fall into your space.
# Interact (⋄)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Melee}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have at least one free hand.
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> You handle, manipulate, or operate something physically—an object, tool, mechanism, or part of the environment. Common uses include: 
- retrieving or stowing an item, 
- drawing a weapon, 
- opening a door, 
- swapping one held item for another, 
- or manipulating anything small or tactile.

**Limits:** Interact cannot replace the effects of specialized actions (e.g., Disable, Steal, Administer First Aid).  
If interacting with a held or guarded object, you will make skill checks at GM discretion.
# Grab an Edge (↻)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Trigger:</font> You would fall past an edge, handhold, or other ledge capable of supporting a grip.
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have at least one usable arm.
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> Attempt to grab the edge by rolling [[Athletics|Acrobatics]] or **[[Core Skills|Reflex]]** versus a DC set by the fall height and surface.
- **Crit Success.** You catch the edge cleanly and stop your fall. No hand is required to remain free afterward.
- **Success.** You grab the edge and stop your fall. You take fall damage as if the fall were **20 ft shorter** than the distance fallen before grabbing.
- **Crit Fail.** You fumble and strike the ledge, taking **half** the fall damage you would have taken had you hit the ground

**Limits:** Pulling yourself onto stable ground is a [[Movement Actions|Climb]] action unless the GM rules otherwise.
# Finger (✧)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Auditory}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Visual}]]
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> You subtly call attention to a creature that is not [[Condition|Unnoticed]] to you. Any creature that can see or hear you increases its [[Detection Rating]] of the pointed-out target by **1**, but cannot exceed your own [[Detection Rating]] of that creature.
# Administer First Aid (⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Interact}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must possess medical tools equivalent to a **First Aid Kit** and have a free hand or be wielding the kit.
<font color="#d83931">Effect:</font> Perform emergency medical treatment. Roll **First Aid** versus an appropriate DC based on the patient’s condition.
- **Crit Success.**  
    The patient stops **Dying**, regains consciousness in 1d6 hours, _or_ gains advantage on their next flat check to resist **Poison** or **Disease**.  
    If suffering from **Persistent Bleed**, they immediately make a flat check with a **Bonus Die**.
- **Success.**  
    The patient stabilizes one **Death Save**, or gains advantage on their next save vs **Poison/Disease**, or makes an immediate flat check to end **Persistent Bleed**.
- **Fail.**  
    No effect.
- **Crit Fail.**  
    You deal **1d4 Blunt Damage** or apply **1 Physical Stress** (GM decides based on injury severity).

**Limits:** Some conditions or damage types may require specialized kits beyond First Aid.

# Toss an Item (⋄/⟐)

[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]

<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must be holding an item you can physically throw.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> You hurl an item toward a space or creature.  
If targeting a creature, make a **Throw** attack check versus their **Reflex** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]].

- **Crit Success.** You strike your exact target. If the target is a creature, apply any effects of a thrown improvised weapon (GM discretion based on weight and shape).
    
- **Success.** You hit the intended space or creature.
    
- **Fail.** The object misses and lands **1d12 ft** away from the intended space in a random direction (GM chooses or rolls). It may hit an obstacle along its flight path.
    
- **Crit Fail.** If the object’s trajectory contains an obstacle, the GM determines whether the object impacts it.  
    If there are no obstacles, the object flies wildly, landing **3d10 ft** away in a random direction.
    

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but:

- You may adjust the angle and spin of the throw, gaining **+2** to the attack check or to any check used to avoid trajectory obstacles.
    
- On a Success, the object lands exactly where you choose within its throw arc (no scatter).

# Thrown Object Guidelines

Thrown object performance depends on its mass relative to the thrower’s carrying capacity.

|**Object Weight**|**Arc of Throw**|**Range Increment**|**Heft Penalty**|
|---|---|---|---|
|≤ 1/15 of base carry capacity|Up to 15 ft|20 ft|–18|
|≤ 1/10 of base carry capacity|Up to 10 ft|15 ft|–24|
|≤ 1/5 of base carry capacity|Up to 5 ft|10 ft|–30|
|≤ 1/2 of base carry capacity|None|5 ft|–36|

**Heft Penalty:** Apply this as a penalty to the Throw attack check and any checks made to maintain projectile control.

---

# Limits & Notes

- Irregularly shaped items or objects not intended for throwing may impose additional penalties (GM discretion).
    
- Throwing through tight spaces or complex flight paths imposes further penalties, especially when performing trick throws.
    
- Large or unwieldy items might require **two hands** to throw, and may require a **Major (⟐) version** of the action if precision is desired.
