Creatures are assigned a [[Level]] based on the number of [[Talent Point]]s they've earned, including NPCs. 

A creature's [[1.1 Player Characters|Level]] determines its Core Skills. Core Skill Points granted by [[Level]] can be used to increase their [[Core Skills]], but no higher than the maximum enumerated in the below table.

| Level | Total TP | Notes                                  | Total Core Skill Points \| Max Bonus |
| ----- | -------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1     | 1        | Noob commoner/literal child.           | 3 \| +2                              |
| 2     | 3        | Apprentice. Skilled commoner.          | 4 \| +3                              |
| 3     | 6        | Good starting point for an adventurer. | 5 \| +3                              |
| 4     | 10       | Experienced professional.              | 7 \| +4                              |
| 5     | 15       | Seasoned adepts.                       | 8 \| +4                              |
| 6     | 21       | The elite.                             | 9 \| +5                              |
| 7     | 28       | Regional champion.                     | 11 \| +6                             |
| 8     | 36       | National heroes / master-tier.         | 13 \| +7                             |
| 9     | 45       | World-class. Mythic mortal.            | 15 \| +7                             |
| 10    | 55       | Legendary hero.                        | 17 \| +7                             |
