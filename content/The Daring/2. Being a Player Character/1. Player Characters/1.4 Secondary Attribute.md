## <font color="#ff0000">Vigor</font>
**Vigor = [[1.2 Level|Level]] + 4 + ([[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]](min 0) × 3) + ([[Core Skills|Fort.]] × 2)** + [[Talent Bonus]].
*Vigor represents a creature's reserve of physical energy and bodily health. When a creature runs out of Vigor, they begin **[[Condition|Dying]]**.*
> Vigor is a measure of how badly you can afford to get your shit rocked. Most blows don't gruesomely connect, and you twist/endure them - until you're empty and you get struck for real.
## <font color="#00b0f0">Focus</font>
**Focus = Level + 2 + (max([[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]], [[1.3 Primary Attribute|INS]](min 1)) × 3) + ([[Core Skills|Will.]] × 2)** + [[Talent Bonus]].
*Focus represents the reserves of will, concentration, and determination. When a creature runs out of Focus they suffer a **[[Breakdown]]**.*
> Focus is your mental fortitude, determination, and so on. Tense/dangerous situations will chip away at your focus, and the majority of special abilities cost some amount of it.
## Endurance
**Dice # = [[1.2 Level|Level]] + [[Core Skills|Fort.]] + [[Core Skills|Will.]].**
A PC dice pool (d4s) used to restore energy during periods of rest. Each spent die restores (1d4 + [[Core Skills|Fort.]]) to [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] or (1d4+[[Core Skills|Will.]]) to focus.
## Passive Skill
*Passive Skill, for every skill, is calculated with **13 + [[1.3 Primary Attribute|Attribute Modifier]] + [[Skill]] + [[Subskill]]**.*  
When a Passive Skill is utilized in a contested **[[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]**, it becomes a **[[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]]** - which is subject to conditional effects. It shouldn't be that complex, just run it and try.
## Carry Capacity
*Carry Capacity is how many kilograms a creature can lift unassisted before having their locomotion impeded.*  
For each increment of **Carry Capacity** a creature is lifting unassisted, they suffer **[[Condition|Strained]] +1**.  
**Carry Capacity = [[1.3 Primary Attribute|Physique]] mod × 2**.
