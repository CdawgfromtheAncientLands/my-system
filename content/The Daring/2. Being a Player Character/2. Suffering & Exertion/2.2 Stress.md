**Stress** can be suffered whenever the GM says so based on a PC's character traits, environment, circumstances, or actions. The GM dispenses Stress as necessary across PCs (and NPCs) in accordance to the following Stressor guide:

| Level  | Description                                                         | Stress       | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mild   | More than just annoying. <br>Nerves jarred, but the spirit holds.   | 1d4 to 1d4+2 | - Beginning a threatening/tense  encounter OR being ambushed<br>- Critically failing a Skill Check<br>- Mildly disturbing imagery.<br>- A horrifying roar echoes from down the road at night.<br>- Something hidden moves in the dark nearby.<br>- Shocking violence.<br>- An embarrassing or notable failure.<br>- Spending ~10 minutes in a stuffy, rotting dungeon. |
| Harsh  | Gut-dropping. <br>Anxiety is seeded, adrenaline spikes, eyes widen. | 2d4 to 3d4   | - Witnessing an ally become [[Condition\|Dying]]<br>- Being Critically Hit<br>- Recovering from your [[Condition\|Dying]] condition<br>- Personal failure at a crucial moment<br>- You are taken with a knife to your throat.<br>- Witnessing gruesome, horrible, or incomprehensible things.                                                                          |
| Severe | Horrifying, heart-pounding.<br>Emotional and mental shredder.       | 4d4+         | - Witnessing sudden death.<br>- An ally dies.<br>- An old trauma resurfaces.<br>- You are faced with certain death (or something worse)<br>- Mass tragedy.<br>- Betrayal by a trusted ally.                                                                                                                                                                            |
# Out-of-box Stress Distribution
The wonder of the Stress system is that it allows for building tension, tradeoffs between power and safety, and the expression of social/environmental factors in a way that truly matters to a PC's effectiveness and well-being. 

The issue is that it's almost entirely GM-specific. The game WILL work well if a GM chooses not to apportion any Stress beyond enemy abilities and Stress Costs, but it works BEST when the stakes are expressed through all available avenues.
# Stress vs. Focus Costs
Paying a [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] cost for an ability is DIFFERENT from suffering Stress, and is treated as a different mechanic - i.e. "<font color="#0070c0">Suffers Stress</font>" vs. "<font color="#0070c0">Pays a Focus Cost</font>".
