# What is an <font color="#ffc000">ACTION</font>?
Actions are TWO different things - but it shouldn't be very hard to understand. 
1. An <font color="#ffc000">Action</font> is a codified rules flow that a creature may choose and use in or out of encounters. 
2. An <font color="#ffc000">Action</font> is a per-turn encounter resource (<font color="#ffc000">⟐⋄⋄, ↻</font>).
The **RESOURCE** is spent as a cost to execute the **RULES FLOW**. Actions are both the objects/rules AND the resources necessary to perform them.

*So you spend your Actions to use Actions.*
# <u>Major Actions <font color="#ffc000">⟐</font></u>
*Major Actions* are the star focus of a turn. In shorthand, they're represented by a dotted diamond (⟐). This resource is used to perform the most impactful, flexible, and effective encounter options a creature has. 

**A creature's <font color="#ffc000">⟐</font> may be exchanged for a single <font color="#ffc000">⋄</font> at any time.**
# <u>Minor Actions <font color="#ffc000">⋄</font></u>
*Minor Actions* are the accessories and peripheral maneuvers of a turn. These have poor offensive strength, but high tactical application and personal impact.
# <u>Reactions <font color="#ffc000">↻</font></u>
*Reactions* are used in response to certain triggers, and can be used on other creature's turns.
# <u>Free Actions <font color="#ffc000">✧</font></u>
*Free Actions* are similar to Reactions, but don't expend any resource to utilize.
# <u>Riders <font color="#ffc000">※</font></u>
Riders are effects that ride along with other actions, usually increasing their cost in some way. The [[1. Types of Actions|Action]] that it modifiers will usually be expressed like this:
#### Rider Action
Rider, ⋄: (Strike, ⟐)
This means that this action is a Rider that costs a [[1. Types of Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]] and rides on a [[1. Types of Actions|Major ⟐ Action]] [[Strikes|Strike]].
