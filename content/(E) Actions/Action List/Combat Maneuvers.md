# Grab (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have an open hand.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Make an [[Melee|Unarmed]] check versus a target's [[Core Skills|Reflex]], [[Athletics|Muscle]], or [[Melee|Unarmed]] [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] (pick the current highest). Counts as the same as [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]] and [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]] for the purposes of [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]].
- **Crit Success.** As Success, but target is [[Condition|Restrained]] by you instead and counts as a Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2 Stress).
- **Success.** Your target is [[Condition|Grabbed]] by you until the end of your next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]]. 
- **Crit Fail.** If you currently have your target [[Condition|Grabbed]] or [[Condition|Restrained]], they break free immediately. If not, they may [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]] you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to automatically succeed on the attempt instead of rolling for it.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you may choose the [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] to contest and add the following effect to Success (and, by extension, Crit Success):
- **Success.** You may immediately [[Movement Actions|Traverse]] up to half your [[1.5 Characteristics|Speed]] as a [[1. Types of Actions|Free ✧ Action]] afterward. 

**Moving with a Grabbed Creature.** When you travel any distance with a creature [[Condition|Grabbed]], the action that causes your movement is also a [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Forced Move}]] action. The amount of distance you can move with a [[Condition|Grabbed]] creature is dependent on their [[1.5 Characteristics|Weight]] class compared to yours:
- *Lighter Than You.* You move at full speed.
- *Same Class.* You move at HALF speed. If making a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] to do so (i.e. dragging while climbing), suffer -3 to it.
- *Up to 1 Class Larger.* You move no more than 5ft. If making a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] to do so, suffer -8 to it.
# Trip (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have an open hand.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Make an [[Melee|Unarmed]] check versus a target's [[Core Skills|Reflex]], [[Athletics|Muscle]], or [[Melee|Unarmed]] [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] (pick the current highest). Counts as the same as [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] and [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]] for the purposes of [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]. 
- **Crit Success.** As Success, but the target takes [[2.1 Damage|Blunt Damage]] as if they'd taken 5ft worth of fall damage and counts as a Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2 Stress).
- **Success.** Your target is knocked [[Condition|Prone]].
- **Crit Fail.** Your target may [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to automatically succeed on the attempt instead of rolling for it.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you may choose the [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] to contest and add the following effect to Success (and, by extension, Crit Success):
- **Success.** The target slips 5ft (10ft on a crit success) in a direction of your choice as they fall. This action gains the [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Forced Move}]] trait.
# Shove (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have an open hand.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Make an [[Melee|Unarmed]] check versus a target's [[Core Skills|Reflex]], [[Athletics|Muscle]], or [[Melee|Unarmed]] [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] (pick the current highest). Counts as the same as [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]] and [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] for the purposes of [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]. 
- **Crit Success.** As Success, but counts as a Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] and pushes an extra 5ft. 
- **Success.** You push the target up to your [[Melee Reach]] away from you. You may follow them with a [[Movement Actions|Traverse]] or [[Movement Actions|Step]] as a [[1. Types of Actions|Free ✧ Action]], but moving no further than the distance you pushed them.
- **Crit Fail.** Your target may [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to automatically succeed on the attempt instead of rolling for it.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you may choose the [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] to contest and add the following effect to Success (and, by extension, Crit Success):
- **Success.** You shove the target an additional 5ft, and may do so in any direction instead of just away.
# Disarm (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You have an open hand.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Make an **[[Melee|Unarmed]]** check versus the target’s **Reflex**, **Muscle**, or **[[Melee|Unarmed]]** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] (target’s choice).
- **Crit Success.** A held item of your choice flies from their grasp and lands in a space adjacent to them (you choose).
- **Success.** You weaken the target’s grip on a held item of your choice. 
	- Further attempts to Disarm them of the same item this turn gain a stacking +1 bonus on the check
	- They suffer a stacking -1 penalty to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made with the item and/or [[1.2 Difficulty Class|DC]]s originating from it. 
	- The weakened grip ends when the target adjusts their hold with an Interact action OR if they drop the item.
- **Crit Fail.** You are **[[Condition|Vulnerable]]** to that target until the start of your next turn.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but the range for a Crit Success is only +5 above the DC.
# Demoralize (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Auditory}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Visual}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Emotion}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Linguistic}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> The target must be able to perceive you.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> You use cutting words, threatening presence, or psychological pressure to rattle a creature within 30ft. Make an **[[Guile|Intimidation]]** check vs. their **[[Core Skills|Willpower]]** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]]. Regardless of outcome, your affected target is immune to your [[Combat Maneuvers|Demoralize]] attempts for the next 10 minutes.
- **Crit Success.** As success, but [[Condition|Panicked]] 2 and Harsh [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (2d4+2)
- **Success.** The target becomes **[[Condition|Panicked]] 1**. Counts as Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2).
- **Crit Fail.** Your jeers land flat or backfire; the target gains **+2** to their next [[Core Skills|Willpower]] check (or Will [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]]) made within the next 10 minutes.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you may choose to target all creatures of your choice within a 15ft. square (60ft range). Roll once; compare individually.
# Feint (⋄)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Melee}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> The target must be within your [[Melee Reach]].
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> You disguise a strike, exploit a blind angle, or manipulate the opponent’s expectations.  
Roll **[[Finesse|Quick Fingers]]** or **[[Guile|Deception]]** versus the target’s **[[Observation|Perception]]** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]].
- **Crit Success.** As Success, but [[Condition|Vulnerable]] 2.
- **Success.** The target becomes **[[Condition|Vulnerable]]** to **your next Strike** this round and suffers a **Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]]** (1d4+2).
- **Crit Fail.** The target may immediately perform a **Disarm** against you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to **automatically critically succeed**.
# Disable (⋄/⟐)
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must be able to touch the targeted item.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> You attempt to sabotage, dislodge, or interfere with a piece of technology or gear held or worn by a creature. Roll **Sleight of Hand** (or **Sabotage**, if you have it) versus the target’s **Reflex** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]].  
This check targets both the creature and the item simultaneously.
- **Crit Success.** As Success, and the item is heavily disabled. It requires **two full Interact (⟐)** actions to repair or re-secure before it becomes usable again.
- **Success.** You minorly disable the item; it becomes unusable until fixed with **one Interact** action.
- **Crit Fail.** The target may immediately **Grapple** you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to automatically succeed.
    

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you gain one of the following benefits

**Limits & Notes:**  
Disable suffers **+16** to the DC if the item is actively wielded. GM may apply modifiers based on item fragility, character familiarity, or environmental pressure. If the creature **Resists**, they may roll Hand-to-Hand or Labor instead of Reflex.

# Steal (from a Creature) (⋄/⟐)

[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]], [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Interact}]]

<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> At least one free hand. You must be within reach of the item you want to steal and be at least **Concealed** from the target.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Roll **Sleight of Hand** versus the target’s **Perception** [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]].

- **Crit Success.** You take the item cleanly and the target does not notice.
    
- **Success.** You take the item, but the target notices after **Xd4 seconds**, where **X** is your **Degrees of Success**.
    
- **Fail.** You fail to steal the item and the target notices.
    
- **Crit Fail.** As Fail, and the target may attempt to **Grapple** you as a [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]].
    

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, and you choose one:

- You may Steal an item that is **actively worn or strapped** to the target (but not held in hand).
    
- You may Steal without requiring Concealment (DC increases by GM discretion, usually +10–15).
    

**Limits & Notes:**  
If you attempt to steal an item that is worn, use Disarm rules as a baseline.  
Steal checks made while **Concealed** suffer **+40%** to their DC unless the PC is **Hidden**.  
The check always suffers **+X%**, where X = kg weight of the item.
