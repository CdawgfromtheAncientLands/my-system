PCs are the stars of the show. They have special abilities and considerations that NPCs do not.

PCs are defined by their [[Attributes]], [[Talents]], and [[Skills]]. 

PCs gain [[Experience]] through accumulating [[Victories]] awarded by the GM. 

For every 4 [[Experience]] a PC earns, they earn 1 [[Talent Point]].

Creatures are assigned a [[Level]] based on the number of [[Talent Point]]s they've earned, including NPCs.
