As [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]]s adventure, they will accumulate [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Triumph]]s, which convert into [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Experience]] during Full Rests. For every 4 [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Experience]] a PC gains, they earn 1 [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Talent Point]].

Creatures are assigned a [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Level]] based on the total number of [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Talent Points]] they've earned, including NPCs.

A creature's [[1.1 Player Characters|Level]] is the sole influence on its [[Core Skills]]. Core Skill Points granted by [[1.2 Levels & Progression|Level]] can be used to increase their [[Core Skills]], but no higher than the maximum enumerated in the below table.

| 2     | 3        | Apprentice. Skilled commoner.          | 4 \| +3                              |
| 3     | 6        | Good starting point for an adventurer. | 5 \| +3                              |
| 4     | 10       | Experienced professional.              | 7 \| +4                              |
| 5     | 15       | Seasoned adepts.                       | 8 \| +4                              |
| 6     | 21       | The elite.                             | 9 \| +5                              |
| 7     | 28       | Regional champion.                     | 11 \| +6                             |

| 9     | 45       | World-class. Mythic mortal.            | 15 \| +7                             |
