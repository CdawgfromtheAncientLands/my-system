# Injury Tracks
Each [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury]] sticks on a character and adds a tick to one (or more) [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury Track]]s. As ticks are added to a track, they compound toward a grand total of 3 - which, if they ever move past that, transforms into a permanent [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Disability]].

# Scars 
[[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Scar]]s are <u>permanent attribute penalties</u> that take effect if an injury taken at [[Condition|Peril]] 1 or more passes its full healing period without being the target of an [[1.3 Medical Activities|Operate]]. 

A [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Scar]] is a permanent -1 to the attributes at risk, lingering after the injury itself has healed.
# Disability 
These are lasting effects that are significantly worse than [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Scar]]s, occurring when the same [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury Track]] is ticked 3 times. Permanent injuries occur - like a lame or severed limb, blindness, etcetera.

This is up to the GM to decide effects. A [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Disability]] is usually severe enough to retire a [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]] from adventuring entirely, unless they are particularly strong, brave, or suicidal.

A [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Disability]] does not trigger on injuries made at [[3. Breaking & Dying|Peril]] 0.
# Trauma 
Trauma is a permanent shift in how a character operates based on extremely impactful negative experiences. A character will gain a [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Trauma]] when they suffer a [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]] at peril 3 or higher.

Characters suffering a [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Trauma]] MUST replace their [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] or [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Soul]] tags (one of their choice) with another. The GM may decide to limit their choice to a select few, and this does not need to be immediately decided when the Trauma is assigned. This does not change any traits or talents of the character.

After a Traumatic [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]], the player MUST revise or replace up to one existing trait to better reflect the character’s new outlook. The trait must be negative - ex. "Hatred/fear of reptiles", "People = tools", so on.
