**Breaking & Dying** are two methods of possibly permanent and crippling harm triggered when hitting mental and physical limits. They are tied together by the **Peril** condition:
#### <u>Peril X</u>
*You are further unraveled, mentally and physically.*
- When you become **Dying**, increase your **Dying** value by **X**.

The severity of a **Breakdown** or a bout of **Dying** is determined by a PC's **Peril** level when the effect is triggered.
# <font color="#c00000">Dying</font>
*When a creature's Body reaches its limit, they begin Dying.* 
*Dying* is an X condition ranging from 1 to 4.
#### <u>Dying X</u>
*You are fading away.*
- You are Unconscious.
- At the end of your [[Turn]] (or, every 10 seconds), roll a [[Death Save]] (Flat Check vs. DC 12+X).
- If X becomes 4, you die. When you stop Dying, suffer Peril+1.

Whenever a player begins [[Condition|Dying]] after hitting 0 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]], they suffer an Injury. Injuries require time to heal, rolled when they are applied and based upon your current [[Condition|Peril]] condition.
#### Injury Tracks
Each [[Injury]] sticks on a character and adds a tick to one (or more) [[Injury Track]]s. As ticks are added to a track, they compound toward a grant total of 3 - which, if they ever move past that, transforms into a permanent [[Disability]]. 
#### Scar Effects 
[[Scar]]s are <u>permanent attribute penalties</u> that take effect if an injury taken at [[Condition|Peril]] 1 or more passes its full healing period without being the target of an [[Operation]]. 
## Injury Process
1. A [[PC]] hits 0 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Body]] and becomes [[Condition|Dying]]
2. The GM checks the current [[Condition|Peril]] score and assigns the proper [[Injury]] from the below table.
3. The injury's [[Heal Period]] is rolled and marked down
4. The affected [[Injury Track]]s are ticked.
5. The GM decides, narratively, what the injury is depending on its cause and ticked tracks.
6. The injury is fully logged on the [[PC]]'s [[Injury Tracker]].

## Injury Effects & Heal Periods per Peril Rank

| Peril | Injury Effect                                                                                         | Heal Period |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 0     | 50% chance of rolling injury table                                                                    | 1d4+1 days  |
| 1     | The injury ticks a randomly-assigned [[Injury Track]].                                                | 3d3+3 days  |
| 2     | The injury ticks two randomly-assigned [[Injury Track]]s. The first ticked track is at [[Scar Risk]]. | 4d6+2 days  |

# <font color="#0070c0">Breakdown</font>
*When a player's Focus hits 0, they:*
- Suffer a Breakdown based on their current [[Condition|Peril]] level (check table below).
- Suffer [[Condition|Peril]]+1.

| Peril | Breakdown Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0     | <font color="#ffc000">You shudder and briefly snap.</font><br>> Suffer [[Condition\|Panicked]] +1 <br>> Suffer [[Condition\|Stunned]] +1                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 1     | <font color="#de7802">For a harsh several seconds, you are truly out of your mind.</font><br>> Suffer [[Condition\|Panicked]] +2<br>> You become [[Condition\|Stupefied]] 1 for the next 1d10 minutes.<br>> Your next turn must be spent reacting unreasonably (roll) to the current situation.                               |
| 2     | <font color="#c00000">You can't take it.</font><br>> Suffer [[Condition\|Panicked]] +3. You can't reduce your [[Condition\|Panicked]] condition below 1 for the next hour.<br>> You are [[Condition\|Stupefied]] 2 for the next hour.<br>> Spend the next 1d10 minutes reacting unreasonably (roll) to the current situation. |
| 3+    | <font color="#ff0000">Traumatic Break!</font> Suffer a permanent [[Trauma]], and become totally useless for the next 1d10x10 minutes, pursuing an unreasonable course of action (roll) in response to the current situation.                                                                                                  |
