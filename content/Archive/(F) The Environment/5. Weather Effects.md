# Wind
Wind affects ease of movement, turbulence on [[2. Liquids|Liquid]] surfaces, [[Shooting]] [[Strikes|Strikes]], [[4. Ambient Effects|Noise Level]], and the use of [[1.4 Characteristics|Fly Speed]]. When **Wind** is present in a scene, its **direction is declared once** and typically remains constant for the duration of that scene. Wind direction only changes with **significant time passage** or **high-intensity wind conditions** (such as Storms or Hurricanes), at GM discretion.
## **Breeze**
_The wind is present but not threatening._
- No mechanical effect.
- Light objects flutter; cloaks and loose fabric stir.
- May be used for drama, atmosphere, or to gently motivate smoke or gas clouds.
## **Gale**
_Strong, steady wind. The air resists effort and precision._
- The area is **[[4. Ambient Effects|Noisy]]**.
- **–2** to **[[Shooting]] [[Strikes]]**  
    _(no penalty for high-velocity weapons such as firearms or lasers)._
- Flying **against or across** the wind treats the air as **[[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]**.
- Light unsecured objects are displaced.
- Smoke or gas moves **10 ft** in the wind’s direction at the end of each round.
# **Storm**
_Wind becomes chaotic and hazardous. Positioning is no longer reliable._
- The area is **[[4. Ambient Effects|Loud]]**.
- Movement in any direction **against or across the wind** counts as **[[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]**. Movement with the wind is increased by 5ft for every 10ft traveled.
- **–4** to **[[Shooting]] [[Strikes]]** made not in the wind's direction, +15ft range to those that are.
    _(–2 for high-velocity weapons such as firearms or lasers)._
- During any [[1. Types of Actions|Action]] that involves **leaving the ground** or performing a **[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] action** midair, the creature must make a DC 15 [[Athletics|Muscle]] check or be moved 1d3x5ft in the wind's direction during the action.
- [[1.4 Characteristics|Medium]] or lighter creatures appear unstable; debris, hair, and loose gear thrash around.

# **Hurricane**
_Violent, deafening stormwinds capable of stripping control entirely._
- The area is **[[4. Ambient Effects|Deafening]]**.
- **–6** to all **Ranged Strikes** not in the wind's direction, +30ft range to those that are.
- Movement in any direction **against or across the wind** counts as **[[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]**, movement WITH the wind is doubled.
- During any [[1. Types of Actions|Action]] that involves **leaving the ground** or performing a **[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] action** midair, the creature must make a DC 18 [[Athletics|Muscle]] check or be moved 1d6x5ft in the wind's direction during the action.
- **[[1.4 Characteristics|Medium]] or lighter** creatures making [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] actions on the ground must make a DC 16 [[Athletics|Muscle]] check or be moved 1d3x5ft in the wind's direction during the action.

# Precipitation
## Rainfall

| **Level**    | **Rainfall Effects**                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Drizzle**  | Slight temperature drop. Mildly relaxing.                                                                                                                           |
| **Shower**   | Decrease your **[[Detection Rating]]** of targets **40ft or farther** from you by **1**.                                                                            |
| **Downpour** | The area becomes **[[4. Ambient Effects\|Noisy]]**. <br>Decrease your [[Detection Rating]] of targets **30 ft or farther** by **1**.                                |
| **Torrent**  | The area is [[4. Ambient Effects\|Noisy]]. <br>Decrease your [[Detection Rating]] of any non-adjacent targets by 1, and targets 20ft or further by an additional 1. |
| **Deluge**   | The area is [[4. Ambient Effects\|Loud]].<br>Decrease your [[Detection Rating]] of any non-adjacent targets by 2.                                                   |
Rain always stacks with existing noise and visibility effects unless explicitly noted. Rainfall itself can decrease ambient [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] slightly, but being soaked by it (or paired with harsh winds) can decrease the ambient [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] by 1 or more levels.
## Snowfall Levels

| **Level**    | **Snowfall Effects**                                                                                                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Light**    | As [[5. Weather Effects\|Shower]], but with snow instead - and no noise.                                                                    |
| **Moderate** | As a [[5. Weather Effects\|Downpour]], but with snow instead - and no noise.                                                                |
| **Heavy**    | As [[5. Weather Effects\|Torrent]], but with snow instead - and no noise. Some parts of the ground may be [[1. Terrain\|Slippery Terrain]]. |
| **Whiteout** | As [[5. Weather Effects\|Deluge]], but all terrain is [[1. Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] and no noise.                                       |
Snow tends to be quieter, but far more visually disruptive than, rain. Snow is commonly paired with [[4. Ambient Effects|Cold]] or worse temperatures. Those soaked by it (or buffeted with harsh winds) can decrease their ambient [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] by 1 or more levels.

# Hail
An active hailstorm deals [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]] to all creatures in it at the end of their [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]]s.
- [[1.4 Characteristics|Size]] 1/2 or smaller creatures take half damage (rounded down). 
- [[1.4 Characteristics|Size]] 1 or bigger creatures take damage from hail a number of times equal to their size score.

| **Level**    | Damage |
| ------------ | ------ |
| **Light**    | 1      |
| **Moderate** | 1d4    |
| **Severe**   | 1d4+2  |
| **Deadly**   | 1d6+3  |
Some shelter between yourself and the hail can halve (partial, like a tree or loose net) or fully mitigate (a medium or larger raised shield, a roof) damage taken.

# Big Things & Small Weather
Large creatures or structures (trolls, dragons, houses, vehicles) may be:
- unaffected by certain weather effects
- less affected by obscuring precipitation
- more affected by large hailstones

Apply common sense relative to scale and target profile.
# Relativity of Conditions / Effects
Think of weather as _force multipliers_. Depending on creature size, trait, or physiology, weather might:
- dampen hearing
- reduce scent
- obscure vision differently
- make one creature easy to see and another nearly invisible

A giant is easy to spot in a downpour; a mouse is impossible. When in doubt, nudge effects toward clarity and tension rather than strict simulation.

Exceptional humidity may increase the effective ambient temperature, make [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Fire Damage]] weak or ineffective, or induce fatigue.

Some things like thin or poor-quality air and exceptional dryness may make an environment [[8. Environmental Mood|Oppressive]] or [[8. Environmental Mood|Dreadful]].
