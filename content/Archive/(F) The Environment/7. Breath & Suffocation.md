When a creature cannot breathe, it begins consuming its remaining air. Once that air is gone, suffocation sets in - and things escalate fast.

## Air Supply
Creatures can hold a limited amount of air.
> **Minutes of Air = [[2. Attributes & C|PHY]] Modifier + ([[Athletics|Swim]] skill × 2)**

- This represents controlled breath, lung capacity, and physical conditioning.
- Creatures without lungs or that do not breathe are immune.
- Amphibious or specially adapted creatures may increase this value at the GM’s discretion.

---
## Losing Air
A creature loses air whenever it cannot maintain a controlled breath.

A creature immediately loses **1d3 minutes of air** when any of the following occur:
- It **takes damage** while unable to breathe
- It **speaks, shouts, gasps, or opens its mouth** intentionally
- It performs an action that requires forceful exhalation (spells, breath weapons, exertion)
## Suffocating
When a creature’s remaining air reaches **0 minutes**, it begins **Suffocating**
A suffocating creature immediately begins **Dying**.
- The creature cannot stabilize, recover, or benefit from death-prevention effects while it remains unable to breathe.
- Any effect that would normally stop or delay dying **fails** until the creature has access to air again.

Once the creature can breathe, suffocation ends—but dying consequences remain.

---
## Regaining Air

A creature that can breathe freely regains air at a rate of:
- **1 minute of air per uninterrupted minute** of normal breathing

Gasping, combat, or continued stress may delay recovery.
