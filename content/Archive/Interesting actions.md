# Disable (⋄/⟐)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Wrestle}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> You must be able to touch the targeted item.
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> You attempt to sabotage, dislodge, or interfere with a piece of technology or gear held or worn by a creature. Roll **[[Finesse|Quick Fingers]]** or [[Finesse|Tinkering]] versus the target’s **[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]]** [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]]. This check targets both the creature and the item simultaneously.
- **Crit Success.** As Success, and the item is heavily disabled. It requires **two full [[Misc Actions|Interact]] (⟐)** actions to repair or re-secure before it becomes usable again.
- **Success.** You minorly disable the item; it becomes unusable until fixed with **one Interact** action.
- **Crit Fail.** The target may immediately **Grapple** you as a [[1.4 Actions|Reaction ↻]] and may choose to automatically succeed.
    
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, but you gain one of the following benefits

**Limits & Notes:**  
Disable suffers **+3** to the DC if the item is actively wielded. GM may apply modifiers based on item fragility, character familiarity, or environmental pressure. If the creature **Resists**, they may roll Hand-to-Hand or Labor instead of Reflex.

# Steal (from a Creature) (⋄/⟐)

[[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Mental}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Interact}]]

<font color="#d83931">Requirements:</font> At least one free hand. You must be within reach of the item you want to steal and be at least **Concealed** from the target.

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄):</font> Roll **Sleight of Hand** versus the target’s **Perception** [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]].

- **Crit Success.** You take the item cleanly and the target does not notice.
    
- **Success.** You take the item, but the target notices after **Xd4 seconds**, where **X** is your **Degrees of Success**.
    
- **Fail.** You fail to steal the item and the target notices.
    
- **Crit Fail.** As Fail, and the target may attempt to **Grapple** you as a [[1.4 Actions|Reaction ↻]].
    

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐):</font> Same as ⋄, and you choose one:

- You may Steal an item that is **actively worn or strapped** to the target (but not held in hand).
    
- You may Steal without requiring Concealment (DC increases by GM discretion, usually +10–15).
    

**Limits & Notes:**  
If you attempt to steal an item that is worn, use Disarm rules as a baseline.  
Steal checks made while **Concealed** suffer **+40%** to their DC unless the PC is **Hidden**.  
The check always suffers **+X%**, where X = kg weight of the item.
