**Breaking & Dying** are two methods of possibly permanent and crippling harm triggered when hitting mental and physical limits. They are tied together by the **Peril** condition:
#### <u>Peril X</u>
*You are further unraveled, mentally and physically.*
- When you become **Dying**, increase your **Dying** value by **X**.

The severity of a **Breakdown** or a bout of **Dying** is determined by a PC's **Peril** level when the effect is triggered.
# <font color="#c00000">Dying</font>
*When a creature's Body reaches its limit, they begin Dying.* 
*Dying* is an X condition ranging from 1 to 4.
### <u>Dying X</u>
*You are fading away.*
- You are Unconscious.
- At the end of your [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] (or, every 10 seconds), roll a [[Flat Check|Death Save]] (Flat Check vs. DC 12+X).
- If X becomes 4, you die. When you stop Dying, suffer Peril+1.

Whenever a player begins [[Condition|Dying]] after hitting 0 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]], they suffer an Injury. Injuries require time to heal, rolled when they are applied and based upon your current [[Condition|Peril]] condition.
### Injury Process
1. A [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]] hits 0 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Body]] and becomes [[Condition|Dying]]
2. The GM checks the current [[Condition|Peril]] score and assigns the proper [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury]] from the below table.
3. The injury's [[3. Breaking & Dying|Heal Period]] is rolled and marked down
4. The affected [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury Track]]s are ticked.
5. The GM decides, narratively, what the injury is depending on its cause and ticked tracks.
6. The injury is fully logged on the [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]]'s [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Injury Tracker]].

#### Injury Effects & Heal Periods per Peril Rank

| Peril | Injury Effect                                                                                                                                                                               | Heal Period |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 0     | 50% chance of rolling injury table                                                                                                                                                          | 1d4+1 days  |
| 1     | The injury ticks a randomly-assigned [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such\|Injury Track]].                                                                                                | 3d3+3 days  |
| 2+    | The injury ticks two randomly-assigned [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such\|Injury Track]]s. Only the first ticked track is at risk of a [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such\|Scar]]. | 4d6+2 days  |
# <font color="#0070c0">Breakdown</font>
*When a player's Focus hits 0, they:*
- Suffer a Breakdown based on their current [[Condition|Peril]] level (check table below).
- Suffer [[Condition|Peril]]+1.
- Their [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] takes over, heavily influencing what the character does.

During and for some time after a [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]], a character is stuck in a numb state, preventing cascading [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]]s.
- Their [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] is locked at 0. 
- They cannot [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]].
