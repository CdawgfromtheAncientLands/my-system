# <font color="#ffff00">Triumphs</font>
<font color="#ffff00">Triumphs</font> are awarded to each member party after accomplishing objectives. The most basic example of this is the completion of a challenging combat encounter, worth 1 <font color="#ffff00">Triumph</font>. 

When a [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]] is rewarded a [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Triumph]], they may refresh the per-session uses of ONE of their [[Single Identity Talents]] or [[Multi-Identity Talents]].

Triumphs convert into Experience during Long Rests, which is spent on increasing [[1. Skill|Skill Score]]s.

# <font color="#de7802">Daring</font>
[[1.1 Player Characters|Player Character]]s are [[Act of Will]]. They are people (or creatures) full of flavor and motivation, embarking on an adventure - regardless of how hard it pushes them, prepared to climb the highest mountain and delve the deepest pits.

At the beginning of a PCs turn in an encounter, they gain [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Daring]] equal to the amount of [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Triumph]]s they have banked.

A GM may choose to award [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Daring]] during an encounter if [[1.1 Player Characters|PCs]] push into uncertainty, take risks, or seize momentum creatively - regardless of how successful their attempts are. Climactic combats should be generous with this.
### Inspired Daring
When a player is bold, inventive, or sharply engaged with the story, the GM can award **Inspired Daring** (or [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Inspiration]]) - a steadier, banked form of nerve that persists beyond any single moment of crisis. 
A PC can only have 2 points of [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Inspired Daring]] at a time, and they reset when the GM says so - end-of-session, end-of-arc, next long rest, whatever - depending on the importance of the situation they were gained in.
