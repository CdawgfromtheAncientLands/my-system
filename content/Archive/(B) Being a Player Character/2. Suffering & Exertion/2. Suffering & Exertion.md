[[1.1 Player Characters|PC]]s will go through tough things. As they do, they'll suffer [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]] and [[2.2 Stress|Stress]]. 

These things drain a creature's [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] and [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] respectively. 

Note that some [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]] types ([[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Spirit Damage]], [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Psychic Damage]]) drain [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] and not [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
