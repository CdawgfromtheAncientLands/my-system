# **Never Give Up**

You refuse to accept a dead end until you’ve torn it apart and checked the wiring twice. Even when reality slams the door, you linger, thinking, improvising, scavenging for leverage. When people around you sink into despair, it grates you. You want them upright, trying, _doing_. You’ll take high risks to pry open possibilities, but you know the difference between “unlikely” and “insane.” When you truly must give up, it sits in your gut like rot.

_People may call you unshakeable, naïve, irritating, or inspiring._

• _“If the door’s shut, find a window.”_  
• _“There must be something we can do!”_  
• _“Let’s salvage what we can and work from there.”_

# **Kill the Past**

Old wounds demand resolution. Loose ends itch until you tear them clean. You move toward the things other people bury - grudges, debts, mistakes, traumas - because leaving them unaddressed feels worse than any confrontation. You do this for yourself and for others, insisting that closure is the only honest way forward. When digging up the past threatens your mission or your life, you still push, driven by the need to end what was left open.

_People may call you obsessive, pushy, principled, or brave._

• _“Why let it sit? It'll just eat at you for as long as you live.”_  
• _“I can't bear to leave this alone. Please, just let me ask you one more time...”_  
• _“Time doesn't heal **all** wounds...”_

# **A Life Without Purpose Is Wasted**

You need direction like lungs need air. A goal - any _real_ goal - keeps your spine straight and your days coherent. Wandering feels like decay, and watching others drift or stagnate upsets you enough to intervene (or at least judge). When your purpose clashes with comfort or safety, the tension grinds until something breaks. 

_People may call you focused, intense, obsessive, or admirable._

• _“Tell me the goal. Then I’ll know what to do.”_  
• _“Drifting isn’t living.”_  
• _“Make meaning. Don’t wait for it.”_
