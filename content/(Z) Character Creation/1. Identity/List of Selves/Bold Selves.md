# **Hard & Fast**

You prefer to confront things head-on - openly or not. You refuse to let issues linger or stand in your way. You’ve learned that detours breed doubt, and hesitation gives difficulty a foothold. When something needs to be done, there's no pussyfooting.

_People may call you forceful, blunt, decisive, relentless, or impatient._  

• *"If it’s in the way, move it. If it won’t move, break it."*
• "*Don’t overthink it - start moving, and think while you run."*
• _“Do something to help or get out of my way!”_

When you choose to be **Hard & Fast**, answer these questions for your GM:
- What core lesson or experience taught you that hesitation is dangerous—or shameful—and how has it shaped the kind of obstacles you refuse to step around?
- When the juggernaut approach risks breaking something you care about—an ally, a secret, a fragile peace—what makes you push anyway, and what’s the line that finally stops you?

# **Opportunity In Fear**

You treat anxiety and dread as goalposts - between them is work that must be done. Where others break and falter is where the most important stakes lie for you. When others freeze or over-explain their own nerves, you’ve already adjusted your stance and taken the step forward.

_People may call you composed, unnerving, courageous, or unhinged._  

• _“The shivers tell me I'm still awake - lets me know I'm still listening.”_  
• _“Nobody's been great without being at least a little uncomfortable.”_  
• _“The only way past that fear is through its teeth.”_

# **Ice Breaker, Moment Taker**

You refuse to let momentum die on the vine. When situations stall, when people hesitate, when tension crystallizes into a breathless pause... you strike. Stillness and gawking are the enemies of progress - when the world won't move on its own, **that's** the chance to roll it into your court.

_People may call you disruptive, decisive, relentless, or dangerously proactive._

• _“MY time!!!”_  
• _“Well, if nobody’s doing anything about this, I will!”_  
• _“Quit waiting for the perfect moment - it's now.”_
