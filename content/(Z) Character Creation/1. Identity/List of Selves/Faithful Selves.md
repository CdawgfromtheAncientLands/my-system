# **No Such Thing as Coincidence**

You treat coincidences, symbols, timing, and strange alignments as signals - not curiosities. When meaning flickers at the edge of the moment, you pivot. You revise plans, reorient direction, or take sudden risks because ignoring a sign feels like inviting the universe to close the door. When logic contradicts a sign, logic bends. When danger aligns with it, danger only confirms the pull.

_People may call you intuitive, superstitious, insightful, or unnerving._

• _“No... no, that can't be it.”_  
• _“Hold on... what did you just say?”_  
• _“If the wind's that way, there must be something.”_

# **Destiny Favors Those Who Seize It**

You don’t wait for destiny - you chase it. When a moment feels right, important, or charged, you act before hesitation can sour the opportunity. Clarity, fear, intuition, excitement; any of these can mark a crossroads. You choose boldly, confident that destiny trails the ones who step forward first. When a move fails, you reinterpret and push harder. When doubt rises, you feel the pull of the next possible path.

_People may call you driven, intense, delusional, or unstoppable._

• _“Destiny waits for no man - so hurry up!”_  
• _“Destiny follows those who move.”_  
• _“Our action IS the meaning”_

# **Something Bigger Is Watching**

You behave as though unseen eyes follow your steps... not with paranoia, but with purpose. A force, a prophecy, your ancestors, fate itself - whatever the shape, you act like your choices are observed and weighed. You avoid actions that would disappoint that presence. You rise to challenges because someone - or something - expects more of you.

_People may call you principled, intense, dramatic, or unsettling._

• _“Come on... act like you're worth something.”_  
• _“This is only a test!”_  
• _“This counts. Don't embarrass yourself now.”_
