> Primary Attributes are centered on the ability of the average human – which is 8 to 12 (-1 to +2). Primary Attributes serve as elements in Secondary Attribute calculation as well as modifiers to be added to Skill Checks.

| Attribute             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Physique (PHY)**    | *Physique is a primary attribute representing a creature’s health, quality of build, immune strength, fast/slow muscle power, and overall physical might.*                                    |
| **Agility (AGI)**     | *Agility is a creature’s overall ability to quickly and accurately manipulate their own body – like balance, hand-eye coordination, manual dexterity, and speed.*                             |
| **Instinct (INS)**    | *Instinct is a creature’s self-understanding, introspective ability, strength-of-instinct, ability to quickly and fully internalize sensory input, and a bunch of other crap.*                |
| **Intelligence (IQ)** | *Intelligence is a creature’s ability to learn and retain information, draw conclusions, and apply knowledge to the real world. It grants the most starting Experience out of any attribute.* |
| **Charisma (CHA)**    | *Charisma is a creature’s ability to elicit emotions in, command the attention of, and overall manipulate others. It’s not used for any Secondary Attributes. But it can be very powerful…*   |

 When you make a [[1.1 Skill Checks]], add that Skill’s **Linked Attribute** as a modifier to your proficiency/ranks and any situational bonuses.

Any temporary changes to an attribute score immediately change its modifier and thus affect linked Skills and any Secondary stats that reference that attribute.
