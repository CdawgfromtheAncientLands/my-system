Guile is influence through deception, pressure, exaggeration, and misdirection. Where Diplomacy works with goodwill, Guile works in the shadows - through subtle manipulation, intimidation, or the art of crafting false impressions.
### **Deception** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|CHA]]
Lying, misdirection, and framing false impressions.
- Forged identities
- Bluffing during negotiations
- Distracting opponents
### **Intimidation** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|CHA]]
Applying fear, threat, or dominance.
- Menacing gestures
- Forcing compliance
- Breaking morale
### **Theatrics** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|CHA]]
Dramatic presentation, manipulation of emotions or drawing of attention.
- Feints, dramatic reveals
- Performance under pressure
- Selling a story or persona
### **Disguise** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|CHA]]/[[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Concealing identity or appearing as someone else.
- Costuming and makeup
- Adopting behaviors, accents
- Creating false appearances
