Lore measures your understanding of cultures, histories, beliefs, and the shared myths of the world. It is accumulated knowledge - recorded, remembered, and passed down - and the ability to contextualize events through what is known.
### **History** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Past events, timelines, and legacies.
- Ancient wars, dynasties
- Significant figures and relics
### **Culture** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Norms, practices, relationships between societies.
- Customs, taboos
- Political interactions
### **Religion** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Faiths, pantheons, rituals, and dogma.
- Sacred rites
- Religious history
### **Mythos** [[1.3 Primary Attribute|IQ]]
Legends, supernatural lore, ancient mysteries.
- Folklore, curses
- Eldritch or esoteric traditions
