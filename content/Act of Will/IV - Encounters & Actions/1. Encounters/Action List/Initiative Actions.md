# Delay Turn (↻/⋄)
Trigger. It’s the beginning of your turn and you've gained your Actions.

Effect. Your turn is skipped. At the end of any of the following turns, you may choose to “jump in”, beginning your skipped turn directly afterward and permanently moving your position in [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]] to that turn.
# Prepare Action (⋄)
Effect. Declare a singular Action. Spend all required resources for it (after spending the required [[1.4 Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]] for this action), but do not execute it. Instead, designate a trigger of some kind – the declared Action becomes a [[1.4 Actions|Reaction ↻]] until the beginning of your next turn, with your designated trigger as its [[Trigger]].
