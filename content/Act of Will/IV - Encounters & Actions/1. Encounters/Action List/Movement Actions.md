# Traverse (⋄/⟐)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄). </font>You walk/jog/run, swim, climb, fly, or burrow up to your respective [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]]. 

<font color="#d83931">Effect (⟐). </font>Same as ⋄, except:
- You may freely swap between multiple movement modes during the traversal.
- Move an additional amount of feet equal to half your Speed.
- During this traversal, you may Leap or Tumble Through once as a [[1.4 Actions|Free ✧ Action]].

<font color="#c00000">Balancing. </font>When moving across narrow, unstable, or treacherous surfaces, the GM may call for an [[Finesse|Acrobatics]] check vs. a DC. Resolve the check using the following results before beginning the [[Movement Actions|Traverse]]. 
- **Crit Success.** Move unimpeded.
- **Success.** Move up to half Speed.
- **Fail.** You move 5ft.   
- **Crit Fail.** Fall [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]].
# Climb (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements. </font>You have at least one [[Open Hand]].
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄). </font>If you have a [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Climb Speed]], you [[Movement Actions|Traverse]] using it. If not, make a [[Athletics|Labor]] check. If attempting with only 1 free hand, suffer -4 to the check.
- **Crit Success.** Same as Success, but climb up to your full [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Land Speed]]. 
- **Success.** You climb up to half your [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Land Speed]] across/up/down a steep surface.
- **Fail.** You fail to move effectively and must remain still to avoid falling.
- **Crit Fail.** You begin freefalling.

<font color="#c00000">Notes. </font>
The DC to climb up a sharp hill is ~15, a rocky outcropping is ~18, and it increases from there. 
# Swim (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements. </font>You have at least one [[Open Hand]].
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄). </font>If you have a [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Swim Speed]], you [[Movement Actions|Traverse]] using it. If not, make a [[Athletics|Swim]] check. If attempting with only 1 free hand, suffer -4 to the check. If you are untrained in [[Athletics|Swim]], decrease your level of success by 1. 
- **Crit Success.** Same as Success, but swim up to your full [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Land Speed]]. 
- **Success.** You swim up to half your [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Land Speed]].
- **Fail.** You swim 5ft, and may suffer the whims of the current.
- **Crit Fail.** You are helpless in the water.

<font color="#c00000">Notes. </font>The DC to swim through a calm pond is ~11, a slow river is ~14, and a raging river is ~21.
# Step (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements. </font>Your current relevant [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]] is at least 10ft.
<font color="#d83931">Effect. </font>You carefully walk, burrow, fly, or swim 5ft. For the purposes of triggering [[1.4 Actions|Reaction ↻]]s, you do not count as having entered/left an area or performed a [[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] action.
# Change Stance (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Effect. </font>You move from your current stance to a new one. Some examples of this are: going [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]], standing up, crane stance, mounting/dismounting a vehicle or animal, or any special ability granted to the Change Stance action by a Talent you have.
# Crawl (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Requirements. </font>You are [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]].
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄). </font> You crawl up to 5ft per 20ft of [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Land Speed]] (rounding down), remaining [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]] while doing so.
# Leap (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]
<font color="#d83931">Effect (⋄). </font>You jump over a horizontal or vertical length equal to the length of your occupied space (i.e. a medium-sized creature would cross a 5ft gap). You may attempt a DC 14 [[Athletics|Run/Jump]] check to improve the distance covered:
- **Crit Success.** Double the leapt distance.
- **Success.** Increase the distance leapt by 2ft (kind of useless unless you want to jump 7ft up).
- **Crit Fail.** Fall flat on your face. The GM decides the point in your leap at which you fail.
# Tumble Through (⋄)
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{R.A.P.}]]
<font color="#d83931">Effect.</font> You [[Movement Actions|Traverse]] as a [[1.4 Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]]. During the traversal, you may make an [[Finesse|Acrobatics]] check vs. the [[Core Skills|Reflex]], [[Athletics|Labor]], or [[Melee|Hand-to-Hand]] [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]] (pick the current highest) of a creature blocking your path (their choice) OR vs. a set DC if facing an obstacle that is not a hostile creature.
- **Crit Success.** Same as Success, but without the movement penalty.
- **Success.** Move through the obstacle's space as if it were [[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]].
- **Fail.** Your movement ends in front of the obstacle.
- **Crit Fail.** You fall flat on your face in front of the creature.
