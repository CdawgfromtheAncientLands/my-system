The most common type of counter is the Skirmish, or Combat Encounter. Like all encounters, it begins with Initiative. 
### Creature Turns
At the beginning of  creature's turn, they get a [[1.4 Actions|Major Action]] (⟐) and two [[1.4 Actions|Minor Action]]s (⋄), for a total of ⟐⋄⋄. A creature may spend these as they like during their turn.
