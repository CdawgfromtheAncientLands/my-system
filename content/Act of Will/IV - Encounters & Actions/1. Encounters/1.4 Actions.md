# What is an ACTION?
Actions are TWO different things - but it shouldn't be very hard to understand. 
1. An Action is a codified rules flow that a creature may choose and use in or out of encounters. 
2. An Action is a per-turn encounter resource (⟐⋄⋄, ↻).
The **RESOURCE** is spent as a cost to execute the **RULES FLOW**. Actions are both the objects/rules AND the resources necessary to perform them.

*So you spend your Actions to use Actions.*
# <u>Major Actions ⟐</u>
*Major Actions* are the star focus of a turn. In shorthand, they're represented by a dotted diamond (⟐). This resource is used to perform the most impactful, flexible, and effective encounter options a creature has. 

**A creature's ⟐ may be exchanged for a ⋄ at any time.**
# <u>Minor Actions ⋄</u>
*Minor Actions* are the accessories and peripheral maneuvers of a turn. These have poor offensive strength, but high tactical application and personal impact.
# <u>Reactions ↻</u>
*Reactions* are used in response to certain triggers, and can be used on other creature's turns.
# <u>Free Actions ◌</u>
*Free Actions* are similar to Reactions, but don't expend any resource to utilize.
# <u>Riders ※</u>
Riders are effects that ride along with other actions, usually increasing their cost in some way.
