#### <u>Grabbed</u>
*You are caught by something and held in place.*
- You are **Immobile**.
- You must succeed on a **DC 3 [[1.4 Flat Check|Flat Check]]** to perform any **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]]** action.
#### <u>Immobile</u>
*You have your locomotion (walking, swimming, etc.) totally impeded.*
- You are **[[2.8 External Conditions|Vulnerable]]**.
- You cannot use any actions with the **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]** trait.
#### <u>Paralyzed</u>
*You’re a mind in a locked suit of flesh, willing but unable to move.*
- You are **Immobile**.
- You cannot perform [[1.4 Actions|Action]]s that require any physical movement (even tiny ones).
- You are **Vulnerable 2**.
#### <u>Restrained</u>
*You struggle to meaningfully move against your bindings.*
- You are **Immobile**.
- You cannot use any **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Attack}]]** or **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Manipulate}]]** action that doesn’t contribute to physically freeing yourself.
---
#### <u>Prone</u>
*You are lying on the ground or otherwise prostrated.*
- You cannot use any **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]** [[1.4 Actions|Action]]s except **[[Movement Actions|Crawl]]** or **[[Movement Actions|Change Stance]]**.
