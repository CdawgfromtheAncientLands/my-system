# What is a condition?
Occasionally, bad shit will happen to you, or other creatures. For ease of reference and citation, common altered states (Conditions) are written on this page. 

Some conditions have a single on/off effect; others **stack** in levels (marked as X). Any **X-condition** always has a number (its current rank) attached to it. If that number would be **0**, treat the condition as **not applied**.

When applying conditions, two different phrasings may be used:
##### “Suffers *Condition* +X”
- Increase the target’s current rank of that condition by **X**.
##### “Is/Becomes *Condition* X”
- Set the target’s current rank of that condition to **X** (or **1** if X isn’t specified), **unless** their current rank is already higher; in that case, no change occurs.
