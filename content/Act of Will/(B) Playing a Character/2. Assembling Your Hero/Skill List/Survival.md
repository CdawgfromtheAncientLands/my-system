Survival is the knowledge and instinct required to endure the wilderness, understand an environment, and adapt to places where the world does not care if you live or die. The skill blends practical know-how, field improvisation, and a sense for the natural world.
### **Forage** [[2. Attributes & C|INS]]/[[2. Attributes & C|IQ]]
Finding natural resources and supplies.
- Identifying edible plants
- Locating water, shelter materials
- Scavenging useful detritus
### **Track** [[2. Attributes & C|INS]]/[[2. Attributes & C|IQ]]
Following physical signs left by creatures or people.
- Prints, trails, broken foliage
- Tracing wounded targets
- Determining pace and direction
### **Navigate** [[2. Attributes & C|INS]]/[[2. Attributes & C|IQ]]
Traveling efficiently and safely.
- Using maps, stars, landmarks
- Plotting overland routes
- Avoiding hazards or dead-ends
### **Fieldcraft** [[2. Attributes & C|INS]]/[[2. Attributes & C|IQ]]
Hands-on wilderness tasks and improvised construction.
- Building shelters, fires
- Setting snares or simple traps
- Making tools from available materials
