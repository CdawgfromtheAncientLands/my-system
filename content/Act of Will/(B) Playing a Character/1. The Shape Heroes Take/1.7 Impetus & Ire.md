As you collect [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Triumph]]s, they stew and flicker within your heart - your confidence, energy, and courage soar to great heights! This confidence manifests in [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Impetus]]: that burning mixture of dopamine and adrenaline that lets you feel unstoppable. [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Impetus]] is a resource that can be spent, either: 
- In lieu of [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Resolve]]
- To perform [[Daring Actions]] in an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]]
- Or something else during narrative scenes, idunno.
Whenever your turn begins in an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]], you gain [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Impetus]] equal to your current [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Triumph]]s. [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Impetus]] goes away when an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]] ends.
## Inspired Impetus
**Inspired Impetus** is like normal impetus... except it sticks around between encounters - track it differently from the normal stuff.
- Whenever you gain a [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Triumph]], you also gain 1 [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Inspired Impetus]]. 
# Ire (The Bad Stuff)
*With every action comes... something.*
-Sir Isaac Newton.

As your momentum builds, so do the stakes. **Ire** does not mean the world simply becomes stronger - it means your actions matter more, and the consequences of failure grow sharper. Each success pulls you deeper into the situation: enemies become more alert, risks become harder to ignore, and mistakes carry greater weight. 

You are gaining the power to push forward, but also becoming more exposed with every step. This creates a rising tension that drives play toward decisive, high-stakes moments where pressing on could secure victory… or bring everything crashing down.
## Explain it Already!
[[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire]] is a global gauge of how tense things are. [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire]] applies at all times.

- The base [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire Level]] is equal to the highest among the party members' cached [[1.6 Triumphs & Escalation|Triumph]]s (should thankfully be easy to figure out). 
- During an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]] it may increase (or decrease!) depending on how things go, but never below its base level.
- [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire]] cannot go below 0 or above 9.

# Effects of Ire
The force of "Calamity" is upon the players - bad things that happen are measurably worse. Enemies may draw on rising [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire]] as instinct, fury, discipline, or supernatural force - whatever expresses the scene the best.

Every enemy statblock is expected to possess additional unique [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire]] abilities on their monster profile at Ire 3, 5, and 7.

All instances of [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]] and [[2.2 Stress|Stress]] suffered by the party and their allies is increased by the current [[1.7 Impetus & Ire|Ire Level]]. Note that the party's allies may NOT be full player-character sheets - meaning that yes, Short Round and Watson are at significantly higher risk with no apparent benefit from Triumphs or Impetus. Hey - if you care about them, you'll keep them out of the fight!
# Escalating Ire
*The GM should increase Ire by 1 when the party loses momentum (or when an enemy's ability specifically does so).*
This means:

1. **The party fails to change the board in a round**
    - No enemy meaningfully weakened, removed, or disrupted
    - No objective progress
2. **A PC or ally encounters deadly or traumatic harm**
	- Specifically a Breakdown or Death save made
3. **The encounter horrifically escalates**
    - Reinforcements, phase change, major environmental shift
# Restoring Hope 
*The GM should reduce Ire by 1 when the party decisively shifts control of the situation.*

1. **A key enemy is removed**
    - Boss phase, elite, controller, anchor
2. **The objective meaningfully advances**
    - Not just “progress” - IRREVERSIBLE PROGRESS.
