*Skills are the muscle memory, knowledge, instincts, and techniques utilized in performing certain courses of action or pertaining to distinct subjects. They are the meat and cheese of this system.*
# What's a skill?
When the time comes to actually take action, you're going to roll a Skill Check (2d12+Skill+Attribute).

The full formula for a skill check's bonus = (Linked [[1.1 Attributes|Attribute]] Mod) + ([[1. Skill|Primary Skill Score]]) + ([[1. Skill|Subskill Score]]).
# Core Skills
Before we get into that, though... there's a set of THREE very special skills that define a PC's defenses - the Core Skills. As you might've seen, they're used in calculating [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]] and [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] - and they don't belong to a Discipline or anything. The Core Skills are:

[[1.9 Levels & Progression]]
### <u><font color="#ff0000">Fortitude</font></u>
*The ability to endure physical suffering.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Physique]]
### <u><font color="#ff0000">Reflex</font></u>
*The ability to react, with speed, to stimulus.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Agility]]
### <u><font color="#ff0000">Resolve</font></u>
*The ability to assert your will and resist mental strain.*
> **Linked Attribute:** [[1.1 Attributes|Awareness]]
## Discipline
A Discipline is a wide family of general techniques and knowledge that contribute to one's excellence in several different (but similar) individual skills. A Discipline's rank is added to every check made for Skills within that Discipline.

Disciplines can range from +0 to +4.

## Skills
Skills are where expression matters. They range from Layman (+0) to Legend (+4). A character is 20% [[1.1 Attributes|Attribute]], 40% [[Talent]], and 40% [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]. Improving your [[Skill Rank]]s leads to unlocking new and powerful [[Talent]]s to prove your mettle with.
