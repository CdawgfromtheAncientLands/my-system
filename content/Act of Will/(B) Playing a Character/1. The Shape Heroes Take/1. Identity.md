# Heart (Instinct, Feelings, Motivation)
*Inside every soul is a wellspring of hope, love, and courage. Every hardship and obstacle may turn them into fear, hatred, and cowardice... and with every new triumph, they may change again.*

> *Are you kind out of hatred for the cruel - or love for the meek?*
> *Do you follow the law because you'd be hurt otherwise - or because breaking it hurts others?*

These are the kinds of questions the [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] helps to answer about a player character. For a PC, their [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] is their source of [[Resolve]] and [[Core Skills|Willpower]] - *why* they do. In a simpler (but less poetic) word, ***Motivation***.

Additionally, it serves to guide a character's instinctive reactions under high stress. When stakes rise, plans collapse, or pressure becomes deadly, a character must battle their own [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] for control of what to do - the character’s reactions may override their better judgment, values, or long-term goals.

A character's [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Heart]] doesn't have to be what they *want to be*. A black, doomed heart could belong to an incredibly kind [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Soul]], or a man in a dangerous profession may decide to be cruel out of kindness - to protect others through distancing himself. 

Particularly meaningful encounters may cause a **[[Change of Heart]]**.
# Soul (Beliefs, Behavior, Principles)
_Inside every heart is a sense of direction - an idea of what matters, what is worth protecting, and what kind of person they are trying to become. Beliefs give shape to action, even when the cost of following them is high._

> _Do you show mercy because you’ve seen what vengeance becomes?_  
> _Do you honor duty because chaos once took everything from you?_  
> _Do you trust in faith, luck, or destiny because certainty has failed you before?_

These are the kinds of questions the [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Soul]] helps to answer about a player character. A [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Soul]] is a character’s guiding star - _what_ they believe in and _where_ they are trying to go... it answers the question of what a character holds meaningful enough to shape their choices. In simpler (but less poetic) terms, _**Principles** or **Beliefs_**.

Additionally, the Soul guides a character’s **deliberate choices** when they have the time, clarity, or control to decide. When the moment allows for restraint, sacrifice, or resolve, a character looks to their Soul to determine the path they believe is right - even if it is difficult, dangerous, or costly.

A character’s [[1.1 Heart & Soul|Soul]] does not need to align cleanly with their Heart. A ruthless Heart may serve a merciful Soul. A fearful Heart may cling to an idealistic Soul. Much of a character’s struggle - and growth - comes from the effort required to keep belief aligned with instinct when the world refuses to cooperate.

Particularly meaningful encounters may cause a **[[Crisis of Faith]]**.

# Heart & Soul Together
Every character lives in the space between Heart and Soul.

- The **Heart** supplies the energy and pressure. It drives you in the moment - your instinctive sense of justice, disgust, fear, or more.
- The **Soul** shapes how that energy manifests. It's what you consciously try to stand for - and combined with your Heart, it makes up the *person that you are.*

There will always be some overlap between the two concepts. Motivations and Beliefs are ambiguously intertwined, and both reflect a character’s experiences, values, and sense of self.

When the two align, action feels effortless. When they conflict, that tension defines who the character truly is. 

Your character's Heart and Soul don't HAVE to be enemies - in fact, they could complement each other nicely. Then again, they don't have to match each other either..!

# Identity Tags
Each Heart and Soul is defined by a **Tag**, chosen from the following pairs. Although presented in opposition, a character may choose opposing Tags between their Heart and Soul. In fact, many compelling characters do. Stories in _The Daring_ are not just "days in the life" - they are disruptions of the status quo.

Each tag pair answers a core question in a distinct manner.

> **[[Bold Selves|Bold]]** or **[[Tortured Selves|Tortured]]**  
>**What are danger and pain, to me?**
> **[[Dutiful Selves|Dutiful]]** or **[[Free Selves|Free]]**  
>**What do I owe, and who do I owe it to?**
> **[[Detached Selves|Detached]]** or **[[Empathetic Selves|Empathetic]]**  
>**How much do I let myself care for others?**
> **[[Idealist Selves|Idealist]]** or **[[Skeptical Selves|Skeptical]]**  
>**Where does meaning come from - what I feel, or what I see?**
> **[[Faithful Selves|Faithful]]** or **[[Pragmatic Selves|Pragmatic]]**
>**What do I trust when certainty fails?**
