Talents are comparable to the "Feats" of D&D 5e and PF2e. Instead of classes, we have a massive and complicated web of Talents!

**Talents** are special abilities that define how your character approaches challenges and sets them apart from others with similar training. While your **[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]]s** and **[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s** express how capable you are, **Talents** shape how you apply that capability in play. 

A Talent might grant a new option, enhance a specific action, trigger an extra effect under certain conditions, or let you bend the usual rules in your favor. They are the source of your character’s unique edge - turning the same core abilities into entirely different playstyles - what makes player characters *very special boys and girls* in the eyes of the GM.

NPCs may possess Talents too - but never to the degree that a PC does.
