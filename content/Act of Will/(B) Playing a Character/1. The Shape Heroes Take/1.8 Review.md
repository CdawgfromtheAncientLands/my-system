So.... a Player Character is composed of:

1. Their Heart & Soul
2. Their Origin
3. Their 5 primary attributes
4. Their Speed, Senses, and Size
5. Their Vigor & Willpower
6. Their skills & specialties
7. Their talents
8. Their arms, armor, and equipment

And a Player Character tracks:

1. Their current Vigor, Willpower, and Endurance
2. Their Stress level (see Killing Your Hero)
3. Their Triumphs & current Impetus
4. Their Experience
5. Any ammunition or consumables they may have
6. The current Ire level, if they're smart
7. And any conditions they may have applied.
