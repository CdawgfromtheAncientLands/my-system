A **Carrying Kit** represents how your gear is distributed across packs, pouches, straps, and pockets. It abstracts backpacks, bandoliers, scabbards, holsters, belt rigs, and load frames into a single, meaningful item.

You can only have **one Carrying Kit equipped at a time**.

Carrying Kits **do not stack**.  

If an effect increases carrying capacity or access, it modifies your current kit rather than adding a second one.
# What a Kit Provides
### **Weight Negation**  
This many kilograms of carried items do **not** count toward your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Carry Capacity]]. They are treated as stored within the kit. 

If an item is within your [[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]], it is always considered readily available.  
If it is not, accessing it requires extra time, actions, or favorable circumstances, as determined by the GM.
### **Quick-Access Capacity**  
You may designate items totaling this many kg of items as _Quick-Access_. They are held within the carrying kit in easily accessible areas - like holsters, scabbards, chest pouches, or pockets.

Items in your [[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access Capacity]] can be drawn, stowed, or used with a single [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action, no need for rummaging, fumbling, or special handling.

Quick-Access Capacity is **binary**—items are either quick-access or they are not.

Items not stored in [[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access]] require at least 3 [[Misc Actions|Interact]] actions to retrieve.
### **Reflex Penalty**  
Some kits reduce your ability to dodge or evade. Apply this penalty to your **Reflex** while the kit is worn.
