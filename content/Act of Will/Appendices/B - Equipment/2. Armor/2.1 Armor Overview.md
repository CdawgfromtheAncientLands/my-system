# Armor Complexity
Armors come in a variety of complexities, requiring different training and techniques to effectively utilize.

> **If you are not properly trained in the usage of an armor, suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]]- and [[2. Attributes & C|Physique]]-based [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made while wearing it.**

The three classes of armor complexity and their requirements are as follows:
- Simple armors can be worn as easily as clothing.
- Martial armors can be wielded effectively by those who take the [[Armor Training]] talent.
- Advanced armors can be wielded effectively by those who take the [[Advanced Armor Training]] talent.
# Weight Class
Armor weight class determines the dice of the wearer's [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]] roll, how comfortable it is to sleep in, how high [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] affects the bearer, as well as the time it takes to don/doff.

> **If you do not meet the [[2. Attributes & C|PHY]] requirement for a suit of armor, suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]]- and [[2. Attributes & C|Physique]]-based [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made while wearing it.** (Stacks with penalty from Complexity).

- Light armors require at least -2 [[2. Attributes & C|PHY]], and do not change the wearer's [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]]. 
	  Don/Doff: 1 min, 1 min.
	  Can only gain the half-benefits of a [[1.2 Rest|Long Rest]] if doing so while wearing this armor.
- Medium armors require at least +0 [[2. Attributes & C|PHY]], and the wearer rolls a 1d10 for [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]].
	  Don/Doff: 10 min, 1 min.
	  Can only gain the minimal benefits of a [[1.2 Rest|Long Rest]] if doing so while wearing this armor.
	  You suffer the effects of high [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] at double the frequency.
	  You suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Finesse|Sneak]] checks.
- Heavy armors require at least +2 [[2. Attributes & C|PHY]], and the wearer rolls a 1d8 for [[1.2 Initiative|Initiative]].
	  Don/Doff: 30 min, 10 min
	  Can't benefit from [[1.2 Rest|Long Rest]]s while wearing. 
	  You suffer the effects of high [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] at double the frequency.
	  You suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] twice-over on [[Finesse|Sneak]] checks.
# Carried/Worn Weight
How many kg the armor actually weighs vs. the effective weight imposed on the armor’s wearer when it is donned. Armor usually has much better weight distribution when worn.
# Durability
Durability is rolled after encounters as usual as well as when the wearer is critically hit or suffers Acid or Ballistic damage. For each dice level difference between an armor’s current and maximum durability, it suffers -1 to each of its armor ratings, to a minimum of 1.
# Reflex Penalty
The armor’s wearer suffers this penalty to their [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]] checks and [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Passive Skill]] [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Reflex]].
# Armor Ratings
When the armor’s wearer suffers damage, they reduce incoming damage of some specific types by the number listed on the armor – ex. Ballistic 7, Energy 5, etc. An armor’s Energy rating, also called Insulation, applies to Fire, Cold, and Electricity damage.
