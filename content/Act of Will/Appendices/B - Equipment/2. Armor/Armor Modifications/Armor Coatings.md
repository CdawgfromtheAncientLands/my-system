## **Fireproofing**
**Coating Modification – All Armor**  
_Fire-retardant treatments layered over the armor’s surface to resist flame and extreme heat._

**Buy & apply cost:** 0.2× the armor’s cost  
**Repair $+** 10%

**<font color="#92d050">Bonuses</font>**
- +2 [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] against [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Fire Damage]].

## **Lacquering**
**Coating Modification – All Armor**  
_A thick chemical sealant that protects the armor from corrosion, rot, and chemical attack._

**Buy & apply cost:** 0.4× the armor’s cost  
**Repair $+** 10%

**<font color="#92d050">Bonuses</font>**
- +3 [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] against [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Acid Damage]].
- Increase the armor’s **maximum Durability by 1 level**.

## **Electroplating**
**Coating Modification – Metal Armor Only**  
_A thin electrochemically bonded layer of protective metal applied to exposed surfaces._

**Buy & apply cost:** 0.4× the armor’s cost  
**Repair $+** (–60)% - this coating reduces maintenance costs.

**<font color="#92d050">Bonuses</font>**
- +1 [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] against [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Acid Damage]].
- Increase the armor’s **maximum Durability by 1 level**.

## **Menacing Paintjob**
**Coating Modification – All Armor**

## **Slicking**
**Coating Modification – All Armor**  
_A waxed, oiled, or treated surface finish that sheds water, grime, and clinging substances._

**Buy & apply cost:** 0.1× the armor’s cost  
**Repair $+** 10%

**<font color="#92d050">Bonuses</font>**
- + [[2.2 Armor Traits|{Sleek}]]
- Ignore [[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]] or [[1. Terrain|Slippery Terrain]] caused by sticky or adhesive substances (mud, webs, sludge, tar, resin, etc.).
- If something would stick onto you, roll a DC 8 [[1.4 Flat Check]] - on a success, it totally fails to stick.
**<font color="#c00000">Penalties</font>**
- Every time you suffer [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Fire Damage]] from high [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]], roll a DC 3 [[1.4 Flat Check]]. On a failure, this coating is rendered permanently useless.
- When you suffer 10 or more [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Fire Damage]] at once (post-armor), the coating catches fire - suffer 1d6 Persistent Fire Damage, DC 4 flat check.
