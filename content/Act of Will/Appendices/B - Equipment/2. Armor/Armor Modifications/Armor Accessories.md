## **Weighted Knuckles**
**Accessory Modification – Armor with Gauntlets**  
_Dense reinforcement or weighted inserts built into armored gloves, turning mass into momentum._

**Bonuses**
- +1 damage to [[Melee|Unarmed]] [[Strikes]] with your hands.
- +1 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]].
**Penalties**
- Suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Finesse|Quick Fingers]] and [[Medicine|First Aid]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s.
- Suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]], [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]], and [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]].
- +1kg Worn/Carried Weight

## **Spiked Gauntlets**
**Accessory Modification – Armor with Gauntlets**  
_Spurs, studs, or spikes mounted to the hands, making close contact actively dangerous._

**Bonuses**
- + [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Serrated}]] 1d4 to [[Unarmed Weapons|Natural Blow]]s with your hands.
- + [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] on checks to **maintain a [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]]** with your hands.
**Penalties**
- Things you handle with these spiked gauntlets are highly likely to be prodded, bruised, punctured, or cut. Be careful what you touch, seriously.
- Suffer 2x [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Medicine|First Aid]] checks.

## **Clinch Plates**
**Accessory Modification – Armor with Gauntlets**  
_Textured plates, ridges, or studs integrated into armored palms to improve grip and leverage._

**Bonuses**
- +1 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]] or maintain a [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] using your hands.
- + [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] on checks to **hold onto ledges, ropes, or creatures** while armored.
- +1 [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]] against [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]] you.

## **War Chimes**
**Accessory Modification – All Armor**  
_Loose rings, chains, plates, or fittings deliberately designed to amplify sound and presence._

**Bonuses**
- +1 to [[Guile|Intimidation]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Checks]] while the armor is visible and audible.
**Penalties**
- You cannot benefit from the [[2.2 Armor Traits|{Hushed}]] trait at ALL.
- Suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Finesse|Sneak]] checks where sound is relevant.
- Creatures using the chimes against you have +1 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Checks]] made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] or [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]] you.

# **Drop Harness**
**Accessory Modification – All Armor**  
_Straps, hooks, reinforced loops, and retention lines mounted to the armor’s frame for securing gear._

You may secure a handheld item of 2kg or less to a [[Armor Accessories|Drop Harness]] with an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action. Those items count as [[3.1 Carry Kits|Quick-Access]].
[[2.1 Armor Overview|Light Armor]] has 2 slots, [[2.1 Armor Overview|Medium Armor]] 3, and [[2.1 Armor Overview|Heavy Armor]] 4.

**Bonuses**
- If you'd drop an item attached to a [[Armor Accessories|Drop Harness]], it instead hangs securely but loosely from your armor, taking an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action to properly stow.
- If using an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action to pick up items, you may pick up any number of items attached to the [[Armor Accessories|Drop Harness]] as long as you have the open hands to do so.
- Once per [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]], you may [[Misc Actions|Interact]] to pick up an item on the [[Armor Accessories|Drop Harness]] as a [[1.3 Actions|Free ✧ Action]].

**Penalties**
- Suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on any [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to squeeze through tight spaces.
- Unstowed items may be used as leverage against you, giving creatures +1 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]] or [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]] you. An enemy may also try to rip them off or sever them.
- +1kg Worn/Carried Weight

## **Biting Soles**
**Accessory Modification – Armor with Boots**  
_Serrated spurs, cleats, or ridged plates fixed to armored footwear to bite into unstable ground._

**Bonuses**
- Ignore [[1. Terrain|Slippery Terrain]] and [[1. Terrain|Difficult Terrain]] from snow, ice, or loose ground.
	- If not on [[1. Terrain|Slippery Terrain]], you have +1 [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]] against [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made to [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]], [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]], or otherwise forcefully move you. 
	- If not on [[1. Terrain|Slippery Terrain]] and you are being forcefully dragged by a creature that has you [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Grabbed]] or [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Restrained]], they must roll [[Athletics|Muscle]] against your [[Athletics|Muscle]] [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill DC]] or be unable to do so.

**Penalties**
- Suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Finesse|Sneak]] checks while moving across hard or smooth surfaces.

## **Rescue Loops**
**Accessory Modification – All Armor**  
_Reinforced handles, drag-rings, and grab points integrated into the armor’s structure._

**Bonuses**
- Creatures with hands, claws, or gripping mouths/appendages gain [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] on checks made to move you while you are [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]] or [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Unconscious]].
- The loops may be used as a secure fastening for rope.
**Penalties**
- Creatures with hands, claws, or gripping mouths/appendages gain [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] on checks made to move you while you are [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Prone]] or [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Unconscious]].
- +2kg Worn/Carried Weight
