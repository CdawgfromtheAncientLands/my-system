Armor modifications come in four flavors - [[Armor Structure Mods]], [[Armor Layers]], [[Armor Coatings]], and [[Armor Accessories]].

| Armor Type                          | Max Structural Mods | Max Layers | Max Coatings | Max Accessories |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------- | ------------ | --------------- |
| <font color="#4bacc6">Light</font>  | 2                   | 3          | 1            | 1 + Gloves      |
| <font color="#d99694">Medium</font> | 1                   | 2          | 1            | 2 + Gloves      |
| <font color="#c00000">Heavy</font>  | 1                   | 2          | 1            | 3 + Gloves      |
A piece of armor can't have multiple [[Armor Accessories]] that couldn't reasonably coexist with each other - such as more than two capes, three built-in shields, a helmed/mask based mod with a "no helmet" mod at the same time, and so on.
# Cost
Modifications will always cost a fraction of the armor's cost to apply, as well as a bit of time.
They will also add to the cost of repair by a percentage that cumulates with other mods (not compounding - for example, two 30% increases turn into a 60% increase).

If getting armor made-to-order with mods pre-included (not applied aftermarket), any [[Armor Structure Mods]] cost half as much to apply and only add half their maintenance cost penalty.
