Moving through liquid - whether water, sludge, mud, or viscous ooze -  affects mobility depending on depth and consistency.

| **Type**              | **Submersion**            | **Effects**                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waterlike Liquids** | _Partially (up to knees)_ | Creatures without a [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Swim Speed]] move as if in **[[1. Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]**.                                                                                                                 |
|                       | _Mostly (up to waist)_    | Creatures without a [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Swim Speed]] move as if in **[[1. Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]** and are [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Clumsy]] 1.                                                                                 |
|                       | _Fully (up to neck)_      | Creatures must have a [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Swim Speed]] or succeed at [[Movement Actions\|Swim]] actions to move more than 5ft at once. Those without [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Swim Speed]] are **[[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Clumsy]] 2**. |
| **Viscous Liquids**   | _Partially (up to knees)_ | All creature movement counts as **[[1. Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]**, and they are **[[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Clumsy]] 1**.                                                                                                             |
|                       | _Mostly (up to waist)_    | All creatures move as if in **[[1. Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]** and are **[[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Clumsy]] 2**.                                                                                                                       |
|                       | _Fully (up to neck)_      | Everything submerged is **[[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Strained]] 2**. Creatures must have a **[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Swim Speed]]** or succeed at [[Movement Actions\|Swim]] actions to move more than 5 ft at once.                        |
## Additional Notes
Liquids distort visibility, impose blur, and reduce the clarity of silhouettes depending on depth and agitation. *High pressure in bodies of liquid may injure creatures descending too quickly or deeply.*
### **Projectiles Through Liquid**
Use the rules for **Storm Winds** as a baseline. Adjust penalties based on viscosity, turbulence, and the physical properties of the projectile. Thrown or fired attacks passing into or through liquid typically gain increasing penalties the deeper the attack's target is.
### **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Auditory}]] Effects**
- Normal liquid can be treated as **Noisy**.
- **Viscous** liquids may count as **Loud** or even **Deafening**, depending on density and pressure.
### **[[Action & Effect Traits|{Visual}]] Effects**
For seeing or tracking through liquid, a GM may utilize the same visibility rules used for:
- rain
- snowfall
- fog
As seen in [[5. Weather Effects]].
