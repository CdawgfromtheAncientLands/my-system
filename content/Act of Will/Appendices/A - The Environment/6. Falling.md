When you freefall a number of feet past the sq. ft of space they occupy (see [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Size]] classes), they begin tracking the number of feet past that. When they stop falling via sudden contact with a solid object, they suffer [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]] according to their [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Weight]] class and per 5ft fallen.

| Weight Class | **Feet Fallen per Combat Round** | Fall Damage per 5ft (max fall damage) |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- | :-----------------------------------: |
| Miniscule    | 70 ft                            |               0.34 (3)                |
| Tiny         | 150 ft                           |                1 (30)                 |
| Small        | 300 ft                           |               1d4 (80)                |
| Medium       | 400 ft                           |               1d6 (200)               |
| Large        | 500 ft                           |              1d10 (500)               |
| Huge         | 700 ft                           |             1d10+5 (1000)             |
| Gargantuan   | 850 ft                           |             2d10+5 (1000)             |
Fall damage **cannot** be reduced by armor, resistances, or typical mitigation.

### When do I stop??
All creatures finish falling (take fall damage)...
- Instantly if 100ft or less
- Beginning of their next turn if any more

The GM is the final arbiter of where a creature is, mid-fall, when external factors intervene (wind, ledges, spells, grapples, etc.).
