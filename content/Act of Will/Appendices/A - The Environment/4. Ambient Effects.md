# <font color="#d83931">Ambient Noise</font>
Intense or chaotic sound can disrupt auditory actions, make communication difficult, and overwhelm creatures sensitive to noise.

| **Noise Level**         | **Effects**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Normal (0–69 dB)**    | No special effects.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Noisy (70–89 dB)**    | For every **20 ft** an **[[Action & Effect Traits\|{Auditory}]]** effect travels:<br>- The check to perceive or deliver it suffers -1<br>- Saving throws related to hearing gain +1 benefit.                                                                         |
| **Loud (89–109 dB)**    | As [[4. Ambient Effects\|Noisy]], but the effect occurs every 10ft instead of 20.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Deafening (110+ dB)** | As [[4. Ambient Effects\|Loud]], but the effect occurs every 5ft instead of 10. Additionally:<br>- All creatures in the area are [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Deaf]]. <br>- [[Action & Effect Traits\|{Concentrate}]] actions must pass a DC 3 [[1.4 Flat Check]] or automatically fail. |
# <font color="#d83931">Ambient Temperature</font>
Those in [[2.1 Armor Overview|Medium Armor]] or [[2.1 Armor Overview|Heavy Armor]] suffer effects from high temperature at twice the frequency.

| **Temperature Level**                                                                        | **Description**                                                         | **Effects (Every 10 Minutes)**                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <font color="#0070c0">**Cold** (–10°C to 10°C)                             </font>           | Chilly air, biting wind, discomfort over long periods.                  | Every hour:<br>- 1d4 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]] and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]].<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1 (Max 3)                                             |
| <font color="#0070c0">**Bitter Cold** (–25°C to –11°C)                                       | Frostbite risk, freezing breath, exposed skin numbs quickly.            | You are [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Strained]] +1.<br>- 1d4 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]] and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]].<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1 (Max 5)                     |
| <font color="#0070c0">**Deadly Cold** (–25°C or colder)                                      | Killing frost, exposed flesh freezes instantly.                         | Every 10 minutes:<br>- 2d3 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]] and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Cold Damage]].<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1                                               |
| <font color="#c00000">**Heat** (30°C to 40°C)                             </font>            | Oppressive humidity or scorching dry air, sweat loss, dehydration risk. | Every hour:<br>- 1d4 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]] (ignoring armor) and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]].<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1 (Max 3)                            |
| <font color="#c00000">**Blistering Heat** (41°C to 55°C)                             </font> | Heatstroke danger, blistered skin, exhausting dryness.                  | Every 10 minutes:<br>- 1d4 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]] (ignoring armor) and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]].<br>Every 30 minutes:<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1 (Max 5) |
| <font color="#c00000">**Deadly Heat** (56°C+)                             </font>            | Lethal radiance, burning air, metal sears to touch.                     | Every 10 minutes:<br>- 2d3 [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]] (ignoring armor) and [[3.2 Stress\|Stress]] equal to the suffered [[3.1 Damage & Resistance\|Fire Damage]].<br>- [[2.1 Reading a Condition\|Fatigued]] +1                              |
##### **Shelter**
Shelter removes **Stress effects** and slows **Fatigue** by half, but does not remove **Damage** in Deadly environments unless magical/technological.
# <font color="#d83931">Light Levels</font>
Light conditions determine how creatures perceive their surroundings using **[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Vision]]**.

| **Level**    | **Effects**                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bright**   | Anything in **Bright Light** can be seen clearly, being [[2.4 Detection Rating\|Observed]].                                                                                                                        |
| **Dim**      | Objects and creatures in **Dim Light** can be no better than **[[2.4 Detection Rating\|Concealed]]** to creatures whose only **[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Precise Sense]]** is normal [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Vision]]. |
| **Darkness** | In **No Light**, creatures can be no better than **[[2.4 Detection Rating\|Hidden]]** to creatures whose only **[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Precise Sense]]** is normal [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)\|Vision]].                |
# Other Factors
Some specific environmental factors not mentioned thus far can also have notable effects.
- Strong odors may induce [[2.1 Reading a Condition|Sick]]ness, [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] on exposure, or harm the ability to [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]] or use [[Observation]].
