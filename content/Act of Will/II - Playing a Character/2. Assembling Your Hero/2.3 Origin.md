People are shaped by layers that push and pull against each other - this includes your characters. 

Put them together in this order:
1. Your [[2.3 Origin|Species]] determines your base characteristics, and your [[2.3 Origin|Bloodline]] may modify them
2. Your [[2.3 Origin|Upbringing]] allows you a free [[1. Character Identity|Identity]] talent, [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Core Skill]] bonuses, and training in 2-3 [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]]s. Becoming trained in a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]] in this fashion does NOT increase [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s as usual.
3. Your [[2.3 Origin|Vocation]] grants you a set of starting [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s, an attribute increase, and a unique talent.

None of these stand alone. A hardened veteran raised in comfort is different from one raised in war. A celestial-touched outcast lives differently than one born into reverence. The same foundation, shaped by different pressures, produces entirely different people.

In this volume, only basic examples of these items are included. More will be included in setting expansions. 
# Species
A character's species determines their [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Sense]], [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Size]], and [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]]. Here's the base one:
### Human
Humans learn quickly, push themselves harder than they should, and thrive in environments that would break more rigid peoples. They build, organize, destroy, and rebuild... often within the same generation. Where others inherit identity, humans tend to **construct it**, piece by piece, through choice and circumstance.
[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Size]]: [[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Medium]]
[[1.4 Characteristics (WIP)|Speed]]: 20ft. Land
Humans have no special senses. 
*Instead of having [[2.3 Origin|Bloodline]]s, [[2.3 Origin|Human]]s receive an extra [[Identity Talent]] from their [[2.3 Origin|Upbringing]].*

# Starting Exp
Your GM will tell you how much EXP you start with! A GM is recommended to start an adventure around ~15-30 Exp, which is somewhere between [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]] 2 and 3.
