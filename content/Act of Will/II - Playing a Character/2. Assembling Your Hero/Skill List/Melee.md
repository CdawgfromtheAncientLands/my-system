**Melee** is your capacity to fight up close - blades flashing, fists flying, or heavy weapons swinging. It reflects training, instincts, footwork, stance, and the ability to strike or counterstrike under pressure at arm’s reach.
### **Blade** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]
Cutting and thrusting weapons.
### **Maul** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]/[[1.1 Attributes|PHY]]
Impact-based weapons.
### **Flail** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]
Flexible weapons with momentum.
### **Polearm** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]
Long-reaching weapons on a pole.
### **Unarmed** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]/[[1.1 Attributes|PHY]]
Hand-to-hand fighting.
