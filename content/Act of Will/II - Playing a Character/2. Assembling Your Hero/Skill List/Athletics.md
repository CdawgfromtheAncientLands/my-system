Athletics measures raw physical ability - movement, power, endurance, and the capacity to push your body through extreme demands.  
It covers everything from sprinting over broken terrain to swimming against a current or bracing your weight under crushing force. When a scene demands **whole-body control and exertion**, Athletics answers.
### **Run & Jump** [[1.1 Attributes|PHY]]
Covers bursts of speed, leaps, dodges, and rapid maneuvering.
- Sprinting in combat
- Evading collapsing terrain
- Leaping gaps, vaulting obstacles
- Chases and footraces
### **Swim** [[1.1 Attributes|PHY]] 
Movement through water or similar resistance.
- Crossing rivers, lakes, or flooded ruins
- Holding breath, diving, resisting currents
- Swimming while encumbered
### **Muscle** [[1.1 Attributes|PHY]]
The application of raw physical force.
- Lifting, pushing, dragging heavy loads
- Breaking doors or restraints
- Adds to damage with muscle-powered weapons
### **Acrobatics** [[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]
Balance, agility, and coordinated body control.
- Tumbling past threats
- Tightrope walking, narrow ledges
- Controlled falls, rolls, aerial flips
