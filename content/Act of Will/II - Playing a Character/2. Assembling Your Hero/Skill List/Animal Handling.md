**Animal Handling** is the skill of understanding, managing, and working with animals - whether domestic, wild, or extraordinary. It blends instinctive empathy, practiced technique, and the ability to project calm or authority. When you interact with creatures that don’t think like people, Animal Handling measures how effectively you guide, comfort, command, or befriend them.
### **Ride** [[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|AGI]]
Managing mounts and riding animals safely and effectively.  
This subskill covers the physical and instinctive knowledge required to stay mounted, steer a creature, and cooperate with it during motion or danger.
- Guiding a mount through rough or narrow terrain
- Staying seated during sudden impacts, leaps, or shifts
- Performing munted combat maneuvers
### **Command** [[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Directing trained animals through vocal, physical, or practiced cues. This subskill is used when giving actionable instructions to animals.
- Sending a dog to search or retrieve
- Coordinating a pack, team, or harnessed group
- Snapping an animal out of hesitation in a tense moment
### **Tame & Train** [[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Long-term behavior shaping, socialization, and bonding.  
- Calming a wounded or frightened animal
- Domesticating a wild creature over days or weeks
- Teaching animals tricks, tasks, or routines
- Creating reliable working or companion animals
