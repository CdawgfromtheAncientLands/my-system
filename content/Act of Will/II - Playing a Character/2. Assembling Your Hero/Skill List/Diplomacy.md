Diplomacy is controlled conversation and the ability to influence others through reason, respect, clarity, and good faith. It governs leadership in tense moments, the delicate navigation of formal settings, and the skill of teaching or guiding others.
### **Persuasion** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]
Changing minds through honesty, appeal, or logic.
- Negotiations, bargains
- Appealing to reason or emotion
### **Leadership** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Command presence and morale guidance.
- Coordinating groups under stress
- Inspiring teamwork
- Issuing decisive orders
### **Teaching** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]
Instructing others with clarity and patience.
- Training companions
- Demonstrating techniques
- Knowledge transfer under pressure
### **Etiquette** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Correct behavior in formal or delicate situations.
- Noble courts, ceremonies
- Organizational hierarchies
- Diplomacy between cultures
