**Arts & Expression** is the creative skill of shaping ideas into sensory or emotional form. It covers performance, crafting meaning, and producing works that stir, persuade, delight, or haunt.
### **Visual Arts** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Drawing, painting, sculpting, design.
- Schematics, murals
- Identifying artistic styles
### **Music** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]
Instrumental and vocal performance.
- Rhythm, harmony, improvisation
- Reading or composing music
### **Wordcraft** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]
Writing and spoken craft.
- Poetry, speeches, oratory
- Rhetoric, creative writing
### **Culinary Arts** [[1.1 Attributes|CHA]]/[[1.1 Attributes|LOG]]/[[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]
Preparing food with skill or artistry.
- Cooking in adverse conditions
- Flavor balancing
- Presentation and serving
