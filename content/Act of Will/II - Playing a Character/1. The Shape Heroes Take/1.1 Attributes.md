Players are, in addition to their Heart & Soul, also a game object composed of numbers and measures. 
# Primary Attributes
Primary Attributes are centered on the ability of the average human – which is 8 to 12 (-1 to +2). Primary Attributes serve as elements in Derived Attribute calculation, as well as contributing to Skill Checks.

| Attribute           | Description                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Physique (PHY)**  | *Physique is represents a creature’s bodily health, quality of build, immune strength, fast/slow muscle power, and overall physical might.*                                     |
| **Agility (AGI)**   | *Agility is a creature’s overall ability to quickly and accurately manipulate their own body - like balance, hand-eye coordination, manual dexterity, and speed.*               |
| **Awareness (AWA)** | *Awareness is a creature’s self-understanding, introspective ability, strength-of-instinct, ability to quickly and fully internalize sensory input, and a bunch of other crap.* |
| **Logic (LOG)**     | *Logic is a creature’s ability to consciously learn and recall information, draw conclusions, and apply knowledge to the real world.*                                           |
| **Charisma (CHA)**  | *Charisma is a creature’s ability to elicit emotions in, command the attention of, and manipulate others - done through presence, emotion, or sheer force-of-will.*             |
# Derived Attributes
*Derived Attributes* (you probably don't need to memorize the terminology, but it's useful for distinguishing them) are complex stats made from other attributes. There are only THREE necessary to play the game:
## <font color="#ff0000">Vigor</font>
> *As the dagger shears through the air toward your neck, you breathe in sharply and twist to the side - it only nicks your shoulder.*

**Vigor** represents a creature's reserve of stamina and bodily health - most commonly used to brace and avoid grievous injury. Your **Vigor** turn lethal blows into glancing strikes, rolling with the punches until you can't anymore. Anytime you take **Damage**, subtract it from your current **Vigor** (Unless you'd rather not?).

Loss of **Vigor** reflects mounting muscle fatigue, compromised footing, and minor injuries accumulating into something more dangerous (see Breaking & Dying).

**Vigor**, on occasion, may be spent to perform particularly demanding physical activities - representing bursts of motion and energy.

**Vigor = [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]] + 4 + ([[1.1 Attributes|PHY]] × 3) + ([[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Hardiness]] × 2)** + [[1.5 Character Talents|Talent Bonus]].

That means:
- 3× your Physique modifier (But never less than +1)
- Plus 4
- Plus your character's [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]].
- Plus 2x your [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Hardiness]] skill.

## <font color="#00b0f0">Willpower</font>
> _Your grip tightens as doubt creeps in. You steady your breathing, forcing your thoughts back into focus as you look ahead and wait for the enemy to overcommit._

**Willpower** represents a creature’s reserve of concentration, focus, and resolve - used to express their full potential through being spent to perform maneuvers, special techniques, or spells.

Anytime you gain **Stress**, compare it against your current **Willpower**. When your **Stress** exceeds your remaining **Willpower**, you must perform an [[Act of Will]].

Loss of **Willpower** reflects mounting mental fatigue, fractured concentration, and emotional strain building toward collapse.

**Willpower = [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]] + 2 + (max([[1.1 Attributes|LOG]], [[1.1 Attributes|AWA]]) × 3) + ([[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]] × 2)** + [[1.5 Character Talents|Talent Bonus]].

That means:
- 3× your higher of [[1.1 Attributes|Logic]] or [[1.1 Attributes|Awareness]] (But never less than +1)
- Plus 2
- Plus your character's [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]]
- Plus 2× your [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]] skill
- Plus its [[1.5 Character Talents|Talent Bonus]].
## Recovery Dice Pool
**Dice # = [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]] + [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Hardiness]] + [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]]**
A PC dice pool (d4s) used to restore energy during periods of rest. Each spent die restores:
- (1d4+[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Hardiness]]) to [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]], or
- (1d4+[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]]) to Willpower.
