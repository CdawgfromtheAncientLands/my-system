_Specialties represent a character’s narrow expertise: niche knowledge, training, or experience too focused to be a full Skill, but highly impactful in the right moment. A Specialty matters most when it’s the exact right tool at the exact right time._ 

Specialties are not a primary progression path. A Specialty never replaces a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]. Instead, it provides a **small, precise edge** when directly applicable. They exist to:
- Reward **character identity**
- Enable **situational brilliance**
- Create **memorable moments of relevance**

> A Specialty in _Smithing_ does not apply to all metalwork.  
> A Specialty in _Egyptology_ does not apply to all ancient history.  
> A Specialty in _Karate_ does not apply to all unarmed combat.
### **Using Specialties**
When making a Skill Check, the GM may allow a relevant Specialty to apply its [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Advantage]] (see [[1.9 Levels & Progression]]).
- Only **one Specialty** may apply to a check
- If relevance is unclear, err on the side of **not applying it**
- Specialties should **never outshine [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s**.
### **Ranks**

|Rank|Effect|
|---|---|
|**Dabbler**|You have basic familiarity and practical exposure|
|**Specialist**|You have focused expertise and refined understanding|
# **Language Specialties**
Language Specialties represent fluency in a language rather than a simple numeric bonus.

- **Dabbler** — Basic communication; simple speech and text
- **Specialist** — Fluent; nuanced speech, idioms, and complex writing

They typically **do not grant bonuses**, except in highly specific cases (e.g., decoding wordplay, cryptography, or subtle phrasing).
# **Martial Arts Specialties**
Represents training in a formal style (e.g., _Karate, Sambo, Muay Thai_).

- **Dabbler** — Understands fundamentals
- **Specialist** — Applies techniques reliably

These **do not grant bonuses to attacks**, but act as **prerequisites** for techniques, stances, or abilities tied to the style.
# **Subject Specialties**
Applies to very specific areas of knowledge or practice, such as:  
_Smithing, Appraisal, Lip-Reading, Cartography, Egyptology, Gambling, Sculpture, Knot-Tying_

- **Dabbler** — Recognizes basics, performs simple tasks
- **Specialist** — Handles complex tasks, notices fine detail

These enhance a **narrow slice** of the much-broader [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s, being utilized for skill checks to recall or utilize knowledge on the subject.
# **Instrument Specialties**
Represents proficiency with a specific instrument, like:  
_Guitar, Oboe, Waterphone, Sousaphone, Double Bass_

- **Dabbler** — Competent performance, simple pieces
- **Specialist** — Advanced play, improvisation, expressive control

Applies to relevant **[[Arts & Expression]]** checks.
