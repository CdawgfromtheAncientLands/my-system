As you build [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]], they stews and flicker within your heart - a burning mixture of dopamine and adrenaline that lets you feel unstoppable! 
# **Force of Will**
This confidence manifests in [[1.6 Fervor|Force of Will]]: a pool of 1d4s that you can spend and roll, either:
- In lieu of [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] (1 dice = one [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]])
- To perform [[Acts of Will]] during roleplay or encounters.
Whenever your turn begins in an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]], you gain [[1.6 Fervor|Force of Will]] equal to your current [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]]. [[1.6 Fervor|Force of Will]] goes away when an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]] ends.
### Lingering Force
**Lingering Force** is like normal Force of Will... except it sticks around between encounters - track it differently from the normal stuff.
- Whenever you gain [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]], the GM may decide to also award 1 [[1.7 Force of Will|Lingering Force]].
- A GM may also award [[1.7 Force of Will|Lingering Force]] for good behavior, excellent roleplay, or particularly outstanding accomplishments that don't necessarily merit gaining [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]].
