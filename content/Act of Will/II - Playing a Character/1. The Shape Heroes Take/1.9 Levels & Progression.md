So... you gain [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Experience]] through accumulating and reflecting on [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]] awarded by the GM! There are plenty of ways the GM may want to do this, all covered in the optional rules - but the main way this happens is through [[Rest]]s - see that section. Fervor converts into Experience at a 1:1 ratio.

For every 5 [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Experience]] a PC earns, they earn 1 [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Talent Point]]. 

You can spend your [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Experience]] and [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Talent Point]]s whenever you would gain them - not midday, not during a fight, not while you're chatting with the guy you're about to kill.

As a creature's level increases, so do its [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Core Skill]]s. Core Skill Points granted by [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]] can be used to increase [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Core Skill]]s, but no higher than the maximum enumerated in the below table.

# Dice Number Going UP!!!!!!
[[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]], [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]], and [[1.3 Specialties|Specialty]] ranks are increased using all that sweet [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Experience]] you've earned. When increasing [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]]s, their maximum rank is dependent on the rank of their parent [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]]. Additionally, a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]]'s increasing is gated by the character's current [[1.9 Levels & Progression|Level]]. 
### Improvement Cascade!
When improving a Discipline, gain a number of serial [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Skill]] Rank increases equal to your previous maximum Skill rank for that Discipline!

Apply these increases one at a time to any Skills within that Discipline. No Skill can exceed your new maximum.

To illustrate this, here's an example: 
> I increase my [[Melee]] [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Discipline]] to +3: *Authority* (Nice!). Currently, I'm a [[Melee|Maul]] *Expert*, but a *Layman* in all the other Melee Skills. Because I upgraded to +3, I have 3 increases to distribute! I'll increase [[Melee|Maul]] to *Master* level, and I'll put the other 2 increases into my [[Melee|Polearm]] skill, bringing it to *Expert* - I've had my eye on a masterwork [[Polearms|Halberd]] at the smith's for a while!

| Discipline Rank    | Bonus | EXP cost from previous | Child Skill Max | Level Req                             |
| ------------------ | ----- | ---------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| *Untrained* (D)    | 0     | 0                      | Adept (+1)      |                                       |
| *Trained* (C)      | +1    | 4                      | Expert (+2)     | [[1.9 Levels & Progression\|Level]] 1 |
| *Practitioner* (B) | +2    | 6                      | Expert (+2)     | [[1.9 Levels & Progression\|Level]] 3 |
