Rising [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]] makes you faster, stronger, more flexible... but something changes. Not the world itself - but how you experience it. The stakes haven’t changed, but your grip on them has.

Your pulse quickens. Your focus sharpens. Every movement feels faster, heavier, more immediate. Hesitation turns to decision, and you *commit*.

A simple strike becomes brutal. A close call becomes a desperate scramble. Details you might have ignored before become impossible to miss: every wound, mistake, and opening.**You’re deeper in it.**
 
Mechanically, this means:
- *Add your current Fervor to every instance of [[3.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]] and [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] you suffer*.
- *Monsters gain new abilities when the party reaches 2, 4, and 6 [[1.6 Fervor|Fervor]].*
