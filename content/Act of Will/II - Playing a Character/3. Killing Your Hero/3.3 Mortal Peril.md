As you suffer wounds and mental scars, your [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] condition increases.
# Grievous Physical Harm
When you would suffer damage that you can't mitigate with [[1.1 Attributes|Vigor]], this happens:
- You are **Injured** ([[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +1). If it was a Critical Hit or the damage exceeds your [[1.1 Attributes|PHY]] score, suffer [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +2 instead.
- Roll a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Hardiness]] save. The DC = 10 + the suffered damage. On a failure, you drop unconscious and begin [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]]. 
#### Condition: <u>Dying</u>
*Your life is in peril as you lie crumbling.*
- At the beginning of your [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] (or every 10 seconds), roll 1d4 ([[1.4 Flat Check|Death Save]]). 
- If the result is less than your current [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] value, suffer [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +1. 
- If you would stop [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]], you regain consciousness naturally in 1d4 hours. When you awaken, suffer a [[3.2 Stress|Severe]] [[3.2 Stress|Stressor]] (2d4).
# Mental Breaks
When you suffer [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] - and your new [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] level is greater than your current [[1.1 Attributes|Willpower]] - suffer a [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Mental Break]].

When you suffer a [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Mental Break]], suffer [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] +1 and choose:
- Perform a [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]].
- Attempt to [[3.4 Desperation|Lock In]], making a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]] save to refocus.
# The Peril Condition
**Peril** measures how close you are to irreparably breaking - physically, mentally, or both. It represents mounting injuries, faltering resolve, and the world closing in around you. As your Peril rises, everything becomes harder, riskier, and more desperate.
- You suffer a **penalty equal to your Peril** on all Skill Checks.
- The higher your Peril, the closer you are to permanent debilitating harm.
#### <u>Peril X</u>
*The world unravels around you as you fall to pieces. As you increase your [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]], mark each increase as Physical or Mental.*
- Suffer -X to all [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s. 
- When X=5, check your Mental and Physical Peril marks.
	- If you have 3+ Mental Peril, suffer a Crisis
	- If you have 3+ Physical Peril, you die on the spot. 
## Removal
Physical [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] can be removed by a medic or doctor using [[1.3 Medical Activities|Treat Wounds]].

Otherwise, the only universal way to remove [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] is to have a full Reprieve.
