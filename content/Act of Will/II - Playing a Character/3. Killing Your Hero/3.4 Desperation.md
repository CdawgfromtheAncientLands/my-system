So, you've suffered a [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Mental Break]]. You have two choices:
# Desperate Times...
When pressure builds, you can choose to just... let go. Let what go? Your basest impulses and emotions, of course! If you choose to perform a [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]], you are willingly heading into a risky situation - follow these rules! Any [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] you suffer during a [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]] doesn't take effect until the [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]] is over.

You must, as soon as possible, spend your [[1.4 Actions|Reaction ↻]] and a number of [[1.4 Actions|Action]]s you have equal to double your current [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] condition reacting unreasonably to the situation at hand in accordance with your [[1. Character Identity|Heart]]. During this period, [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] adds to your [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s instead of subtracting from them. 

After your [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]] is over, immediately apply all delayed [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] and become [[3.4 Desperation|Shaken]] for your next X [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]]s (ending at the end of your turn), where X is your current [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] condition. [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made in obvious and meaningful accordance with your [[1. Character Identity|Heart]] ignore the effects of [[3.4 Desperation|Shaken]].
#### Condition: <u>Shaken</u>
*You shudder as your body and mind register terrible shock.*
- Any penalties you suffer from [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] are doubled. Doubling from this effect does not convert into bonuses via [[Acts of Will|Dramatic Reversal]] or any similar effect, and only contributes to penalties.
- You have a +25% chance to automatically fail and waste any [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]] action you attempt.
# Locked In
Despite all your fervor and feeling, you may attempt to keep a cool head. Roll a [[1.2 Disciplines & Skills|Nerve]] save (DC = 14 + half current [[3.2 Stress|Stress]]) - on a success, you [[3.4 Desperation|Lock In]]. 
- Gain all the BENEFITS of a [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]] (Delayed [[3.2 Stress|Stress]], invert [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] penalty) for the same duration that a [[3.4 Desperation|Desperate Act]] would last.
- Reduce you and your allies' current [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] by Xd4 (X = your current [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] rank).

If you fail, though... Your thoughts scatter. Your timing slips. Your body hesitates at the worst possible moments. You haven't outwardly lost control - but inside, something's cracked.
- You become [[3.4 Desperation|Shaken]]
# In a Narrative Scene
The same mechanics still apply, but outside of a normal turn structure. A good player will do something risky and interesting in accordance with their character's [[1. Character Identity|Identity]].
