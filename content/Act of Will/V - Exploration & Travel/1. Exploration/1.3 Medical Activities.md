# Treat Wounds — 10 Minutes
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Requirements:** Access to a [[First Aid Kit]] or equivalent supplies. The patient is not [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]].
**Effect:** You stabilize injuries and calm the body of a patient. Attempt a DC 16 [[Medicine|First Aid]] check. 

- **Crit Success.** Same as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]], but restore double the [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
- **Success.** Reduce your patient's [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] condition from [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Injury]] or [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]] to 0. Restore 2d6 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to your patient.
- **Crit Fail.** Your patient suffers a Mild [[3.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2) as you fumble around.

**Limits & Notes:**  
Creatures restoring [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] from this [[1.3 Medical Activities|Treat Wounds]] are immune to the restorative effects until they suffer an [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Injury]] or have become [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]] again. Treat Wounds does **not** remove Injuries or prevent their penalties.

# Resuscitate — 1 Minute
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Requirements:** Access to a [[First Aid Kit]] or equivalent supplies. The patient is not [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]].
**Effect:** You try to motivate a patient back to consciousness. Attempt a DC 16 [[Medicine|First Aid]] check.

- **Crit Success.** Same as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]], but restore an additional 2d4 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to the patient.
- **Success.** If your patient is currently unconscious due to [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Injury]], they are now conscious with 1 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
- **Crit Fail.** Your patient suffers a Mild [[3.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2). If they would [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Breakdown]] from this, they do so and fall unconscious for another 1d4 hours directly after.

# Operate — X Hours 
[[Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]], [[Action & Effect Traits|{Concentrate}]]
**Requirements:** Access to a [[Surgeon's Kit]] or equivalent supplies. The patient is not [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]].
**Cost:** This takes 2 hours per [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Peril]] level of the treated [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Injury]] (min 1).
**Effect:** You attempt to stabilize and set an injury on the fast-track to healing. Your patient suffers 1d6 [[3.2 Stress|Stress]] every hour of treatment, with sedation or anesthetic possibly reducing or removing said [[3.2 Stress|Stress]]. Attempt a DC 16 [[Medicine|Surgery & Treatment]] check.

- **Crit Success.** Same as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]], but reroll any number of dice and take the better result.
- **Success.** Roll the dice of the injury's [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Heal Period]] and reduce the days remaining by that much, but to no less than X days remaining where X is the number of dice rolled. Mark the injury as *Operated*, and it cannot be targeted by this activity again.
- **Crit Fail.** Your patient begins [[3.3 Mortal Peril|Dying]] and suffers a [[4. Trauma, Injury, & Degrees of Such|Scar]] for the injury being treated.
