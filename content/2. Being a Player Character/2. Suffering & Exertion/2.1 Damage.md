There are two overall categories of damage – [[material damage]] that can be blocked with physical barriers and come from close contact with something real and tangible – like fire, a bullet, or a pool of acid – and [[immaterial damages]] that can’t reasonably be blocked by any sort of real-life material. Some damages have special rules attached to them.
## <u><font color="#ff0000">Material Damages</font></u>
### **Kinetic Damage** 
Kinetic Damage is a group consisting of damages suffered from unfortunate impacts with physical objects of different shapes and speeds. It includes:
- **<font color="#ff0000">Blade</font>**, suffered from cutting and slashing from a sharp edge.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Blunt</font>**, suffered from impact with, well, blunt objects.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Ballistic</font>**, suffered from impacts with small objects at incredible speeds, like bullets.
### **Energy Damage** 
Energy Damage consists of damages suffered from violent transfers of energy.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Fire</font>** is suffered when a creature suffers drastic increases in temperature.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Cold</font>** is suffered when a creature suffers drastic decreases in temperature.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Electricity</font>** is suffered when a creature contacts high amperage electrical currents.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Sonic</font>** occurs when a creature suffers intense oscillations or pressure, like a shockwave.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Acid</font>** is the violent denaturation of material through chemical reactions.
### **Precision Damage** 
Precision Damage is a special damage type caused through strikes to vital areas, usually occurring in conjunction with another damage type during an attack.
# <u><font color="#ff0000">Immaterial Damages</font></u>
- **<font color="#ff0000">Bleed</font> Damage** is caused by loss of vital internal fluids or energy.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Psychic</font> Damage** consists of tangible harm to one’s neurological function.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Poison</font> Damage** directly attacks one’s biological material through toxic affliction.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Spirit</font> Damage** is harm directly to one’s soul.
- **<font color="#ff0000">Necrotic</font> Damage** is the decay of organic material on the creature.
