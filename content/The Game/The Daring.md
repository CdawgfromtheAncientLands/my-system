That's the point of the game. Characters go on journeys which are dangerous in some manner.

The system will be interacted with through dice. Bitch.

The point of the game is to have highly-personalized PCs that struggle with themselves and each other - far from superheroes. They have real issues, and real concerns.

This iteration is mostly catered to "realistic" settings and conflicts. 

This isn't a super-heroic romp through hordes of goblins - popping out on the other side, clean and shiny after chugging some potions, to walk toward a monologuing villain and shove his face in the dirt.

In this system, the pools of blood at your feet, the screams and yelps of the horde, and each nick from their knives matters (relatively high damage and low endurance). Gruesome work and tense situations fray your nerves.

Mechanically, the system is about confronting challenges by exerting yourself, being careful not to break under the demands. You are encouraged to gather every advantage possible.
