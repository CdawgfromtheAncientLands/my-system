Cover represents obstacles that partially or fully shield a creature from attacks, explosions, or detection. When behind an object that could block line-of-fire or obscure your presence, you gain one of three Cover types.

Cover only applies against attacks or effects coming from directions where the line of fire is partially blocked. You can have Cover against one creature but not another.

| **Cover Type**   | **Bonuses**                                                                                                                                                                          | **Examples**                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lesser**       | +1 to [[Core Skills\|Reflex]] [[1.1 Skill Checks\|Skill Check]]s and [[1.4 Secondary Attribute\|Skill DC]]s made against attacks or effects coming from the other side of the cover. | Creature your size or 1 rank smaller, low railing, thin bushes. |
| **Intermediate** | As Lesser, but +2 to [[Core Skills\|Reflex]] and can attempt to [[Defensive Actions\|Hide]] with +1 to the check.                                                                    | Chest-high barricade, creature larger than you, small tree.     |
| **Greater**      | As Intermediate, but +3 to [[Core Skills\|Reflex]] and +2 to [[Defensive Actions\|Hide]]                                                                                             | Battlement, arrow slit, pavise shield.                          |
## Using Cover

### **Increasing Cover**
If you are benefiting from any Cover, you may use [[Movement Actions|Change Stance]] to treat that Cover as **one degree higher** until the start of your next turn.  
When **Prone**, you may also use Change Stance to treat your Cover as **Greater** if the attack is not coming from higher ground.
### **Total Blockage**
If an obstacle **fully** blocks line of effect, you have **full protection**—the attacker cannot target you at all unless the GM rules otherwise.

## GM Guidance

A GM may allow players or creatures to overcome Cover tactically:
- Shooting through an arrow slit provides **no penalty to shoot from inside**, but gives **Greater Cover** against incoming ranged attacks.
- Leaning around a corner to reduce Cover for your attack usually requires **an action** (typically an Interact or Change Stance action).
- Cover may shift dynamically as a creature moves, crouches, or locks into firing positions.

Cover is situational and relative—terrain, height, angle, and object size determine what counts. The GM adjudicates edge cases using the examples above as anchors.
