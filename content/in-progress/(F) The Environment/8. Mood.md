Some environments feel particularly soothing or oppressive.

### <font color="#ffc000">Using Mood at the Table</font>
The GM sets a Mood whenever the environment’s _emotional pressure_ is relevant. This can be:
- the soul-deep quiet of an ancient forest
- the sickening hum of a corrupted reactor
- the cheerful calm of a shrine or sanctuary
- the twisting dread of a haunted archive

Moods should be used intentionally—not on every map square. They work best when:
- entering a new region
- dealing with supernatural or psychological forces
- resting somewhere unusual
