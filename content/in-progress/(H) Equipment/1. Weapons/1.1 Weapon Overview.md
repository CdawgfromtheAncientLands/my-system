# What is a Weapon?
A [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon]] is a tool explicitly meant for use in [[Strikes|Strike]]s. It has durability, a complexity, weight, some traits, a number of required hands, damage dice + type, and a [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon Family]]. 
# Families
Every weapon belongs to a [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon Family]]. There is a [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon Family]] for each [[Ranged]] and [[Melee]] [[1. Skill|Subskill]]. 
# Weapon Complexity
Weapons come in a variety of complexities, requiring different training and techniques to effectively utilize.

> **If you are not properly trained in the usage of a weapon, suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made with it.**

The three classes of weapon complexity and their requirements are as follows:
- <font color="#9bbb59">Simple</font> weapons can be wielded effectively after just a few moments of testing them out.
- <font color="#fac08f">Martial</font> weapons can be wielded effectively by those who are *Adept* or greater in the weapon's [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon Family]], or who take the [[Weapon Training]] [[Talent]]. 
- <font color="#de5ad8">Advanced</font> weapons can be wielded effectively by those who are *Master* or greater in the weapon's [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Weapon Family]], or who are *Adept*+ and take the [[Weapon Training]] [[Talent]]. 
# Weight Class
Weapons come in a variety of weights. The heaviest weapons deal the most damage, but the lightest weapons are somewhat easier to score hits with.

> **If you do not meet the [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]] requirement for a weapon, suffer [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made with it.** (Stacks with penalty from Complexity).

- <font color="#4bacc6">Light</font> weapons require at least -2 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
- <font color="#d99694">Medium</font> weapons require at least +0 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
- <font color="#c00000">Heavy</font> weapons require at least +2 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
