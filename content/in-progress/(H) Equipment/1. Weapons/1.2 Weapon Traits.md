### **{Anti-Armor}-X**
> _Anti-Armor_ weapons ignore X [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] that would be applied to its incoming [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]].
### **{Backswing}**
_Backswing_ weapons retain momentum after a miss. 
If a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon is a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Failure]] or [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]], the next [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] made with the weapon this [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] gains +1.
### **{Climbing}**
Hands holding a _Climbing_ weapon are considered freely available to [[Movement Actions|Climb]] with. 
These weapons are usually equipped with some sort of hooked edges or picks.
### **{Concealed}**
_Concealed_ weapons can easily be stowed within normal clothing without being obvious.
### **{Concussive}**
These weapons smash as much as they puncture or cut. 
When applying **[[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]]**, **[[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Resistance]]**, or **Immunities** to damage from a _Concussive_ weapon, use the weaker of the target’s values between [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blade Damage]] and [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]].
### **{Counter-Charge}-X**
These weapons are especially good at using a target’s momentum against them. 
When a _Counter-Charge_ weapon hits as part of a **[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]-triggered [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]]** or [[1. Types of Actions|Free ✧ Action]], it deals an extra X damage.
### **{Deadly}-X**
[[Strikes|Strike]]s with _Deadly_ weapons do X more damage on a **[[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Success]]** (Not doubled).
### **{Forceful}**
Whenever a _Forceful_ weapon attacks, it gains a stacking **+1 damage** bonus to further attacks this turn.
### **{Free Hand}**
_Free Hand_ weapons do **NOT** occupy a hand when wielded.  
Using a _Free Hand_ melee weapon while that hand is holding something, though, suffers **-10 to Skill Checks per KG** of mass of the weapon.
### **{Vigorous}-X**
[[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s performed with a *Vigorous* weapon cost X [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to perform.
### **{Lance}-X**
For every 20ft you move in a [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]], gain +X damage to your next [[Strikes|Strike]] with a *Lance* weapon before the beginning of your next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]].
### **{Parry}-X**
_Parry_ weapons can be raised up with the **[[Defensive Actions|Defend]]** action to add X to their wielder’s **[[Core Skills|Reflex]] [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]]** against melee [[Strikes]].
### **{Predatory}-X**
_Predatory_ weapons deal X more damage per rank of [[Condition|Vulnerable]] on the target.
### **{Reach}+X**
_Reach_ weapons add X ft to their effective [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Melee Reach]].
### **{Savaging}-X**
These weapons have characteristics that ravage soft targets, but fall flat when faced with armor. When a _Savaging_ weapon deals damage and none of that damage is reduced by [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]], it deals X extra damage.
### **{Shove/Trip/Grapple/Disarm}**
Weapons with the _Shove_, _Trip_, _Grapple_, or _Disarm_ trait supersede the free-hand requirements for their respective wrestling action, providing all necessary shape and leverage to perform the maneuver with the weapon itself.  
If these weapons would have a bonus to effective **Strike** range, they also extend the range of their respective maneuver.

---

### **{Shove/Trip/Grapple/Disarm – Improved}**

Weapons with _Improved Shove_, etc., benefit from the above trait (you can assume), except that when using their respective maneuver, they can use whatever skill the weapon uses instead of another skill.

---

### **{Sweep}**

Whenever a _Sweep_ weapon attacks a target, its wielder gains a **-10 penalty** on subsequent **Attack Rolls** against different targets until the beginning of the wielder’s next turn.  
This effect does not stack with itself.

---

### **{Swap (X)}**

With an Action, a creature wielding a _Swap_ weapon can change their grip to effectively use the weapon as a different one (noted as X).  
Swapping back is also an action.

---

### **{Thrown X}**

_Thrown_ weapons can be used in a **Shoot/Throw** action with the **Throw** skill, with a range increment of X feet.

---

### **{Versatile}**

_Versatile_ weapons can be wielded with either 1 or 2 hands.

When wielding a **2-handed Versatile** melee weapon in one hand:  
• Increase the weapon’s **{Heft}** by 5  
• Subtract 1 from your **Muscle Damage Bonus** (minimum 0)

When wielding a **1-handed Versatile** melee weapon with two hands:  
• Decrease the weapon’s **{Heft}** by 5  
• Add +1 to your **Muscle Damage Bonus**

The trait can also add additional traits (noted as **+Trait** or **–Trait**) depending on the grip used.

---

### **{Wide Swing}**

These weapons require a wide arc of motion to properly swing.  
If, during an attack with a _Wide Swing_ weapon, there are obstacles or non-target creatures **in front of or to the sides** of the wielder that are equal to the wielder in size or larger, the wielder suffers **-10** on the attack.
