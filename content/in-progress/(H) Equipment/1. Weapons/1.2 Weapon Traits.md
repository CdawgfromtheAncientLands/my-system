## **{Anti-Armor} X**

_Designed to pierce through reinforced plating, spikes, picks, and armor-cracking blades._  
This weapon ignores X [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] when dealing [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Damage]].

## **{Backswing}**
_A weapon that carries momentum through a miss, letting the next strike whip around with force._  
If a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon is a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Failure]] or [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]], your next [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] with it this [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] gains +2.

---
## **{Bind} X**
_A duelist’s technique—catching a foe’s weapon in your own guard to open space for defense._  
On a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]]ful or better [[Strikes|Strike]] against a target with a [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Bind}]] weapon, increase your [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] by X against the next [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] the target attempts against you (until the beginning of your next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]]).

---
## **{Holdfast} X**
_Set against a rush—spears, long blades, and anchored weapons that punish advance._  
If a creature enters your [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Melee Reach]], your first [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] with a [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Holdfast}]] weapon against that creature before the end of your next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]] has a +X bonus.

---
## **{Climbing}**
_Weapons shaped for hooks, spikes, or anchoring during ascent—ice axes, picks, claws._  
Hands holding this weapon count as free for [[Movement Actions|Climb]]ing.

---
## **{Close-Quarters}**
_Short, compact weapons that excel in grapples, tunnels, and tight melee scrums._  
Ignore penalties to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s made with [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Close-Quarters}]] weapons while **Grabbed**, **Restrained**, or fighting in cramped or obstructed environments.

---
## **{Concealed}**
_Slim, easily hidden weapons—daggers, stilettos, folding tools, sleeve blades._  
This weapon can be stowed within normal clothing without being obvious.

---
## **{Concussive}**
_Blunt-and-edge hybrid weapons that shake armor and flesh alike—maces, picks, heavy blades._  
Use the weaker of a target’s [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blade Damage]] or [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]] mitigation when applying [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] or [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Resistance]] to this weapon’s damage.

---
## **{Counter-Charge} X**
_Weapons meant to catch incoming force—glaives, polearms, heavy chopping blades._  
When this weapon hits as part of a **[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]]-triggered [[1. Types of Actions|Reaction ↻]]** or **[[1. Types of Actions|Free ✧ Action]]**, it deals +X damage.

---
## **{Deadly} X**
_Weapons that deliver decisive critical hits—thin thrusting blades, spike-edged axes._  
On a **[[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Success]]** [[Strikes|Strike]], this weapon deals +X damage (not doubled).

---
## **{Executioner} X**
_Brutal finishing weapons—heavy cleavers, beheading swords, crushing implements._  
If a [[Strikes|Strike]] with an [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Executioner}]] weapon hits a creature with X HP or less, it reduces them to 0 HP.

---
## **{Forceful}**
_Weapons that build pressure with each committed swing—curved blades, heavy cutters._  
Whenever you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with a [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Forceful}]] weapon, gain a stacking +1 bonus to damage with said weapon.

---
## **{Free Hand}**
_Light, maneuverable weapons that leave the hand mostly unoccupied._  
This weapon does not occupy a hand when wielded. Using it while holding another object imposes –2 to to the weapon's [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s per kg of the held item.

---
## **{Hooked} X** 
_Blades or tools with catches, hooks, or barbs—sickles, naginata, certain axes._  
When you hit a [[Strikes|Strike]] with a [[1.2 Weapon Traits|Hooked]] weapon, your next [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]] or [[Combat Maneuvers|Disarm]] attempt against the target of that [[Strikes|Strike]] gains +X to the [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]].

---
## **{Impale} X**
_Narrow or forceful weapons that drive deep at the cost of their edge or point._  
When you [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Success]] on a [[Strikes|Strike]] with an [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Impale}]] weapon, reduce this weapon’s Durability by 1 level to deal +X damage.

---
## **{Lance} X**
_Weapons that scale in force with momentum—lances, spears, charging blades._
For every 20 ft you move in a turn, your next [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon before your next turn deals +X damage.

---
## **{Leverage} X**
_Long-handled weapons that turn reach into mechanical advantage._

---
## **{Parry} X**
_Weapons suited for deflecting attacks—rapiers, sabers, balanced swords, parrying daggers._  
You may raise this weapon with **[[Defensive Actions|Defend]]** to add +X to your **[[Core Skills|Reflex]] DC** against melee [[Strikes]].

_Weapons whose shapes replace the need for a free hand—hooks, chains, cudgels, polearms._

_Weapons requiring significant clearance—greatswords, poleaxes, sweeping cutters._
