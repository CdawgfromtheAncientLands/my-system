Ranged weapons share many of the same characteristics shown in [[1.1 Weapon Overview]] and [[1.2 Weapon Traits]]. 
# Damage
Only [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Thrown}]] ranged weapons and Bows account for [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]] bonus when rolling damage.
# Range
As opposed to a [[1.1 Weapon Overview|Melee Reach]], [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Ranged]] weapons have a [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Range Increment]]. [[Strikes|Strike]]s made within the weapon’s first increment are made with no penalty - but for each increment beyond the first, wielders suffer **-2** to the [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]].
# Ammunition
Ranged weapons have an [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Ammo Capacity]] (max pieces of ammunition it can hold) and [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Ammo Type]] (type of ammunition it accepts and uses).
# Reload X
A [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Ranged]] weapon that requires ammunition also requires X [[Misc Actions|Interact]] actions to fully replace the weapon's loaded ammunition.
# Rapid Fire (Rate of Fire / RoF)
Some weapons can fire off multiple projectiles in the same [[Strikes|Strike]] action. In this case, there are 2 characteristics to worry about:
- [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] cost per projectile shot (Known as [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Recoil]])
- The weapon's [[1.3 Ranged Weapons|Burst Max]] characteristic
See [[Strikes|Rapid Fire (⟐)]].
