### **{Black Powder}**
Black Powder weapons are unreliable in rain, humidity, or storm conditions.  
When you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon in such an environment, the GM may call for a **Misfire Check**:  
Roll 1d12. On a **1**, the weapon **Misfires**.

---
### **{Volatile} X**
If this weapon **Misfires**, it violently explodes in a **5-ft burst** centered on the wielder.  
Creatures in the burst must make a DC 16 [[Core Skills|Reflex]] save.
- **Success:** Take half X damage.  
- **Failure:** Take full X damage.  
- **Critical Failure:** Take double X damage.

---
### **{Manual}**
This weapon requires a deliberate mechanical action (bolt cycling, hammer-cocking, cranking, etc.).  Whenever you perform a [[Strikes|Strike]] action with this weapon, you must perform an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action as part of the [[Strikes|Strike]]'s cost.

---
###  **{Misfire} X**
This weapon is prone to malfunction. Whenever you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon and it results in a [[1.1 Skill Checks|Failure]] or worse, roll a DC X [[Flat Check]]. On a fail, the weapon loses 1 durability and is rendered non-functional. 

To clear it, spend an [[Misc Actions|Interact]] action and make a [[Survival|Fieldcraft]], or [[Artifice|Mechanical]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] (Simple = DC 15, Martial = DC 17, Advanced = 19). 
- [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]]: Weapon is functional again.
- [[1.1 Skill Checks|Crit Fail]]: Weapon suffers -1 durability.

For each point of difference between a weapon’s **maximum Durability** and its **current Durability**, increase its Misfire rating by 1 (Misfire 1+ → 2+ → 3+, etc.) until repaired.

---
### **{Multifire} X**
This weapon can fire multiple shots simultaneously. When you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with this weapon, you may choose to fire up to **X ammunition**. If you do, roll the weapon’s damage **X times** and keep the **highest** result.

---
### **{Single Loader} X**
You may spend **X [[Misc Actions|Interact]] actions** to load **1** piece of ammunition into this weapon.

---
### **{Spread} X**
This weapon fires a wide blast of projectiles. When you make a [[Strikes|Strike]] with a [[1.4 Ranged Traits|{Spread}]] weapon, after choosing the primary target, you must select up to **X additional creatures (or any other meaningful target) that:
- are **adjacent** to the primary target, **or**
- are **between you and the target** in the line of fire (15ft wide).

For the [[Strikes|Strike]], make ONE [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]] and compare it to all affected creatures’ [[Core Skills|Reflex]] DCs.
- Any creature whose DC is met or exceeded takes the weapon’s full damage.
- Any creature whose DC is not met takes no damage.

Spread attacks ignore cover from creatures between you and the primary target.

---
### **{Volley} X**
This weapon struggles at short range. When you [[Strikes|Strike]] a target within **X feet**, suffer -3 to the [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]].

---
### **{Versatile} (ranged)**
This weapon may be wielded in 1 or 2 hands.
- When wielding a 1-handed weapon in 2 hands, treat the weapon's weight class as 1 lighter.
- When wielding a 2-handed weapon in 1 hand, suffer -2 to [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s with the weapon.
