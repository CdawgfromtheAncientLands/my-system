**Shields** are somewhere in between worn armor and wielded weapons. 
# Shield Complexity
Shields come in a variety of complexities, requiring different training and techniques to effectively utilize.

> **If you are not properly trained in the usage of a shield, halve its [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] and [[4.1 Shields|{Block}]] trait while using it.**

The three classes of armor complexity and their requirements are as follows:
- <font color="#9bbb59">Simple</font> shields are usable with no training.
- <font color="#fac08f">Martial</font> armors can be wielded effectively by taking the [[Shield Training]] talent.
- <font color="#de5ad8">Advanced</font> armors can be wielded effectively by taking the [[Advanced Shield Training]] talent.
# Weight Class
Armor weight class the time it takes to don/doff.

> **If you do not meet the [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]] requirement for a shield, you cannot use the [[Defensive Actions|Shield Block]] reaction with it.**

- <font color="#4bacc6">Light</font> shields require at least -2 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
	  Don/Doff: 1 [[Misc Actions|Interact]], Free action to drop
- <font color="#d99694">Medium</font> shields require at least +0 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
	  Don/Doff: 1 [[Misc Actions|Interact]], 1 [[Misc Actions|Interact]] 
- <font color="#c00000">Heavy</font> shields require at least +2 [[1.3 Primary Attribute|PHY]].
	  Don/Doff: 3 [[Misc Actions|Interact]]s, 1 [[Misc Actions|Interact]]
	  These shields are pretty hard to carry through tight/contested spaces, sometimes even impossible.
# Durability
Durability is rolled when using [[Defensive Actions|Shield Block]]. 
# {Block X} Trait
When a shield is raised with [[Defensive Actions|Defend]], this number added to your [[Core Skills|Reflex]] [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]] against hostile effects. This is functionally the same as the [[1.2 Weapon Traits|{Parry}]] weapon trait.
## [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]]
When you [[Defensive Actions|Shield Block]], reduce damage taken by this much.
# Traits
Shields can use [[2.2 Armor Traits]] and [[1.2 Weapon Traits]], as well as their own.
