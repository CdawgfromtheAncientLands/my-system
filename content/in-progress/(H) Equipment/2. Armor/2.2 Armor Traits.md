### **{Anchored}**
Anchored armor is optimized for stable stances and planted footing.  
At the beginning of your [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]], this armor gains +1 to its [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blade Damage]], [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]], and [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Ballistic Damage]] [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]]s. This bonus is lost the next time you use a [[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Move}]] action.

---
### **{Bulwark} X**
Bulwark armor efficiently distributes incoming force over its wide area.  
A creature wearing this armor gains **+X [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]]** against [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Material Damage]] from **AoE effects** and **[[1.4 Ranged Traits|{Spread}]]** weapons, even if it would not normally have a Rating against that damage.

---
### **{Breathable}**
Breathable armor is fit for wearing in intense heat.  
Ignore any exacerbation of high [[4. Ambient Effects|Temperature]] that would be caused by this armor. 

---
### **{Claustrophobic} X**
Claustrophobic armor feels trapped, tight, and oppressive.  
While wearing this armor, [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Focus]] costs and [[2.2 Stress|Stress]] suffered are increased by X. [[2.2 Stress|Stress]] from other armor traits ([[2.2 Armor Traits|{Shock Absorbing}]], [[2.2 Armor Traits|{Stuffy}]]) are unaffected by this.

---
### **{Comfortable}**
Comfortable armor is padded, flexible, and designed for long-term wear.  
This armor can be worn while [[1.2 Rest|Long Rest]]ing with **no penalties or drawbacks**.

---
### **{Cumbersome}**
Cumbersome armor is thick, heavy, and awkward to move in.  
The wearer reduces all their [[1.5 Characteristics|Speed]]s by 5ft while wearing this armor.

---
### **{Entrench} X**
Entrenching armor includes braces, locking plates, or stabilizing mechanisms.  
When the wearer uses the **[[Defensive Actions|Defend]]** action, they may defend using this [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Armor]] to gain **+X to each [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] listed on the armor**.

---
### **{Fortressed}**
Fortressed armor disrupts incoming force through mass, surface area, and leverage.  
If a creature has [[Movement Actions|Traverse]]d this round, their next melee [[Strikes|Strike]] against you rolls damage twice and takes the lowest result.

---
### **{Hushed}**
This armor fits smoothly and moves slickly, avoiding any clanking or shuffling its design may normally create.  
Ignore 1 instance of [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on your [[Finesse|Sneak]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s imposed by this armor.

---
### **{Incognito}**
Incognito armor is designed to resemble ordinary clothing.  
Without close inspection or deliberate scrutiny, this armor is **indistinguishable from normal attire**.

---
### **{Inflexible}**
Inflexible armor is rigid and highly restrictive.  
Its **[[Core Skills|Reflex]] Penalty** is also applied as a penalty to your [[Athletics|Acrobatics]], [[Finesse|Quick Fingers]], and [[Athletics|Run & Jump]] [[1.1 Skill Checks|Skill Check]]s and [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Skill DC]]s.

---
### **{Menacing}**
Menacing armor exaggerates the wearer’s presence through size, shape, or sound.  
While this armor is visually present in an interaction, gain +2 to [[Guile|Intimidation]] - but [[1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Diplomacy|Persuasion]], [[Diplomacy|Etiquette]], and [[Guile|Theatrics]].

---
### **{Hard Frame}**
Hard-frame armor relies on inflexible plates and structural bracing to prevent lethal blows.  
When you would be Critically Hit by a [[Strikes|Strike]], reduce the damage by half. For every 5 damage reduced by this, suffer [[Condition|Strained]] +1 until the end of your next [[1.1 Encounters|Turn]].

---
### **{Shock-Absorbing} X, Y**
Shock-absorbing armor is padded, layered, or sprung to dissipate kinetic force.  
When you take [[2.1 Damage & Resistance|Blunt Damage]] or damage from blasts/explosions - and it would pass through the relevant [[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]] - immediately suffer X [[2.2 Stress|Stress]] and reduce that damage by Y.

---
### **{Sleek}**
Sleek armor is tightly profiled and difficult to get a solid hold on.  
Creatures suffer -2 to attempts to [[Combat Maneuvers|Grab]], [[Combat Maneuvers|Trip]], or [[Combat Maneuvers|Shove]] wearers of this armor.

---
### **{Stuffy} X**
Stuffy armor retains body heat and restricts airflow during exertion.  
At the beginning of your 3rd turn (and every other turn afterward) in an [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]], suffer X [[2.2 Stress|Stress]] unless you immediately spend a [[1. Types of Actions|Major ⟐ Action]] purposefully centering yourself and preventing overexertion (You can spend 1 [[5. Triumphs & The Daring|Daring]] and use [[Daring Actions|Upgrade Action]] to do so with a [[1. Types of Actions|Minor ⋄ Action]]).

---
### **{X-Resistant} Y**
This armor provides specialized protection against a specific, uncommon damage type: **X**.  
Its **[[2.1 Armor Overview|Armor Rating]]** is increased by **Y** against that damage type.  
This trait is typically **magical or supernatural** in nature.
