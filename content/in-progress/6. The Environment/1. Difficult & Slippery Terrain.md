Certain ground is uneven, sticky, cluttered, or otherwise harder to cross. Such spaces are considered **Difficult Terrain**.
- Moving into or through **Difficult Terrain** costs **double** the normal movement.
- Spaces occupied by a friendly creature or low obstacles (knee-high barriers, debris piles, shallow rubble, etc.) typically count as Difficult Terrain.
## Slippery Ground
Slippery surfaces—grease, oil, loose scree, wet stone, ice—can be treated as Difficult Terrain, but also risk knocking creatures [[Condition|Prone]].

Difficult & Slippery Terrain\|Somewhat Slippery]] | When a creature would move through **Kind of Slippery** ground, it may either treat the space as **Difficult Terrain**, or attempt a **Gymnastics / Reflex** check. On a failure, it falls **Prone** and its movement immediately ends. This choice and roll occur only once per **Move** action, at the first point of contact with Kind of Slippery ground. |
| [[1. Difficult & Slippery Terrain\|Very Slippery]]     | **Very Slippery** hexes are automatically **Difficult Terrain**. This roll occurs only once per **Move** action, at the first point of contact with Very Slippery ground.      |
