# **Life is an Ugly Choice**
 
Your life is the one thing in the world you cannot replace... so you protect it, even when the cost is ugly. You cut losses fast, detach when needed, and make calls others can’t stomach. You don’t enjoy abandoning people or dropping the rope - but you refuse to die just because of a bad idea. Accusations of cowardice and betrayal sting - and you regret things, sometimes deeply... but regret doesn’t keep you alive.

_People may call you ruthless, realistic, cold, or adaptive._

• _“No, she's not worth it. Let's just skip town, this is dicey.”_  
• _“Who cares about the villagers? I'm not confronting an army!”_  
• _“Well... it's either you or me.”_

# **(The Right) Knowledge is Power**

You treat the world as a system of joints and seams waiting to be understood (or broken). Every obstacle has leverage. Every person has a hinge. When something resists you, fascination sharpens into daring, sometimes recklessness. You read structures quickly, find cracks others miss, and trust your instincts even when they burn you. Failure hits hard, but the moment you see a new angle, the world becomes solvable again.

_People may call you shrewd, calculating, relentless, or clever._

• _“Hold still... I’m thinking..!”_  
• _“Hold on - I know exactly what you're thinking, and I've already got the solution.”_  
• _“Do you think she's got a secret? A big, life-ruining one?”_

# **Mercy is Too Risky**

Hesitation always comes around to bite you - end things, quickly and totally. Pity, negotiation, second chances - these are idealistic luxuries that don't come for free. The moment someone proves dangerous, you eliminate the risk, fast and clean, no indulgence. When others protest, you justify, lock down, or simply act before they can stop you. Trusting strangers is a knife fight - and you'd rather bring a gun than entertain them.

_People may call you brutal, efficient, decisive, or cold._

• _“Ok... I've heard enough.”_  
• _“Words can't fix people - I'd say sorry, but it doesn't matter whether you forgive me or not.”_  
• _“If we bring him into this, then we'd **have** to kill him by the end. Let's avoid that.”_
