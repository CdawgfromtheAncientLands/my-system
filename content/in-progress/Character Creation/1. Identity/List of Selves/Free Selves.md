# **I'm Me, You're You**

You can stomach rules, but not being ruled. Direction, obligation, and dependency tighten around you like a collar. You'll rip them off to stop yourself from being choked - sometimes easily, sometimes violently. You’ll help when you choose to; you’ll follow when it’s convenient... but the MOMENT someone tries to steer you instead of walk beside you, something inside you snaps. You'd rather die than be a slave.

_People may call you independent, volatile, uncompromising, or inspiring._

• _“You're great, but... I'm not looking for advice.”_  
• _“Come on. Are you gonna let him tell you what to do?”_  
• _“You do you, I'll do me.”_

# **Risk is the Reward**

You chase the pulse—novelty, adrenaline, proof you’re alive. The world feels wrong when it’s quiet or safe for too long. Risk sharpens your senses; danger feels like clarity. You push limits because excitement is worth the fallout, even when the fallout is brutal. People try to slow you down; you laugh, taunt, or slip their grip entirely. The fun _is_ the point. You’ll deal with the consequences later—maybe.

_People may call you chaotic, charismatic, foolish, or exhilarating._

• _“If it doesn’t scare you a little, what’s the point?”_  
• _“Relax—we’ll recover in the morning.”_  
• _“One more jump.”_

# **Integrity Over Authority**

Authority only deserves obedience when it earns trust - and most don’t. You’ve seen control twisted, abused, weaponized, so your instinct is to push back hard. Corruption, hypocrisy, incompetence - any of it flips the switch. You question orders, undermine bullies, and stand your ground even when it costs you allies. When someone you admire becomes part of a rotten structure, it feels like betrayal, and you fight to pull them free—or walk away if they won’t.

_People may call you principled, confrontational, reckless, or righteous._

• _“I answer to truth, not titles.”_  
• _“If they’re corrupt, I resist.”_  
• _“Stand up. Don’t let them lord over you.”_
