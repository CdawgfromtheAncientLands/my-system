When creating a [[1.1 Player Characters|PC]], a player chooses two Identities - a Self and True Self. 

# The Selves
The **Self** is the face they present to the world. A character exhibits and adheres to this during casual play for a variety of reasons - perhaps it's a curated persona, a prideful ideal, or an illusion of what they wish they were. 
This identity is what shows itself during daily interactions, handwaved downtime, and most other scenes. When a **Self** is challenged, the character's pride, reputation, or narrative posture could be at stake.

The **True Self** is the engine beneath the surface - the internalized belief, wound, instinct, or drive that governs the character when stakes rise and things actually start to matter. 

**<font color="#c00000">If the True Self would be challenged or violated, it will supersede the Self and be revealed.</font>**

This is where dramatic tension lives. The Inner Identity determines the character’s **default reaction under pressure**, the direction their personal arc naturally bends, and where their most meaningful internal conflict resides. When the True Self is challenged, the character’s self-identity is threatened and major turning points occur. 
# Tags
Selves have tags. A self can be one of the following tags:

> **[[Bold Selves|Bold]]** or **[[Tortured Selves|Tortured]]**
> **[[Dutiful Selves|Dutiful]]** or **[[Free Selves|Free]]**
> **[[Detached Selves|Detached]]** or **[[Empathetic Selves|Empathetic]]**
> **[[Idealist Selves|Idealist]]** or **[[Pragmatic Selves|Pragmatic]]**
> **[[Faithful Selves|Faithful]]** or **[[Skeptical Selves|Skeptical]]**

This set of ten is set in pairs of opposites, but that doesn't mean a character can't take opposing selves. The most interesting characters oftentimes do - and keep in mind, adventures in The Daring aren't just "days in the life" - they're stories: interruptions of the status quo.

The Self tags a character possesses which [[Identity Talents]] they can take. A character starts with 2 [[Identity Talents]] at character creation, and may gain/swap them during the campaign during intense story moments (GM's behest).
# A Special Inner Self
For certain characters, they may not decide to choose a [[1.1 Selves|True Self]]. Instead, they may select the below self and gain an additional starting [[Identity Talent]]:
### **Double Down ([[1.1 Selves|True Self]])**
**Tag:** The tag of your chosen [[1.1 Selves|Self]].
You embody one core truth so completely that there is no inner conflict - only intensification.  
_People may call you consistent, stubborn, transparent, or uncompromising._  

_“If you’re going to live a way, live it fully.”_
