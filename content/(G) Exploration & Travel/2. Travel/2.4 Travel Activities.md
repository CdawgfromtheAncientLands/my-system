## Traverse Area
**Base Cost:** 1 [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]
You move **8 miles**. This may be between hexes or within a currently occupied hex.
Designate a navigator to roll a [[Survival|Navigate]] check, DC depends on circumstances/environment.
- **Success.** You travel with direction and ease, reaching your intended destination.
- **Fail.** You must spend **1 additional [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]** to reach your destination.
- **Crit Fail.** You lose **1 additional [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]** and become **lost**. The GM determines where you actually end up (often randomly). Everyone suffers **1 [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Fatigue]]**.

A **[[Survival|Navigate]] check is only required** when the environment is unfamiliar, hazardous, or actively disorienting - **or** when the party lacks navigation tools or trained navigators. Otherwise, travel proceeds without a roll at the expected cost.

Short in-area travel that meaningfully consumes part of the day (typically an hour or greater) still consumes a full [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]], whether or not you continue moving.

## Explore Area
**Base Cost:** 1 [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]
You meander through and closely observe a hex, discovering landmarks, routes, and points of interest that would be missed by simply passing through.
Roll a [[Survival|Navigate]] check; the DC depends on the environment. Multiple creatures may assist by rolling the check - each additional success grants **+1** to the primary check.
- **Success.** You familiarize yourself with the area’s landmarks, features, and routes.
- **Fail.** You discover some or no landmarks (depending on density) and must spend **1 additional [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]** to fully explore the area.
- **Crit Fail.** You lose **1 additional [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]** aimlessly meandering and discover nothing of note, though the GM may introduce incidental details at their discretion.

Exploring an area is slower but yields broader, more reliable information than traversal alone.

## Search Area
**Cost:** 1 [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]
You deliberately search a hex or specific location for hidden or obscured features—such as a safe clearing, concealed path, hidden cache, animal den, or covered shelter.
Make an [[Observation|Perception]] check (or another appropriate check, as determined by the GM).
- **Success.** You find hidden features that meet or beat the DC.
- **Fail.** You find nothing of note.
- **Crit Fail.** You lose **1 additional [[2.2 Long-Term Travel|Travel Action]]** due to wasted effort or misdirection.

Searching is focused and deliberate; it does not provide broad familiarity with an area unless combined with exploration.
