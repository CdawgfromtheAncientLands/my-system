In Exploration, actions unfold in broad, intentional beats rather than strict turns. Choices still matter, resources still drain, and consequences still accumulate - but tension builds slowly, through uncertainty, fatigue, missed details, and environmental pressure rather than blades and bullets of [[1.1 Encounters|Encounter]]s.

Things commonly take multiples of 2, 5, or 10 minutes.

Common activities during this time include rest, investigation, or medical recovery.
