# Treat Wounds — 10 Minutes
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]
**Requirements:** Access to a [[First Aid Kit]] or equivalent supplies. The patient is not [[3. Breaking & Dying|Dying]].
**Effect:** You stabilize injuries and calm the body of a patient. Attempt a DC 16 [[Medicine|First Aid]] check. 

- **Crit Success.** Same as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]], but restore double the [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
- **Success.** Reduce your patient's [[3. Breaking & Dying|Peril]] condition from [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury]] or [[3. Breaking & Dying|Dying]] to 0. Restore 2d6 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to your patient.
- **Crit Fail.** Your patient suffers a Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2).

**Limits & Notes:**  
Creatures restoring [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] from this [[1.4 Medical Activities|Treat Wounds]] are immune to the restorative effects until they suffer an [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury]] or have become [[3. Breaking & Dying|Dying]] again. Treat Wounds does **not** remove Injuries or prevent their penalties.

# Resuscitate — 1 Minute
[[2. Action & Effect Traits|{Precise}]]
**Requirements:** Access to a [[First Aid Kit]] or equivalent supplies. The patient is not [[3. Breaking & Dying|Dying]].
**Effect:** You try to motivate a patient back to consciousness. Attempt a DC 16 [[Medicine|First Aid]] check.

- **Crit Success.** Same as [[1.1 Skill Checks|Success]], but restore an additional 2d4 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]] to the patient.
- **Success.** If your patient is currently unconscious due to [[3. Breaking & Dying|Injury]], they are now conscious with 1 [[1.4 Secondary Attribute|Vigor]].
- **Crit Fail.** Your patient suffers a Mild [[2.2 Stress|Stressor]] (1d4+2). If they would [[3. Breaking & Dying|Breakdown]] from this, they do so and fall unconscious for another 1d4 hours directly after.
